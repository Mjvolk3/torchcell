\noindent
The served graph, KG 4.0 (release 2026.10.17-4295f7ca, paired with torchcell v1.8.3),
holds 70 schematized and L0--L4-verified \org{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} datasets. Nineteen
of them, from sixteen sources, were added since the previous ranking
(\emph{The Final Fifty for Datasets 51--100}), and thirteen of those were rows of that
ranking. Those rows have left the list, and the 154 candidates that remain are ranked here
for the next 200 additions, from 70 served to 270. The pool is 46 rows short of that
number; the shortfall is open discovery, named as such, not a reserve.

The ordering changes in one way. The first band is now \term{state across genomes}: a
transcriptome or a proteome measured on strains whose genomes differ by sequence, which is
where the served graph is thinnest relative to what a model of cell state needs
(Sec.~\ref{sec:state}). Eight rows qualify and fill ranks 1 to 8. Behind them the rule of
the previous pass holds: the rows a yeast Perturb-seq is designed against, then the rows
that put two molecular layers on one joinable axis, then scale; inside a band, tier, then
measurements (Sec.~\ref{sec:rule}).

Table~\ref{tab:final} is the recommended first fifty in rank order and ten extra, so a row
that proves unreachable has a named replacement. Table~\ref{tab:sources} gives each row's
citation, link, data location and the reason for its position; Table~\ref{tab:synergies}
names the joins each row opens against a served dataset or another row;
Table~\ref{tab:excluded} records what was considered and dropped; Sec.~\ref{sec:summary}
closes with the summary statistics, and Sec.~\ref{sec:full} prints the whole pool with a
source for every row.
