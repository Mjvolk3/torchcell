\tcfigfit[1.0]{figures/module_lp.pdf}{\textbf{The layer that solves, rounds 15 to 15d.}
  The abundance head sets each gene's enzyme abundance with availability a hard fact,
  the unit abundance and the $k_{cat}$ ceilings form the box, and the flux is the growth
  optimum of the linear program over that box, one HiGHS solve per genotype; the
  program's value $z^*$ enters the readout. Mass balance and the box hold exactly at the
  optimum. The dashed path is the backward pass: the derivative of $z^*$ in a bound is
  that bound's dual, nonzero only where the bound binds, so the backbone is trained
  through the ceilings and only through them; an infeasible program returns no flux and
  no gradient. Colors as in Figure~\ref{fig:readout-key}. Drawn in draw.io from
  \texttt{notes/assets/drawio/028-module.gen.py}.}{fig:module-lp}
