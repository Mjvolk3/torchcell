\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The metabolic module built for gene essentiality (\texttt{notes-tex/028-gene-essentiality})
carried to the target it was designed for: Cachera's betaxanthin screen, 4,930 deletion
strains of BY4741 with the pigment cassette on YPD. The module is an offline store, one
flux vector per deletion from a parsimonious solve on the cassette strain, read through
a zero-initialized affine on the transformer's logit, and every arm is paired by seed
against the same backbone without it under the 027 protocol on Merzbacher's pinned test
genes. The whole-vector store lifts the width-12 backbone's pinned Spearman from 0.079 to
0.121 and, on the strain as built (the deletion-only graph), lifts the top-50 call too;
the width-90 backbone without the store reads the same, and the two do not add. The
gain lives in the production half of the vector and in a free affine, arrives after the
backbone escapes the constant predictor, and is kept by a release rule on the validation
curve. Without any backbone the clean parsimonious vector read by nearest neighbors
carries 0.195, more rank than any single transformer arm, and the max-min driving-force
concentrations carry 0.152 linearly. The second law as fixed reaction directions leaves
the strain with no growth; as a linear program in concentrations it is consistent to
within a kilojoule. Every number is at a validation-selected epoch on the pinned test
set with the across-seed spread, every table is generated from the result files, and
the amino-acid half of the campaign is the sibling document
\texttt{notes-tex/032-amino-acid-module}.
\end{abstract}
