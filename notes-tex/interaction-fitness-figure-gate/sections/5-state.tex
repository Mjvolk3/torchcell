\section{The decision rules, fixed now\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:state}

\pfstep{What a panel needs to enter the paper} Every drawn value in a committed result
file (\fstat{file}); every transformer bar a test score of a checkpoint selected on
validation, over at least three seeds, with the across-seed spread drawn; the regime of
every bar named in the panel or its key; the screen-offset floor drawn wherever a pooled
Pearson is drawn; and the claim the panel carries written in one sentence in this
document before the panel is composed.

\pfstep{The comparison that decides the figure's third claim} The transformer reading the
lower-order terms as inputs (E1) against the boosted trees (L2), both on the same pinned
test triples, paired by triple over 2,000 bootstrap resamples, three seeds of the
transformer. The transformer helps when its seed-mean test Pearson exceeds L2's by at
least 0.02 on an arm with the paired interval excluding zero on every seed, and the claim
is made per arm. If it does not clear that on arm R, the figure states the lower-order
ladder as the measured ceiling of the closure and the transformer as a model of gene
identity that reaches part of it, and the manuscript claims no more. If it clears it on R
and not on Q, the claim is limited to seen query pairs.

\pfstep{The regime question, settled by naming it} A lookup over measured terms of the
same strains is transductive over the closure; a model reading identity is not. The
sheets carry the regime in the key and the performance table in a column. A reviewer who
objects that the lookup is not a model is answered by E1, which gives the transformer the
same inputs; a reviewer who objects that the transformer was handed the answer through
the fitness head is answered by Figure~\ref{fig:sheet}, which shows it did not take it.

\pfstep{Order of work} E3 first, since it is CPU scoring of checkpoints that exist and
turns every validation bar into a test bar. E1 and E4 next, one chain on IGB mmli, the
same launcher as the running arms. E5 is CPU and runs beside them. E2, E6 and E7 follow
once E1 has read. Nothing is submitted until the author says so.
