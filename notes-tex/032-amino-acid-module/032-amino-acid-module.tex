\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The metabolic module built for gene essentiality (\texttt{notes-tex/028-gene-essentiality})
carried to M\"ulleder's 19 intracellular amino acids on the prototrophic deletion
collection on minimal medium. The module is an offline store, one flux vector per
deletion from a parsimonious solve on the prototroph with 19 synthesis maxima beside it,
read through a zero-initialized affine on a 19-output head, and every arm is paired by
seed against the same backbone without it under the 027 protocol on Merzbacher's pinned
test genes. The endpoint sits at its noise floor: the screen's own strain-level retest
rank is 0.20, the second genome-wide screen of the same phenotype (Cooper 2010) agrees
with it at 0.03 over 4,051 shared strains and reproduces its own value for a typical
gene at 0.05, and every model, transformer or ladder, at width 12 or 90, with or without
a store, reads between 0.01 and 0.06. Four forms of the store affine sit at or under
their control, the cached solve head recovers the affine's loss and lands at the
control, and width, 100 more epochs and the rule counts change nothing. What the
campaign fixes is the target's position rather than the module's. Every number is at a
validation-selected epoch on the pinned test set with the across-seed spread, every
table is generated from the result files, and the betaxanthin half of the campaign is
the sibling document \texttt{notes-tex/031-betaxanthin-module}.
\end{abstract}
