## 2026.09.27 - Closed-form values for every loss in multi_dim_nan_tolerant

`tests/torchcell/losses/test_multi_dim_nan_tolerant.py` asserts shapes and finiteness; this file asserts values. Shared regression fixture: predictions [[1, 2], [3, 4], [5, 6]] against targets [[0, nan], [1, 5], [nan, 3]], so each dimension keeps two rows and the per-dimension denominators are the valid counts: L1 [1.5, 2.0], Huber(1) [1.0, 1.5], Huber(2) [1.25, 2.25], log-cosh [0.8793918, 1.3715547], MSE [2.5, 5.0]. Shared SupCR fixture: three unit embeddings with labels (0, 1, 3) at temperature 1, worked anchor by anchor to (2 log(1 + e^c) + log 2) / 6 = 0.4848379660 with c = 1/sqrt(2); random inputs are checked against `_naive_supcr`, an O(N^3) transcription of the per-anchor formula with no sort or cumulative sum. Also the quantile pinball loss at 0.1, 0.5 and 0.9, `isotonic_l2_pav` (a violating pair pools to its mean, a sorted input is the identity), `FastSoftSort` forward and backward against a central difference, `WeightedDistLoss` and its three helpers, `CombinedRegressionLoss`, the two cross-entropy losses, `MonotonicParameter`, the ordinal losses and `CategoricalEntropyRegLoss`. Findings, each pinned by a test whose docstring starts "Finding:": `CombinedRegressionLoss.forward` multiplies a [D, 1] loss column by a [D] weight vector, which broadcasts to [D, D], so the total is the unweighted sum of the per-dimension losses for every loss type (weights [1, 3] on [2.5, 5.0] give 7.5, not 4.375; multi_dim_nan_tolerant.py lines 1113 to 1116); `FastSoftSort.backward` returns the negated Jacobian-vector product (line 776), so `torch.autograd.gradcheck` fails on it; `WeightedDistLoss.forward` soft-sorts predictions descending against ascending theoretical labels, so identical predictions and targets (0, 0, 2, 2) score 3.25 instead of 0.25 (lines 727 and 1006 to 1011), and its default weight is repeated unnormalized so two dimensions sum where explicit weights average (line 965); `_get_batch_label_distribution`'s residual branch is unreachable for a normalized density and moves a deficit the wrong way when reached (lines 906 to 916); `SupCR` with tied labels includes the anchor's own term in the suffix sum (lines 75 to 78); `CombinedCELoss` and `CombinedOrdinalCELoss` return the weighted, not the raw, per-task losses as their second value; `MseCategoricalEntropyRegLoss` weights the MSE total by the valid counts (lines 1617 to 1622).
