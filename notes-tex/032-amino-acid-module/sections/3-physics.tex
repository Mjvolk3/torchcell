\section{Physically constrained stores on the prototroph, and the store read without the backbone\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:physics}

The module so far carries mass balance and nothing else: one parsimonious vector per
deletion. On the essentiality endpoint the store ladder (the essentiality document's
round 113) built the same vector under enzyme ceilings, sampled turnover, GECKO pools
and a minimization of metabolic adjustment, and none moved the score beyond the seed
spread. None of those programs had been run on the prototroph on minimal medium, which
covers every GEM gene rather than the labeled ones. On 2026-09-23 they were, on CPU
beside the packs, with reads that need no card.

The second law as fixed directions admits no growth. The direction rule of the
essentiality study's round 114 (a reaction whose standard energy on the union-external
route is at least $T$ below zero loses its reverse part, at least $T$ above it its
forward part; unpriced reactions keep the GEM box) leaves the prototroph infeasible at
both thresholds, as it had on the essentiality model, with 84 fixed directions running
against the parsimonious wild-type flux at 10 kJ/mol and 44 at 30 kJ/mol. No store is
written for an infeasible parent, and the outcome is recorded
(\texttt{results/target\_physics\_store\_amino\_acids\_sign10.json}, \texttt{\_sign30}).
Standard energies at 1 M cannot be imposed as directions on this model; a
thermodynamically consistent program needs the concentrations as variables, which is
what the driving-force store below does with a linear program.

The stores that do exist. The linear MOMA store on the prototroph (1,161 genes) has 175
lethal deletions against the parsimonious store's 161, moves the growth ratio by more
than 0.01 on 63 genes, and moves the $\log(1+|v|)$ vector on 567 genes with a median
relative distance of 0.0 to the parsimonious deletion vector. Among the 918 deletions
that leave growth unchanged, the HiGHS parsimonious store's vector differs from its own
wild type's for 324 (Table~\ref{tab:store-degeneracy}, from
\texttt{store\_degeneracy.py}) and the MOMA store's, anchored to one wild-type vector,
for 11: the parsimonious optimum has alternative vertices for a third of the no-effect
deletions on this model too, fewer than on the cassette strain (the sibling document).
The max-min driving-force store (\texttt{target\_driving\_force\_store.py}) solves, per
deletion, for the metabolite concentrations between 1 $\mu$M and 10 mM that maximize the
smallest driving force over the reactions the parsimonious flux carries, with transport
and proton-translocating reactions unconstrained and, in the capped variant, reactions
priced above 100 kJ/mol set aside; the sampled store
(\texttt{target\_sampled\_flux\_store.py}) draws six OptGP samples of each deletion's
cone at 99 percent of its optimum and keeps the mean over draws and the per-reaction
spread.

The store read without the backbone (Table~\ref{tab:store-baselines};
\texttt{target\_store\_baselines.py}, the 027 ladder's split, standardization and
selection, with a random forest added, the Flux Cone Learning form on one point per
deletion; nearest neighbors average every training row tied at the $k$-th similarity,
predictions within $10^{-9}$ count as one rank, and the validation criterion is rounded
to $10^{-6}$, so two runs agree to every digit). The screen is genome-wide and the
stores cover the GEM genes, so most training genotypes read the wild-type row and a
gene's row is one vector with no graph, no sequence and no pathway. On the amino acids
every representation under every model sits between $-0.004$ and 0.058 (the HiGHS
store 0.022 by kNN, ProtT5 0.052 by forest, the two concatenated 0.058), where the 027
bilinear ridge read 0.045 and the transformer arms 0.049 to 0.062. The sampled cone
(1,161 deletions, 161 lethal, six OptGP draws each at 99 percent of optimum, 4.3 h on
twelve processes after the model copy's simplex cycling was removed with presolve and
the warm-up's warm starts kept) reads $-0.004$, 0.031 and 0.023 by ridge, kNN and
forest, its mean a median 5.9 relative distance from the parsimonious point; round 143
(w160) is its transformer read. The driving-force
polytope sampled per deletion (\texttt{target\_concentration\_polytope\_store.py}, the
method in the sibling document: the region below the max-min optimum by 1 kJ/mol,
coordinate hit-and-run, six draws, the chain seeded from a hash of the polytope) has on
the prototroph a wild-type region of 249 constrained reactions with 59 binding over 319
of the 2,778 variable metabolites, a median spread of $\ln c$ across draws of 2.08 over
them against 2.62 for an unconstrained one, and 405 viable rows whose mean differs from
the wild type's; its mean and spread stores read 0.016, 0.011 and 0.008 and 0.018,
0.009 and 0.001 by ridge, kNN and forest, the floor of the ladder. Round 148 (w165) is
the transformer read of the polytope mean.

The annotation-only family, on the same split (Table~\ref{tab:go-models};
\texttt{target\_go\_models.py}, the method in the sibling document): DCell's hierarchy
with a 19-output head on the root's state, and a perceptron with one hidden layer of
256 over the propagated GO multi-hot, over the HiGHS store row, over ProtT5 and over
their concatenations, six seeds at 200 epochs, the epoch selected on validation
per-feature Pearson. The GO perceptron reads 0.054 (sd 0.002 over seeds), ProtT5 0.054
(sd 0.008), and the two together 0.065 (sd 0.003), ensembled 0.069, the highest
single-seed read of anything on this target (the transformer arms 0.033 to 0.053 at
width 12, the 027 arms 0.049 to 0.062 at width 90); DCell's hierarchy reads 0.035 (sd
0.015). The store row alone reads 0.019, and appending it lowers every read it joins,
GO from 0.054 to 0.042 and GO with ProtT5 from 0.065 to 0.045, the same sign the
transformer's affine gave the parsimonious store (round 126). So on the amino acids the
flux vector costs a perceptron what it costs the transformer, and annotation with
sequence sets the level, 0.065 against a strain-level retest of 0.20
(Section~\ref{sec:concordance}).
