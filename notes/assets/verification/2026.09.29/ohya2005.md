## Claims

### O1. Growth conditions: the paper says YPD, logarithmic phase, and does not state a temperature; torchcell records YPD / liquid / 25 C with 25 C inferred from the Ohya-lab standard (Suzuki 2018, Ohnuki 2018/2022)
- Previously stated by: loader docstring lines 37-44 and comment lines 314-318; note section "2026.07.15 - Rebuild-provisioning audit" ("Environment corrected"); `2005a/manifest.json` `note`; memory bullet "Environment".
- Verdict: PARTLY CONFIRMED (YPD / liquid / "no temperature in the paper" confirmed; the 25 C deferral chain as written is wrong about Suzuki 2018, and a stronger same-lab source exists that nobody cited).
- Evidence:
  - Paper Methods, `paper.md` line 23 (identical in PMC1316885): "Each strain was grown in yeast extract/ peptone/dextrose medium, and logarithmic-phase cells were fixed." No temperature anywhere in the main text: grep of `paper.md` for `temperat|°C|25 C|30 C|degree` hits only line 77 ("results in a temperature-sensitive phenotype (30)", about the fks1-1144 allele).
  - Every temperature hit in the SI: `si/si12.md` line 49, in the genetic-interaction follow-up, not the screen: "After tetrad dissection, plates spotted with segregants were incubated for 3-5 days at $2 5 \mathrm { { ^ \circ C } }$." `si/si12.md` line 59: "cells were cultured in yeast extract/peptone/dextrose (YPD) and the cell wall was observed with transmission electron microscopy (TEM)." `si/si5.md` line 9: "This mutant was inviable in YPD containg 0.15 M HU". No temperature in si1-si11, si13, si14.
  - SCMD2 portal pages (home, `datasheet.php`) and the CalMorph site pages state no growth condition at all (fetched 2026-09-29; full text saved in the tool log).
  - Suzuki 2018 (Europe PMC full text) does NOT say the 4718-mutant dataset was "grown at 25 C". Its 25 C sentences are all about the 19-mutant re-validation set: "grown at 25 °C for 1 day", "cultured at 25 °C for 6 days", "Cell stocks were struck out on YPD plates and grown at 25 °C for a maximum of ~ 48 h (~ 20 generations)." For the original dataset it says only: "Morphological data of 4718 non-essential gene deletion mutants and 109 wild-type replicates were obtained by subjecting microscopic images of yeast cells to the image processing program CalMorph (ver. 1.2) as previously described [21, 48]." So the docstring's "corroborated by Suzuki 2018 (which reuses this data, "grown at 25 C")" attributes a quote to the wrong experiment.
  - The strongest same-lab source, not cited anywhere in torchcell: the CalMorph User Manual distributed by the Ohya lab (`CalMorph-manual.pdf`, section 6.1 "Cell culture and fixation"): "Liquid culture medium (ex. YPD)" ... "3. Culture the cells at 25°C on a rotator at 25-30 r/min." ... "2. Transfer part of the culture to the flask, and agitate it reciprocally at 25°C in the water bath overnight." ... "3. Transfer the log-phase culture (4×106 ~ 1×107 cells/ml) to a 50-ml disposable centrifuge tube." This is the lab's standard protocol for the exact pipeline, and it confirms liquid culture (test tube on a rotator, then a flask in a water bath), but it is still not a statement about the 2005 screen itself.
  - Dev LMDB: all 4718 records carry `media {'name': 'YPD', 'state': 'liquid', 'is_synthetic': False}` and `temperature {'value': 25.0, 'unit': Celsius}` (`ohya2005_check.out`).
- Consequence for the loader / served records: YPD/liquid stand. 25 C remains an inference; the docstring (lines 41-44) and the manifest note should cite the CalMorph User Manual section 6.1 and Ohnuki 2018/2022 as the deferral, and drop the Suzuki 2018 "grown at 25 C" attribution, which is about a different experiment. The FLAG "pin the verbatim Ohya-2005 temperature once that paper is mirrored" can be closed as "the paper is mirrored and contains no temperature."

- Evidence:
  - Loader `_calculate_wt_reference`: per-column `pd.to_numeric(..., errors="coerce").mean()` over all 122 rows; no SD is stored. SI Table 9 (`si/si2.md`): "Wild type ... Total ... 126". Supporting Text (`si/si12.md`): "The wild-type data are divided by the mean and transformed by the function defined below (1) ... Abnormality is then "p := min(N(F(x)), 1 - N(F(x)))" against the fitted normal of the transformed wild-type data. - Consequence for the loader / served records: the mean-WT reference is a torchcell design choice and is computed correctly; it is not the paper's normalization. The manifest's description "(Suzuki 2018 reports 109 WT replicates for the same Ohya-2005 dataset; the SCMD portal file distributes 122.)" should add the paper's own 126.

1. **The distributed matrices are a Suzuki-2018 re-analysis of the Ohya-2005 images, not the 2005 numbers.** The 2026.07.15 note "correction" (and the docstring's "DATA PROVENANCE" block, and the manifest note) assert the TSVs are "Ohya 2005's OWN published data" and that Suzuki 2018 "merely REUSED this same dataset". The distributor says otherwise. SCMD2 `datasheet.php` (fetched 2026-09-29): "The data sheets here have been published by Suzuki et al. (2018, BMC Genomics) by reanalysing the images first published in Ohya et al. (2005, PNAS) after a quality control. Cell images of the mutants are available at SSBD:ssbd-repos-000349." CalMorph `datasheet.html`: "The data sheets here are the results created by reanalyzing the images published in Ohya et al. (2005, PNAS) after a quality control." Suzuki 2018 Methods: "Morphological data of 4718 non-essential gene deletion mutants and 109 wild-type replicates were obtained by subjecting microscopic images of yeast cells to the image processing program CalMorph (ver. 1.2) as previously described [21, 48]." CalMorph `software.html` lists "Version 1.1 ... A standard version of CalMorph which outputs the morphological data with 501 parameters" (the 2005-era program) and "Version 1.2 ... designed to output the morphological data of the single cells", so ver. 1.2 postdates the paper. Two consequences: (a) the pinned values are the product of a 2018 re-run of CalMorph 1.2 on the 2005 images, so `publication` = Ohya 2005 is right for the images and the experiment, but the numeric matrix should be described as "CalMorph 1.2 re-analysis distributed with Suzuki 2018", and Suzuki 2018 belongs in the record's provenance (the manifest's `reused_by_doi` is the wrong relation, it should be something like `values_generated_by`); (b) the paper's own numbers (from which 254/501 and 2,378 mutants were computed) are NOT what torchcell serves, so any comparison to the paper's mutant calls is a comparison across two CalMorph runs. Hypothesis (untested): the earlier note's reversal was based on the wording of Suzuki 2018's reference [21] alone, without reading the SCMD datasheet page.

2. **SSBD holds byte-identical copies of both TSVs, with a DOI, and that is the durable retrieval source.** `https://ssbd.riken.jp/data/ssbd-000349/sha256sum.txt` lists `c4ba1e84b4ea6273f0162ef9230e15634933c8c0c4910dd7546a21c6293e0fc0  repos/analysis/mt4718data.tsv` and `ab2c31b5150b2a33c15b5d22f1bef8687719975223559a740ea233c1f67b27c3  repos/analysis/wt122data.tsv`, exactly the loader pins. The entry (`https://ssbd.riken.jp/repository/349/`, DOI 10.24631/ssbd.repos.2024.05.349, released 2024-07-12, CC BY 4.0, 45.3 GB, "Organism Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Strain -") also carries `mt4718nmrt.tsv`, `mt4718dmnt.tsv`, `wt122nmrt.tsv`, `wt122dmnt.tsv`, `Ohya2005P.xlsx`, per-strain `singleCell/<orf>/*.xls`, per-replicate `wtSingleCell/<name>/*.xls`, and the raw jpg images (`dataset.json`: 614,274 files under `repos/`, three zips `analysis.zip`, `imgdata.zip`, `morphdata.zip`). 3. **Three wild-type counts, none reconciled in torchcell docs.** Paper Fig. 1B / Table 9: 126 wild-type samples. Distributed average file: 122 rows (CalMorph datasheet: "Single cell data, 126 replicated wild-type" vs "122 replicated wild-type Average data", so 4 of the 126 specimens were dropped from the average sheet, reason unstated). Suzuki 2018: "Selected replicated data (n = 109)". The manifest mentions 109 vs 122 only.

4. **The wild-type replicates carry batch labels that torchcell discards.** `NAME` prefixes `04his3` (95 rows, some without a hyphen: `04his341`, `04his383`...), `his3new` (15, including sub-replicates `11a..11e`), `his3cnt` (6), `his3old` (6, one bare `his3old`). 5. **The `nmrt` / `dmnt` companion files are not mirrored.** SCMD distributes "Number of cells for ratio parameter" (`mt4718nmrt.tsv`, 885.6 KB) and "Number of cells in specimen for ratio parameter" (`mt4718dmnt.tsv`, 1.14 MB) beside the averages; the 40 Total_stage ratio parameters and the 21 per-stage ratios are proportions whose denominators live there. ## What the public showcase page can state safely

- Source: Ohya et al. 2005, PNAS 102:19015-19020, "High-dimensional and large-scale phenotyping of yeast mutants" (PMID 16365294, DOI 10.1073/pnas.0509436102). The screened set: "A set of haploid S. cerevisiae MATa deletion strains (4,786 strains) was obtained from ... (EUROSCARF)"; "Deletion strains of 4,718 ORFs were used." (paper Methods).
- Medium and growth state: "Each strain was grown in yeast extract/peptone/dextrose medium, and logarithmic-phase cells were fixed." (paper Methods). The paper states no temperature; torchcell records 25 C from the Ohya-lab CalMorph protocol ("Culture the cells at 25°C", CalMorph User Manual section 6.1) and the same-lab Ohnuki 2018/2022 Methods. Say "25 C (lab protocol, not stated in the 2005 paper)".
- Phenotype: 501 CalMorph parameters per strain, "information about cell shape (visualized by cell wall staining), the actin cytoskeleton, and nuclear morphology of cells at a specific stage of the cell cycle" (paper); served as 281 base parameters + 220 coefficient-of-variation parameters; ids are the CalMorph ids unchanged (SI Table 1; CalMorph manual), units pixel / pixel^2 / unitless ratios.
- Values: per-strain population averages, one replicate per deletion strain ("we have only one set of data for each deletion strain", paper); wild-type reference = per-parameter mean over the 122 distributed his3 wild-type replicate averages (torchcell aggregation; the paper imaged 126 and used a Box-Cox-transformed distribution instead).
- Data files: `mt4718data.tsv` (4,718 x 501) and `wt122data.tsv` (122 x 501), sha256 `c4ba1e84...0fc0` and `ab2c31b5...27c3`, retrievable from the SCMD/CalMorph portal and from SSBD ssbd-repos-000349 (DOI 10.24631/ssbd.repos.2024.05.349, CC BY 4.0) where the published `sha256sum.txt` lists the same digests.
- Provenance caveat to state: the distributed matrices are the CalMorph 1.2 re-analysis "after a quality control" published with Suzuki et al. 2018 (BMC Genomics 19:149), computed on the 2005 images; they are not the numbers the 2005 paper's statistics were run on.
- Counts: 4,718 records, 0 dropped, 17 ORF names remapped to current SGD identifiers, 0 missing values in the source file.
