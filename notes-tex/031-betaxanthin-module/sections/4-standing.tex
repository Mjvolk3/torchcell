\section{Where the module stands on this target\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:standing}

Six things are measured. The whole-vector store affine lifts the width-12 backbone's
rank to where the width-90 backbone sits and, on the strain as built, lifts its call
too; the two do not add, at either width. The gain lives in the production half of the
vector and in a free affine that trains at full rate; it does not come from the first
epochs, and a release rule on the validation curve keeps it where a fixed late start
loses it, while a nonlinear head over the store loses it and brings the plateau back.
The physically stated stores read in the transformer sit at the parsimonious store's
level: the adjustment store within the seed spread with half its spread and the best
single-seed call, the clean parsimonious vector a little above it on five seeds of six
with the best width-12 ensemble, and the driving-force concentrations at the same rank
with twice the spread; the second law adds a second representation at the flux store's
level, not a higher one. Without any backbone the clean vector read by nearest
neighbors carries more rank than any single transformer arm, and the sampled
driving-force polytope is the one flux-side representation every model reads above
0.12; in the transformer the polytope's mean reads at the flux store's level on every
seed above the control, with the best width-12 ensemble rank, and the sampled cone is
the weakest flux representation. The release rule that lifted the degenerate GLPK
store adds nothing to the adjustment store and takes from the HiGHS store (rounds 149,
152); a store without the vertex noise is read best from the first epoch. The
annotation family sets the level: a perceptron over the propagated GO multi-hot reads
0.200 in three seconds a seed, above every transformer arm, and the same columns in the
affine slot read 0.202 with the seeds identical (round 150), the backbone adding
nothing; the HiGHS vector appended to GO in that affine reads 0.211, above GO alone on
five seeds of six, the highest rank of the study (round 151). Dropout 0.3 on the store
arm reads 0.145, the best single-seed rank and call of any store arm, where width 6 to
90 left the level flat (144, 145). Every arm overfits by epoch 60 and averaging seeds
is worth more than any representation on the call. On the same split the comparators
sit below the best arm, the closest by 0.008: a random forest over GO with the HiGHS
vector, the best arm's own input, 0.204 with a top-50 of 4.79, cosine nearest neighbors
over the HiGHS store 0.195, the ridge 0.152, nearest neighbors over ProtT5 0.145, the
Flux Cone Learning random forest 0.039 (Tables~\ref{tab:store-baselines} and
\ref{tab:target-transfer-comparators}); the best call of any genotype-side read is the
forest over GO alone, 5.39. The strain's own measured amino-acid pool, nineteen
columns, reads 0.285 by nearest neighbors and 0.280 by forest on the same split, above
every genotype-side read and every transformer arm, and appended to GO with the HiGHS
vector gives the forest the best call of any read, 6.19 (Section~\ref{sec:physics});
the transformer reading the same columns is queued (175, 176). The
amino-acid endpoint shares strain-level signal with this one: a deletion's measured
amino-acid pool, M\"ulleder's nineteen concentrations through a ridge, reads its
betaxanthin level out of fold at 0.226 over 4,477 strains, and appended to the gene's
GO multi-hot in the same ridge adds 0.072 to what the annotation reads alone (0.165 to
0.237), the sibling document's Section~4 (\texttt{notes-tex/032-amino-acid-module}). The three levers on one cell,
the HiGHS store under the rule with dropout 0.3, read 0.125, under the GLPK store at
dropout 0.3 alone (round 154): the release is the lever that costs on the clean store.
The dropout curve turns between 0.3 and 0.5: at 0.5 three seeds of six never leave
the plateau and the arm falls back to the incumbent's level, 0.125 against 0.121
(round 155), so 0.3 is a maximum, not a direction. The polytope's spread beside its
mean adds nothing on single seeds and gives the highest six-seed rank of any flux-side
width-12 arm, 0.155 (round 156): the region's value is in the ensemble. The GO affine
under the release rule reads 0.199, the backbone escaping first adding nothing to the
annotation's single read; the rule buys seed diversity, worth 0.003 on the ensemble
(0.206, round 158); dropout 0.3 on the same arm moves it by less than a thousandth
(round 159), so the annotation arm is the affine alone and no setting of the
transformer around it has moved it; the same holds for the flux-and-annotation affine,
which dropout 0.3 leaves at 0.212 with the flux gain beside GO reproduced at 0.010
(161). The polytope's mean appended to that affine takes 0.013 from it on five seeds
of six and the stack reads under GO alone (163): the metabolite side does not add to
the annotation the way the flux vector does. On twelve seeds the best arm holds at
0.214 with a standard error of 0.002, 0.011 above the forest over its own columns and
0.019 above the nearest-neighbor read of the HiGHS store, with a twelve-seed ensemble
of 0.216 (round 164). Adding the region's width to that stack costs again, and the
cost grows with the columns added (0.193, round 166): the metabolite side does not
enter this affine's read at any width. The adjustment store in place of the
parsimonious one adds 0.006 to the annotation on every seed against the parsimonious
vector's 0.009 (round 167): both flux programs enter the annotation's read where the
metabolite programs do not. Weight decay on the affine's own weights runs 0.211, 0.210,
0.204 from none to 0.1 to 1.0 (rounds 169, 170), the heavy setting selecting fifty
epochs later with twice the seed spread: no setting of the transformer and no
regularization of the affine has moved the annotation arms. The two flux programs
together beside GO read 0.216, above the parsimonious vector with GO on five seeds of
six at a paired spread of 0.003 (round 171), the highest single-seed rank of the study
and the second time the flux side has entered the annotation's read; the call does not
follow it. On twelve seeds the annotation-only arm reads 0.203 with a standard error of
0.0005, the study's most reproducible cell, and the flux vector's gain beside it is
$+0.012$ on eleven of twelve (round 172). Against the affine-free control on the same
twelve seeds the annotation arm is $+0.127$ on twelve of twelve, and its ensemble,
0.203, stays twice the control's twelve-seed ensemble of 0.102 (round 173): unlike the
amino-acid target, where averaging the control's seed noise passed the annotation's
small gain, here the gain is five times what the averaging recovers. The measured pool in
the affine adds 0.006 to GO on every seed and nothing beside the HiGHS vector (rounds
175, 176), a linear read of columns whose signal the ladder's nearest neighbors carry at
0.285: the slot bounds the read, not the data. On twelve seeds the two flux programs
beside GO read 0.217 against the parsimonious vector's 0.214, $+0.003$ within a paired
spread of 0.007 (round 177), so round 171's gain does not hold at its first size and
the two arms share one level; the three families in one affine read 0.218 (round 178),
the pool adding nothing beside the flux programs. Every linear combination of the
annotation with the flux and measured sides reads 0.208 to 0.218: the affine's ceiling
on this input family. The pool alone in the affine reads 0.195 with the widest seed
spread and the lowest call of any store arm (round 179), above the ridge on the same
columns (0.149) and 0.09 under their nearest-neighbor read: the ceiling is the slot's.
The same columns through a hidden layer of 64 read 0.240, $+0.045$ over the linear
slot on five of six and the highest single-seed mean of any transformer arm, with a
six-seed ensemble of 0.292 that passes the ladder's nearest neighbors (round 180): the
pool's nonlinear signal does reach the transformer once the slot is nonlinear, and one
seed selects at the budget's end, so 0.240 is a lower bound. The minimum-norm flux
store, the unique point with no vertex noise, reads under the sparse HiGHS vertex both
alone (0.093 against 0.130, round 181) and beside GO (0.204 against 0.211 on six of six,
round 182): the flux side's gain beside the annotation lives in the sparse solution.
Cells queued: GO with the pool through the hidden layer (183), six more seeds of round
180 (184), the pool through a layer of 256 (185), the three families through 64 (186).
