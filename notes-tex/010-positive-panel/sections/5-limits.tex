\section{Established, and not\secstatus{todo}}

\subsection{Established\secstatus{todo}}

The model retrieves published positive trigenic interactions well above base rate
on the held-out split, and its margin over a model that cannot represent
interaction is proportionally wider there than on negatives: average precision
$0.0352$ against $0.0110$ at the Kuzmin 2020 call, and $0.111$ against $0.0109$
at $\tau > +0.20$. This is a statement about ordering and it survives everything
below.

Closing $\tau$ within 20 constructed strains admits at most 6 interactions, on 5
genes with 9 of the 10 doubles built, and the bound is proved rather than
searched for.

Greedy stacking cannot reach most of the wins. In the Kuzmin corpus 32{,}195 of
the 44{,}462 triples that beat wild type sit behind a step that lowers fitness,
and in the free-fatty-acid panel 2 of 720 measured deletion orders never drop
below the chassis. Both come from measurements and neither depends on the
transformer.

The model fails the rungs below the order it was trained on. Five singles average
a predicted interaction of $+0.0714$ where the truth is zero by definition, and
all nine doubles are predicted above their measured $\varepsilon$, a sign test at
$p = 0.004$.

\subsection{Withdrawn\secstatus{todo}}

Two claims made earlier in this document do not survive it, and they are listed
here because the sections that make them are left standing as the record of how
the design moved.

\textbf{The 10-gene two-block panel of Sections~\ref{sec:design}
and~\ref{sec:panel10}.} Its support gate
counted training records rather than distinct screens. \texttt{YER059W}'s 1{,}097
records come from one query double, and re-running the selection with a 50-screen
floor drops the best available triple from $+0.662$ to $+0.069$, a factor of
$9.6$. Section~\ref{sec:panel20}'s panel replaces it.

\textbf{That predictions of this size are calibrated.} The slope of $0.877$ is
real and is now reproducible, but it is a whole-split statistic. On the
\texttt{inference\_4} held-out triples the same checkpoints give $0.24$, and the
head of that space runs far outside the range the model emits on labeled data.
Predicted $\tau$ is a rank, not an estimate.

\subsection{Not established\secstatus{todo}}

\textbf{Whether the ranking survives a disjoint split.} Every retrieval and
calibration number here comes from a split that is random over records. The
additive null falls from $0.400$ to $0.127 \pm 0.033$ once query doubles are made
disjoint, and the transformer has not been refit there. Since only 22 of the 420
query doubles are representable in the 526-gene space, this panel sits in that
unmeasured regime almost entirely.

\textbf{Whether tier A is an interaction claim or a gene claim.} Open, and now
mostly of historical interest since the panel it belongs to is withdrawn.
\texttt{YER059W} is enriched for strong interactions in both directions,
$16.3$ percent above $+0.08$ and $14.1$ percent below $-0.08$, so a large
measured effect of either sign is consistent with it being a high-variance gene
rather than with the specific triples being right. The same question recurs in
sharper form for the 20-strain panel, where \gene{LAT1} alone scores $+0.2441$
and outranks five of the six triples it appears in.

\textbf{Whether the nominated tail carries anything.} The window a panel is drawn
from holds 6 held-out measured triples, so nothing in this document validates it.
The enrichment that is measurable sits two orders of magnitude lower in the
ranking and rests on 2 positive calls.

\textbf{Whether the assay can report $\tau$ at all yet.} On the previous round
singles reproduced Costanzo fitness at $r = 0.706$ but doubles did not
($r = 0.255$), and measured digenic $\varepsilon$ against published
$\varepsilon$ gave $r = -0.245$. The diagnosis was a normalization artifact: the
denominator rests on a single wild-type colony, 8 of 12 singles scored above
on-plate wild type, and all 13 $\varepsilon$ came out negative with
$\mathrm{corr}(\varepsilon, f_a f_b) = -0.926$. That shift is undiagnosed, and no
correction should be applied before it is.

\textbf{The strain-count cost of this panel.} The between-plate standard
deviation of $0.140$ that the current layout advice rests on was an artifact of
one failed plate; clean plates agree to $0.028$, which inverts the advice from
``plates are the only lever'' to ``the colony term dominates''. The design has
not been re-planned since, and the record carries two incompatible figures for
what packing more strains costs.

\subsection{Next\secstatus{todo}}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Re-run the layout design at the corrected between-plate standard
    deviation before fixing the plate, and settle the strain-count cost.
  \item Fix the wild-type normalization, since $\tau$ closure is worthless if
    $\varepsilon$ is not scale-invariant.
  \item Train the transformer on the query-pair-disjoint split. Every claim here
    is conditional on a split whose leakage is quantified and whose replacement
    has not been run.
  \item Read the panel from the plating artifact each run rather than freezing a
    gene set as a constant, which is how the previous basis silently went stale.
  \item Record every cross attempt with a date and an outcome, including
    failures.
  \item Settle the tied optimum in the 20-strain design. Three panels reach the
    same median at the same cost and differ in their weakest member, $+0.020$
    against $+0.081$, and the objective cannot choose between them.
  \item Score the free-fatty-acid panel with the same checkpoints. It already has
    the structure this panel is built to create and all 720 orders are measured,
    so it costs one inference pass and no strains.
\end{enumerate}
