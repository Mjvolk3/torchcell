\section{What each candidate joins to\secstatus{todo}}\label{sec:synergies}

A dataset's value in a substrate is not only what it measures. It is also what becomes
measurable once it sits beside what is already held. Table~\ref{tab:synergies} names 116
such pairs across 66 candidates, each with the axis the two share.

\subsection{A join needs a shared axis, not a shared theme\secstatus{todo}}

The \term{join key} column is required rather than decorative. Two metabolomics papers are
not joinable because both measure metabolites; they are joinable when they measure
overlapping analytes on overlapping strains. The keys in use are the deletion collection,
the sequenced 1,011-isolate panel, a segregant panel from one cross, a guide library over
one gene set, a media formulation, a compound set, and a single gene. A pair with none of
these is a theme and is not listed.

Sixty-two of the 116 pairs have a partner that is already built, so that join becomes
runnable on the day the candidate is ingested rather than after two ingestions. Those are
the cheapest experiments in the table and none of them has been run.

Two kinds of key are distinguished in the table and the difference decides what a join can
do. A \emph{strain-level} key means both layers were measured on the same genotypes, so
the join is a paired observation and the residual between the layers is per strain. A
\emph{gene-level} key means one side is a single genome-wide coefficient, a half-life or a
translation rate, that applies to every strain alike. A gene-level layer cannot explain why
two strains differ; it explains why two genes differ, and the two are not interchangeable.

\subsection{The joins that motivate the ordering\secstatus{todo}}

Four groups carry most of the weight, and each is a specific question rather than an
accumulation.

\notesec{Transcript against protein, and the two terms between them} Steady-state
transcript and steady-state protein are both well covered, by the built Kemmeren 2014 and
Messner 2023 and by several rows here, and they disagree. Four rows make the disagreement
something a model can carry a term for rather than absorb as error. Grossbach 2022 measures
transcript, protein and phosphosite on one set of 112 sequenced strains, so the residual
between transcript and protein is observed within a genotype instead of across a
population. Teyssonniere 2024 does the strain-level version at species scale, 942 isolate
proteomes against the built Caudal 2024 transcriptomes on the same panel, and reports what
the pairing shows: the two correlate weakly and the variants associated with each overlap
by 3 percent. The remaining two are gene-level. McManus 2014 gives translation efficiency
beside mRNA abundance for 5,474 genes and finds ribosome occupancy more conserved than
transcript abundance, so translation buffers rather than tracks. Martin-Perez 2017 gives
turnover for 3,160 proteins, spanning three orders of magnitude, which is why a single
global mRNA-to-protein scaling cannot be right. Sun 2013 supplies the transcript-side
counterpart, decomposing mRNA level into synthesis and decay across 46 deletion strains.

\notesec{Cross-laboratory replication of the deletion transcriptome} Kemmeren 2014 is built
and is the largest genotype-indexed transcriptome with designed perturbations, and nothing
in the built set can check it. Hughes 2000, Hu 2007 and Sun 2013 all profile deletion
strains by expression, in three other laboratories and across fourteen years of array and
sequencing platforms, so agreement can be measured gene by gene on shared strains rather
than assumed. That check matters here specifically: the built Nadal-Ribelles 2025 does not
replicate its own genotype assignment across batches, so a second opinion on what a
deletion does to a transcriptome is not a formality.

\notesec{Metabolism against expression on one genotype axis} Blank 2005 measures flux
through central carbon metabolism on deletion mutants that already carry an expression
profile in the supported Kemmeren 2014 compendium, which is the only place in this table
where the transcript-to-flux step can be fitted rather than assumed. Cooper 2010 measures
the same amino-acid pools as the supported Mulleder 2016 by a different platform on an
overlapping genotype set, which separates biology from batch. Aulakh 2025 adds a second
metabolic panel, the ionome, on the whole collection. Above them sit Jakobson 2025 and
Albert 2018, protein and transcript variation on one sequenced segregant panel, and
Muenzner 2024 against the supported Caudal 2024, proteome and transcriptome on one isolate
panel.

\notesec{A condition axis measured on more than one layer} Brauer 2008 and Boer 2010 are
the transcript and metabolite halves of one chemostat series, same laboratory and same
limitations, and Hackett 2016 adds flux over the same conditions. Airoldi 2016, new to this
pass, supplies the nitrogen-limited counterpart, and its media are the media Jackson 2020
profiles single cells in. That last pairing is what makes a deletion effect in the
single-cell atlas a contrast against a wild-type baseline in the same medium rather than an
absolute.

\notesec{One perturbation, two modalities} Liu 2021 against Kuroda 2019 is isobutanol
tolerance on one collection by two readouts. Mukherjee 2021 against the supported
Mormino 2022 is acetic acid by knockdown at genome scale against twelve genes. Dong 2021
against the supported Lian 2019 is one tri-functional library read out by growth selection
and by a metabolite biosensor, which is how a tolerance screen becomes a production screen.
McGlincy 2021 against Momen-Roknabadi 2020 is two independently designed CRISPR
interference libraries over the same genes, which is the cheapest decisive test of whether
a model has learned gene function or guide design.

\notesec{The same genotype read out in bulk and per cell} Jackson 2020 against the
supported Kemmeren 2014 is the same transcription-factor deletions as a population average
and as a per-cell distribution, which is the only available calibration of what pseudobulk
loses. Hu 2007 extends the bulk side to 269 regulators. Boocock 2025 against Albert 2018 is
the same cross measured in bulk and per cell.

None of these transfers has been measured. Each is a hypothesis about what a model could
learn, and each becomes a run once both halves are held.
