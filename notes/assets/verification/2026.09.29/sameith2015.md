### S3. Paper design: biological replicates per strain, dye swaps, the common reference, and the statistical model for fold changes and epistasis
- Previously stated by: loader header comment lines 52-89 (quotes attributed to "Sameith et al. 2015, Cell Reports, Expression Profiling section"; constants `N_EXPECTED_BIOLOGICAL_REPLICATES = 2`, `N_EXPECTED_DYE_SWAP_TECHNICAL_REPLICATES = 2`, `N_EXPECTED_MAX_REPLICATES_DELETION = 4  # 2 biological × 2 dye-swap measurements`); note section "Technical Replicates" and "2026.01.29"
- Verdict: PARTLY CONFIRMED (the design facts are sourced; the loader's quotations are not from this paper, and its reading "2 biological x 2 dye-swap = 4 arrays" does not match the deposit)
- Evidence, verbatim:
  - Paper line 33 (Results): "WT cultures were grown and profiled alongside deletion mutants on each day to control for biological and technical variation. With the exception of single mutants that behave like WT, each mutant was grown and profiled four times from two independent cultures. Statistical modelling of the data results in an average expression profile for each mutant consisting of P values and fold changes (FC) for each gene relative to the average expression in a collection of WT cultures (Methods)."
  - Paper line 119 (Methods "Gene expression profiling"): "Full details of all gene expression profiling procedures have been described before [46]. In summary, deletion mutants were grown in rich medium (SC, supplemented with 2 % glucose) and harvested in early mid-log phase. WT cultures were grown alongside and processed in parallel. Dual-channel 70-mer oligonucleotide arrays were employed with WT RNA as common reference. All steps after RNA isolation were automated using robotic liquid handlers. These procedures were first optimized for accuracy (correct FC) and precision (reproducible result), using spiked-in RNA calibration [53]. After quality control, normalization and dye-bias correction [54], statistical analysis was performed for each mutant versus a collection of WT cultures [25, 46, 55]. Single mutants differing from WT as well as all double mutants were profiled another two times from an independently inoculated culture. The reported FC is then the average of four replicate mutant expression profiles versus the average of all WTs. Genes that show variable expression changes in the WT collection were excluded from further analyses (57 WT variable genes in total), as well as YDL196W."
  - Paper line 129 (Methods "Expression-based genetic interaction scores"), epistasis: "The epistatic effect of two GSTFs X and Y on the expression of a gene i was measured as the deviation between the expression change observed in the double mutant M_xΔyΔi and the expression change expected given the single mutants M_xΔi + M_yΔi (ε_txpn,XYi = |M_xΔyΔi - (M_xΔi + M_yΔi)|). The overall genetic interaction between the GSTFs X and Y was then scored by counting the total number of genes for which an unexpected expression change can be observed in the respective double mutant, whereby an FC of 1.5 was chosen as a threshold" (OCR'd formula symbols transcribed; only genes with P <= 0.01 in at least one of the three mutants are considered, line 129).
  - GEO series `!Series_overall_design`: "Two channel microarrays were used. RNA isolated from a large amount of wt yeast from a single culture was used as a common reference. This common reference was used in one of the channels for each hybridization and used in the statistical analysis to obtain an average expression-profile for each deletion mutant relative to the wt. Two independent cultures were hybridized on two separate microarrays. For the first hybridization the Cy5 (red) labeled cRNA from the deletion mutant is hybridized together with the Cy3 (green) labeled cRNA from the common reference. For the replicate hybridization, the labels are swapped. Each gene is represented twice on the microarray, resulting in four measurements per mutant. Using the Erlenmeyer growth protocol up to five deletion strains were grown on a single day. In the tecan platereader, up to eleven deletion strains could be grown on a single day. Wt cultures were grown parallel to the deletion mutants to assess day-to-day variance."
  - GEO `!Sample_data_processing` (every sample): "genes, no bg corr, dye corr: Normalization was done using print-tip LOESS as described in (Yang et al. 2002), by estimating the LOESS curve for all gene probes using no background substraction and a span of 0.4. Probes flagged as absent, or with a (nearly) saturated signal (i.e., > 2^15) in either channel were not considered for the estimation of the LOESS curve. Signals were corrected for dye bias using intrinsic gene-specific dye biases estimated from a set of wild-type wild-type hybridizations, as described in Margaritis, Lijnzaad et al., Mol. Sys. Biol. 2009." and "limma: ... The limma R package version 2.12.0 is used. P-values are Benjamini-Hochberg FDR corrected."
  - The deferral target, Kemmeren 2014 (`kemmerenLargeScaleGeneticPerturbations2014/paper.md` line 130): "Each mutant strain (Table S1) was grown twice, from two independently inoculated cultures. Cultures were harvested early during exponential growth in SC medium with 2% glucose. Each culture was expression-profiled in technical replicate to yield four measurements for each profiling mutant. To monitor reproducibility, a common reference design was adopted with a batch of WT RNA applied in dye-swap to one of the channels of each microarray (Figure S1B)." and line 307: "Two independent cultures were hybridized on two separate microarrays. ... For the replicate hybridization from the independent cultures, the labels are swapped. Each gene is represented twice on the microarray, resulting in four measurements per mutant." This is the source of the sentences the loader attributes to "Sameith et al. 2015, Cell Reports"; they do not occur in the Sameith paper.
  - What the deposit holds (gsm_table.csv, sm_contamination.py): 143 double-mutant arrays for 72 pairs, exactly 2 per pair except `cin5-del+yap6-del-2-a` (1 array; SI comment on that pair: `2 replicates`); 144 single-mutant arrays for 82 genes, 62 genes with 2 arrays and 20 with 1. ## New findings not previously recorded
- Wrong PubMed ID in every served record. The loader's `Publication(pubmed_id="26687005", pubmed_url="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26687005/")` (lines 824-830 and 1914-1920) points at "Many lncRNAs, 5'UTRs, and pseudogenes are translated and some are likely to express functional proteins", eLife 2015 (PubMed esummary, fetched 2026-09-29). The Sameith paper is PMID 26700642 (esummary title "A high-resolution gene expression atlas of epistasis between gene-specific transcription factors exposes potential mechanisms for genetic interactions", BMC Biol 2015 Dec 23, DOI 10.1186/s12915-015-0222-5), which is also GEO's `!Series_pubmed_id = 26700642`. Both dev LMDBs store 26687005 (checked on DM key 6). The DOI fields are correct.
- All 72 pairs are in GEO and in the dev DM LMDB; the note's "69 found / 3 missing" is stale (S4).
- SM records are contaminated by double-mutant arrays: 143 double-mutant arrays folded into 45 of 82 SM records, `n_replicates` up to 14, and 8 arrays keyed to the second gene of the title (S5).
- The loader's replicate quotations ("Each mutant strain was grown twice ...", "This included four replicates per responsive mutant ...", "a common reference design was adopted with a batch of WT RNA ...", "Additional WT cultures were grown alongside ...") are from Kemmeren et al. 2014 Cell (mirror `kemmerenLargeScaleGeneticPerturbations2014/paper.md` lines 32, 130), not from "Sameith et al. 2015, Cell Reports". The `verification_report.json` provenance `page` field for both datasets also says "Cell Reports, Expression Profiling". The Sameith Methods defer to that paper ("[46]"), so the chain is Sameith -> Kemmeren 2014, and it should be recorded that way.
- "Four measurements per mutant" = 2 arrays (one per independent culture, dye-swapped between them) x 2 probes per gene. GSE42536 holds 2 arrays per double mutant (1 for cin5+yap6), 2 arrays for 62 singles and 1 array for 20 singles (the SI's `2 replicates` rows, the WT-like single mutants profiled once). The loader constants describe 2 x 2 arrays, which never occurs. Two probes per ORF (6128 ORFs with 2 probes, 12 with 4 on GPL11232, `ORF` column, 15552 rows) are collapsed to one by the loader's probe map (#459 comment already records this half-loss).

- The cin5+yap6 DM record (key 6, `YDR259C+YOR028C`) is built from one array: `n_replicates = 1`, SE and variance NaN for all 6169 genes. The L2 `se_nonnegative` check reported 444168 values with 0 bad, so NaN is evidently skipped by that check.

After the strain fix, the record's `genome_reference.strain` (BY4741) and the strain of the reference RNA (BY4742) differ; the schema has one `genome_reference` on the reference object, so this needs a decision on what that field means for a two-channel array.

## What the public showcase page can state safely
- Sameith et al. 2015, BMC Biology 13:112, DOI 10.1186/s12915-015-0222-5, PMID 26700642 (PubMed esummary; GEO `!Series_pubmed_id`). Data: GEO GSE42536 on platform GPL11232 (two-channel 70-mer oligonucleotide arrays), also ArrayExpress E-MTAB-1385 (paper line 147).
- 82 single-deletion and 72 double-deletion GSTF mutants passed the authors' quality control (paper line 115: "In total, 154 deletion mutants passed our quality control criteria"; SI sheets), and arrays for all 154 are in GSE42536 (287 arrays: 144 single-mutant, 143 double-mutant; S4).
- Background: S288c-isogenic BY4742 (MATalpha) for all single mutants and 68 double mutants; BY4741 (MATa) for four double mutants, HAC1+SNT1, SNT1+SPT2, CUP2+HAA1, SIP4+YER184C (paper line 115; SI `MATa` comments; GEO `strain: BY4741` on those 8 arrays).
- Single mutants come from the Saccharomyces Genome Deletion library (Euroscarf / Open Biosystems), six of them remade in the authors' lab (paper line 115; SI `mutant source`). Double mutants were made by haploid transformation, random spore analysis or tetrad dissection (paper line 115).
- Growth: SC medium with 2% glucose, 30 C, harvested in early mid-log phase (paper line 119; GEO protocols file).
- Design: common-reference two-channel arrays with WT RNA in one channel; each mutant hybridized from two independent cultures on two arrays with the dyes swapped between them; two probes per gene on the array; WT-like single mutants profiled once (GEO `!Series_overall_design`; paper lines 33 and 119; SI `2 replicates` rows).
- Processing: print-tip LOESS normalization without background subtraction, gene-specific dye-bias correction (Margaritis et al. 2009), limma 2.12.0 with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR, each mutant versus a collection of same-day WT cultures (GEO `!Sample_data_processing`; paper line 119).
- Expression-based epistasis: ε_txpn,XYi = |M_xΔyΔi - (M_xΔi + M_yΔi)|, counted over genes with |ε| > log2(1.5) among genes significant (P <= 0.01) in at least one of the three mutants (paper line 129).
- What torchcell stores: per record, log2(mutant/refpool) recomputed from the normalized channel signals with the mutant channel taken from `source_name_ch1` (ch1 = Cy5 on all arrays), averaged over that mutant's arrays; the deleted gene's own probe is negative on 97.5% (SM) and 97.9% (DM) of records with median -2.06 / -1.99 (S6).
- Not to state until fixed: the four BY4741 records (stored as BY4742), the SM records for genes that also appear in double mutants (45 of 82 mix single and double arrays), the NatMX/KanMX marker assignment, the PubMed ID, and any replicate count phrased as "2 biological x 2 dye-swap arrays".
