\section{Design\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:design}

\notesec{Vocabulary}
Every arm is the equivariant cell graph transformer of experiment 010 trained on a subset
of the 025 build (13.5 million records) and scored on trigenic interaction alone.

\begin{description}
\item[composite] A fixed per-gene vector of 3{,}328 dimensions: promoter (768), open
  reading frame as codons under CaLM (768), open reading frame as protein under ProtT5
  (1{,}024) and terminator (768), entered through a two-layer preprocessor whose width
  (339) matches the table's parameter count to 0.13 percent.
\item[fit.] Joint fitness supervision: the model predicts fitness and interaction, both
  at loss weight 1.0, with a perturbed classification token and a linear readout.
\item[Q] The query-pair-disjoint split. A trigenic screen crosses one double-mutant query
  strain against an array of single mutants; 420 query pairs recur, 89 are held out, and
  every triple of a held-out pair is in validation or test (37{,}705 and 37{,}791).
\item[R] The random split of experiment 010, carried onto the 025 build by genotype
  identity: 376{,}732 of 376{,}732 triples matched, 301{,}386 train, 37{,}673 validation,
  37{,}673 test.
\item[S0] The 376{,}732 triples.
\item[S3] S0, the 5{,}694 singles, and the 739{,}219 closure doubles, those whose gene pair
  lies inside some triple: 1{,}121{,}645 records. Every record outside the pinned split
  trains.
\item[S3 strict] S3 without the 85 doubles that are themselves a held-out query pair of
  Q: 1{,}121{,}560 records. The 145{,}208 doubles pairing one held-out query gene with an
  array gene stay, since digenic screens supply them before any trigenic screen is run.
\item[S4] S0, the singles, and 739{,}219 random doubles whose pair lies inside no triple.
  Size-matched to S3 and sharing none of its doubles.
\item[score] The mean of validation Pearson on trigenic interaction over epochs 10 to 29,
  reported only when all 20 epochs are logged. The maximum over epochs is an upward-biased
  order statistic and is never the score.
\item[table] A learned $6{,}607 \times 180$ gene embedding, 1{,}189{,}260 parameters.
\end{description}

\notesec{Reads declared before the runs}
Each planned cell differs from its comparator in one factor, and the $\Delta$ column of
Table~\ref{tab:campaign} is that difference.
S3 against S0 on R with the table tests whether added fitness records help; three seeds
all above the S0 range is the criterion, and the S0 seed spread is 0.003.
S4 against S3 separates the triples' own pairs from doubles in general. The stored-value
identity linking a triple's score to its doubles reaches only $r = 0.23$ on this build, so
S4 matching S3 is a live outcome and would restate the claim as one about data volume.
The composite against the table on R, at S0 and at S3, tests whether the transferable
representation matches the table when every gene is seen in training.
S3 strict against S0 on Q asks the same data question where query pairs are unseen, under
both representations, and whether the table closes on the composite once the held-out
triples' genes appear in training doubles.
The single-region arms on Q rank CaLM, ProtT5 and the flanks; one seed each cannot, since
seeds of one arm spread by 0.015 to 0.033.
