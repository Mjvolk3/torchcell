\pfstep{Every arm here is transductive in the sense the figure needs} The revealed modality
is a gene-level measurement of the \emph{same} strain, present on every training, validation
and test strain of the arm (\texttt{require\_modalities} keeps only genotypes carrying both
labels, \cref{tab:morph-strains}), and it enters through the observed-label channel on
every step with nothing masked, so the model is applied to a strain whose other modality
is known. Morphology itself can be a target and not an input: the observed-label channel
takes per-gene values, and CalMorph is one vector per cell population. The genotype-only
arms (\texttt{M\_cls}, \texttt{MP\_cls}) are the inductive controls on exactly the same
strains.

\pfstep{What the sketch asks of the runs} Panel d is the figure's claim and its only open
measurement: twelve paired differences per contrast, scored on the window rule, with the
ridge from the same measured modality as the bar (0.280 from the proteome, 0.324 from
expression, on the moving features). Panel c read the genotype-only model at
0.046 $\pm$ 0.015 against a ceiling of 0.847 when this section was written; rounds v25 and
v26 (\cref{sec:inductive}) moved it to 0.149 $\pm$ 0.022 by changing the readout and the loss,
within the seed spread of the proteome-as-input arm, so the SI figure's reading is no longer
that the genotype alone does not predict morphology but that both it and the measured input
sit at a sixth of the ceiling, and the measured-input contrast has to be redrawn on the new
base.

\pfstep{The 1{,}011 isolates are training data, not a panel} No morphology is measured on
the Peter 2018 isolates, so they cannot be a morphology panel. The idea behind box g is
narrower: the deletion panels vary expression little from strain to strain, and about a
thousand natural isolates with the Caudal 2024 transcriptome (served) and the Muenzner 2024
proteome (first in the next-build band of the expansion document) would give the expression
and proteome heads far larger between-strain variation to learn from; the test is then
whether those heads, and the morphology arms of panel d that read them, improve on the
deletion strains. The open question before any run is how an isolate's genotype enters a
model whose perturbation is a set of deleted genes. Hypothesis, untested. It is noted here so
the slot exists; it is unlikely to be filled in this round.
