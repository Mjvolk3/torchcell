\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Gene essentiality is the one endpoint on which a flux model has a published, non-trivial
score: on the released Merzbacher 2025 yeast split, flux balance analysis reads an AUROC of
0.69 over all 1,121 labeled genes and Flux Cone Learning 0.73 on the 223 held-out genes.
This document scores the cell graph transformer on the same held-out genes, puts FBA on
every medium the ontology defines, and asks two questions of the metabolic module: whether a
prediction read off its growth column ranks essential genes better than the backbone alone,
and where the gradient goes when the module is one of two paths rather than the only one.
Every number here is at a validation-selected epoch on a pinned test set, with a gene
bootstrap and the across-seed range, and every table is generated from the result files.
\end{abstract}
