<StatusBadge status="planned" />

Planned: start from a compound and retrieve the experiments whose environment
contains that compound or a structurally similar one. Chemical similarity here means
a similarity score between two molecular structures. The intended use is to find what
is already measured for a compound of interest, including measurements made with its
close analogs. The compound input format, the similarity measure, and the threshold
are not decided.
