## 2026.09.29 - One row per cell

Reads the processed LMDB of build 001 once and writes
`$DATA_ROOT/experiments/033-env-chemgen-pooled/cell_table/cell_table.parquet`, 6,042,771
rows and 35 columns. Slurm 2988 did the pass in 4 m 58 s on 16 workers. Every measurement a
cell folds is kept as a list, because which one a trainer reads is a label policy. A
perturbation that is neither a deletion nor a copy-number variant raises, and so does an
unknown concentration unit. `--start`, `--stop` and `--stride` read a slice for a smoke run.
