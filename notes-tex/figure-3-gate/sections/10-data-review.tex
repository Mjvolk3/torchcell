\section{What the campaign has measured, and what else could feed morphology\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:data-review}

\begin{keybox}
\textbf{2{,}518 runs in 33 W\&B projects}, 1{,}766 before SIMB (July) and 734 after
(September on), \cref{tab:wandb-campaign-census}. Before SIMB almost no run reached its
budget (early stopping on the validation loss, patience 20, was the trainer default), so the
July rows are end-window scores and are not comparable with the later rounds.

\textbf{Best window means by target}, after SIMB. Expression from genotype 0.180 $\pm$ 0.006
(v12 \texttt{H\_concat}, 4 runs; a single 10{,}000-epoch run reached 0.233). Proteome from
genotype 0.085 $\pm$ 0.008 (v16, 12 runs); with the strain's expression as input 0.125 $\pm$
0.027 against 0.072 permuted (v20, 12 runs each). Morphology from genotype 0.046 $\pm$ 0.015
(v24, 12 runs, 116 moving features), raised to 0.149 $\pm$ 0.022 by the gene-token readout
with MSE (v26, 12 runs, \cref{sec:inductive}); with the strain's expression as input 0.122
$\pm$ 0.066 (v24, 12 runs); with its proteome 0.145 $\pm$ 0.030 (v24, 12 runs).

\textbf{Other served data} (\cref{tab:morph-input-screen}, one seed). The chemogenomic
growth matrices predict the moving features nearly as well as the proteome does: Hoepfner
2014 HOP 0.263 on 4{,}402 strains and Hillenmeyer 2008 HOP 0.246 on 4{,}597, against Messner
0.282 on 4{,}314 and Kemmeren 0.345 on 1{,}438. The three growth panels together reach 0.310
on the 4{,}075 strains all four cover, where Messner reads 0.240 and adds 0.016 on top of
them.

\textbf{The 1{,}011 isolates} do not reach morphology (none is measured on them) and cannot
enter the current model (an isolate is a set of about 5{,}300 perturbed gene names with no
allele information). They are a proteome-program build, not a morphology lever.
\end{keybox}

\pfstep{What the campaign has shown, in one paragraph} From the genotype alone, through
this trunk, the three targets sit at 0.18, 0.09 and 0.05 against replicate ceilings of
0.77 for expression and 0.85 for the moving morphology features (the proteome ceiling is
the HIS3-replicate estimator of \cref{fig:mockup-a}b), and the nearest-neighbor and ridge
baselines on the same representations sit within 0.01 of the model (\cref{sec:mockups}). Revealing the strain's
own measurement of another modality is the only intervention that has moved a target by
more than its seed spread: +0.05 on the proteome from expression (v20), and, so far on one
split seed, +0.10 on morphology from the proteome. Width, data fraction, mask schedules,
stacking and the Hadamard variant moved nothing beyond noise (\cref{sec:round-complete}). The
figure's honest claim is therefore about measurements predicting a cell-level phenotype,
not about the genotype encoder.

\pfstep{What else in the database predicts morphology} The ridge test the proteome and
expression screens used was run on every served per-deletion matrix
(\cref{tab:morph-input-screen}). Two findings. First, the multi-condition growth matrices
are nearly as informative as the proteome and cover three times the strains of the
expression panel; their condition count matters (the 14-condition Costanzo 2021 and the
34-condition Vanacloig 2022 read 0.11 and 0.13, the 249- and 300-condition HOP sets 0.25
and 0.26), and one-column product screens read 0.05 to 0.13. Second, Ohnuki 2022, CalMorph
on 1{,}982 of the same deletions in a three-deletion background, predicts Ohya morphology
at 0.334 and adds 0.093 to Messner, which is morphology predicting morphology across
backgrounds, a transfer measure and not a new modality. Caveats, all unmeasured: part of the growth signal
is likely a shared slow-growth axis (removing it cut the proteome screen by about a third);
the growth media are not the CalMorph culture; and a growth profile exists only for a strain
already screened, so an arm that reads it is transductive in the same sense as the
proteome arms.

\pfstep{Getting a growth profile into the model} The observed-label channel takes one value
per gene node, which is why expression and the proteome enter it and morphology cannot. A
chemogenomic profile is indexed by condition, not by gene, so it needs the same strain-level
input path a morphology input would: a projection of the strain vector added to the CLS
row before the global head. That path does not exist in the trainer. Proposal, not run:
build a store joining Hoepfner, Hillenmeyer and Ohya (about 4{,}400 strains, more than the
proteome store's 4{,}281), add the strain-vector input, and run the same paired design as
v24 (own profile, permuted profile, genotype only).

\pfstep{The 1{,}011 isolates, assessed} Caudal 2024 holds 943 isolates, RNA-seq TPM per
gene against the population mean, SC at 30\,$^\circ$C (Kemmeren's medium), 5{,}811 of
Kemmeren's 6{,}169 genes, replicate ceiling 0.60 against Kemmeren's 0.77. Muenzner 2024
(796 isolates, 1{,}576 proteins, 761 also in Caudal) is the better-matched set for the
proteome head: same laboratory, same SWATH pipeline and the same minimal medium as Messner.
The schema represents an isolate (sequence variants, natural gene presence and absence,
copy number), but every graph processor reduces a genotype to its gene-name set, so an
isolate would enter the cell graph transformer as about 5{,}300 perturbed genes with no
allele and every accessory gene dropped, nearly the same token set for every isolate. The
018 setup note already states the requirement: per-strain node features (allele-specific
embeddings) and one decoder head per platform. No isolate morphology exists beyond Yvert
2013 (37 strains). Hypothesis, untested: the isolates would improve the shared encoder and
reach morphology only through cross-task transfer, which this campaign has found small
next to a revealed measurement. The build belongs to the proteome program.

\pfstep{Candidates not yet built that match the finding} Galardini 2019 (38 growth
conditions on 3{,}786 deletions in four sequenced backgrounds; GEO and GitHub, public),
Parsons 2006 (82 compounds on about 5{,}000 deletions; accession unconfirmed) and the wider
YeastPhenome (7{,}536 environments; the dev build holds 80 and reads 0.287) are the same
class as the HOP matrices. Lee 2014, the largest of the class, is blocked on author data.
Puddu 2019 (whole-genome sequencing of the deletion collection) is a genotype correction
rather than an input, and the hypothesis that part of the 0.046 against 0.847 gap is strain
background the deletion token cannot express is untested.
