\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Gene essentiality is the one endpoint on which a flux model has a published, non-trivial
score: on the released Merzbacher 2025 yeast split, flux balance analysis reads an AUROC of
0.69 over all 1,121 labeled genes and Flux Cone Learning 0.73 on the 223 held-out genes.
This document scores the cell graph transformer on the same held-out genes, puts FBA on
every medium the ontology defines and a classical ladder of models on the same split, and
asks two questions. The first is how far the held-out AUROC goes: a width-12 transformer
reading the chromosome-pathway indicator and ProtT5, with FBA's four growth ratios on its
logit, scores 0.893 [0.84, 0.94] under Flux Cone Learning's own fold protocol, 0.15 above
it on the same genes, and every part of the gain is an input the ontology computes for
every gene at no training cost. The second is why the solve helps and the metabolic
module, which is built on the same genome-scale model, does not: routed through the
module's growth column the prediction never beats the backbone alone, and the rounds
locate the reason in the layer, whose alternating projections never reach the polytope
FBA optimizes over and which discards the solve when handed it. Every number here is at a
validation-selected epoch on a pinned test set, with a gene bootstrap and the across-seed
range, and every table is generated from the result files.
\end{abstract}
