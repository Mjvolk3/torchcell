\section{The endpoint, the protocol and the stores\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:endpoint}

M\"ulleder's screen measures 19 intracellular amino acids, in mM, on the prototrophic
deletion collection (markers restored) grown on synthetic minimal medium, one culture
per strain, 4,678 strains. Every cell is scored under the 027 protocol: Merzbacher's 639
test genes pinned to test, the epoch selected on validation Pearson (centered five-epoch
moving average), and the mean per-amino-acid pinned-test Spearman over the 19 reported;
the comparators are the 027 rounds' own numbers on the same split, read from their
result files (Table~\ref{tab:target-transfer-comparators}). The backbone is the
essentiality one (pathway multi-hot with ProtT5, width 12, one block, four attention
heads, CRPS on the head, 200 epochs), a fifth of the width 027 trained, with a 19-output
head on the mean channel. The graph regularizer is requested on every cell and never
evaluated at width 12: it is pinned to the second block, and these cells have one, so
\texttt{graph\_reg\_loss} is 0 at all 200 epochs. It is inert in the control and in the
arm alike, so the pairing is unaffected, and every arm is paired by seed over six seeds
against the same backbone with no affine. The screen's own strain-level rank retest is
0.20 (Section~\ref{sec:concordance}).

The study began inside the essentiality campaign (rounds 118 onward there) and has been
its own study, 032, since 2026-09-23, with its own results tree, W\&B project and round
series (A1 onward; the map from the round numbers used here to the A-series is in the
study's note). The betaxanthin half of the same campaign is the sibling document,
\texttt{notes-tex/031-betaxanthin-module}, and the essentiality endpoint the backbone and
the store were built on is \texttt{notes-tex/028-gene-essentiality}.

The store represents the strain, not the wild type. For the amino acids
(\texttt{aa\_reference\_flux\_table.py}, job 2751) the model is the unmodified prototroph
on the dataset's medium; per GEM gene one parsimonious growth solve and 19 linear
programs, each amino acid's synthesis maximum at 90 percent of own growth, kept as
summary columns beside the growth vector rather than as 19 further vectors. On the graph
side the processor had removed the two allele genes and the BTS1 copy from every cell
graph, which this strain never carried; round 124's \texttt{--deletion-only-graph}
leaves the graph as the strain is.
