\section{Open\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:open}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The FBA ratios enter as a readout affine (Round 5). Carrying them as a node
    feature instead, so the transformer sees every gene's ratio and not only the deleted
    gene's, is the untested form; and the ratio on the medium the phenotype was scored on
    alone, against all four, says whether the four columns add anything beyond one.
  \item The flux layer's growth column matched the head and ignored its $k_{cat}$ table
    (Rounds 2 and 4) while the FBA solve added 0.05; a flux arm that receives the FBA
    ratios beside its own growth column tests whether the learned layer adds anything on
    top of the solve.
  \item The module's own hyperparameters have not been moved on this endpoint: the
    $k_{cat}$ tier (measured against predicted), the thermodynamic source, the
    parameterization (projection against nullspace), the number of alternations.
  \item Every cell here is width 12. The depth-against-width isolation (width 90 at one
    block, width 12 at two) has not run on the split-file protocol.
\end{itemize}
