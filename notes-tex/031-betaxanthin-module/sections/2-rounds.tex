\section{The rounds\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:rounds}

Round 117 (jobs 2741 and 2742, w134) reads the whole betaxanthin vector through the
zero-initialized affine. Round 119 (jobs 2749 and 2750, w136) reads two summary columns
only, the growth ratio and the production ratio, and separately the whole vector with
the affine in its own optimizer group at weight decay 0.1 and a tenth of the rate. Round
120 (w137) is the deletion-only graph, and the six rule-count columns of the
essentiality study's round 77 over every GEM gene. Round 122 (w139) is the affine-free
control on the backbone 020 and 027 trained, width 90, nine attention heads, two
blocks, where the graph regularizer is evaluated; a first build at width 48 with four
heads died in that term on every cell (jobs 2757 and 2758, the round-8 failure the
essentiality document records). Round 123 (w140) puts the store affine on that backbone,
and on the deletion-only graph at width 12; round 125 (w142) splits the store into its
growth block and its production block, 4,178 and 4,177 columns, each read alone. Round
127 (w144) adds six seeds to the best cell. Round 128 (w145) is the store on the
deletion-only graph at width 90, and round 132 (w149) the wide control on that graph
without the store, which completes the width-90 two-by-two of graph against store.
Rounds 130 and 131 (w147, w148) answer the plateau finding of rounds 117, 123 and 125
directly: the store on the deletion-only graph with the affine's parameters frozen
(\texttt{--ess-affine-start-epoch}) until epoch 40, and separately until the trailing
five-epoch mean of validation Pearson reaches 0.15
(\texttt{--ess-affine-start-val-pearson}), so the backbone escapes before the affine can
carry the target. A zero-initialized affine without gradient contributes exactly 0, so
each cell is its control until the start epoch, which the result records. Rounds 133 to
142 screen the essentiality finalists and the physically constrained stores of
Section~\ref{sec:physics}, every one at width 12 on the deletion-only graph over six
seeds, paired against round 120's control on that graph and against round 123's store on
it: round 133 (w152) the vector with the six rule-count columns beside it, the
essentiality study's best single model; round 134 (w153) the cached solve head, a
hidden-256 perceptron over the store, in place of the affine; round 135 (w154) the linear
minimization-of-metabolic-adjustment store; round 139 (w156) the parsimonious store
rebuilt on the HiGHS two-stage path the essentiality stores use; round 140 (w157) the
max-min driving-force concentration store; round 142 (w159) the mean of six samples of
each deletion's flux cone. Rounds 144 and 145 (w161, w162) read the size question on the
best cell, width 6 with two heads and transformer dropout 0.3 against the incumbent
width 12 at 0.1. The stores are written on the target model over the source store's
genes and reaction order, with the growth-ratio column unchanged, so each pair isolates
the vector.

Round 117 is read (Table~\ref{tab:target-transfer}). On betaxanthin the whole-vector
affine reads a pinned Spearman of 0.121 (sd 0.011 over six seeds) against the control's
0.079 (sd 0.056): $+0.043$ paired by seed (sd 0.049), above on five of six. What the
seeds show is a change of character more than of level: the control's score depends on
whether its seed leaves the plateau (0.024 to 0.174), the store arm's does not (0.102 to
0.133). Three store seeds were selected on the plateau, at validation Pearson $-0.02$
and epochs 127 to 181, and still read 0.12 to 0.13 on test, so the affine's linear read
of the strain's flux vector carries that rank on its own, on a test set that is 636 of
639 GEM genes. The selection rule cannot see it: the pool the validation set is drawn
from holds 275 GEM genes in 4,082, and the affine reads a constant row on every other
gene, so validation Pearson is nearly blind to the one term that scores on the pinned
test. Averaged over the six seeds the store arm reads 0.135 (the two seed triples 0.146
and 0.133) and the control 0.076, against 027's precursor readout at 0.131 over three
seeds at width 90, its context-only control at 0.115, and the cosine kNN at 0.145. On
the metric the task is about, the top-50 enrichment of the highest producers, the order
reverses: 3.49 for the store arm against 4.06 for the control on single seeds, 4.39
against 4.59 ensembled (a 10 percent base rate, so 35 to 46 percent precision). The
vector lifts the rank correlation of a small backbone to where 027's larger one sat and
does not lift its top-50 call.

Rounds 119 and 120 are read, and they locate the round-117 gain in the whole vector
read through a free affine. The two summary columns read 0.101 (sd 0.061) against the
control's 0.079, $+0.023$ paired (sd 0.072), above on four of six: three seeds were
selected at epoch 197 at validation Pearson 0.04 to 0.05 and read 0.13 on test, the
round-117 pattern, and one seed reads $-0.021$, so the level comes with the control's
spread rather than the whole vector's. Ensembled over six seeds it reads 0.071, under the
control's 0.076, and its single-seed top-50 enrichment is 3.03. The whole vector with the
affine decayed and slowed reads 0.073 (sd 0.017), $-0.005$ paired (sd 0.058), above on
three of six; three of its seeds were selected at epoch 2 at validation Pearson 0, where
the affine has taken two steps, and read 0.05 to 0.08. So the rate the affine trains at
is part of what round 117 measured, and slowing it returns the arm to the control. The
deletion-only graph without an affine reads 0.058 (sd 0.075), $-0.020$ paired (sd
0.076), above on two of six, with one seed at $-0.089$ and every selection between
epochs 3 and 69; the six rule-count columns read 0.074 (sd 0.059), $-0.004$ paired (sd
0.092), above on two of six. Neither the graph nor the rule counts moves the rank, in
either direction, beyond the seed spread. One read cuts across all four: the six-seed
ensemble's top-50 enrichment is 4.8 to 5.2 for every arm of rounds 119 and 120 against
3.0 to 3.6 for their single seeds, and 4.59 for the control's ensemble, so on the call
the task is about, averaging seeds is worth more than any of these representations, as
it was on the essentiality endpoint (the essentiality document's fold-ensemble section). Each ensemble is one
number with no spread, so that ordering among the arms is not a measurement.

The deletion-only half of round 123 is read (job 2760, 3 h 34 min for six cells at a
32.6 GB peak): the store affine on the graph that carries the two alleles, the strain as
built. Against the deletion-only graph without the affine it reads 0.121 (sd 0.025)
against 0.058, $+0.063$ paired (sd 0.065), above on six of six; against the round-117
store arm on the old graph it reads $-0.000$ paired (sd 0.017), above on four of six, so
the rank the affine carries is the same on either graph. What changes is where the
seeds are selected and what the call is worth. Five of six were selected off the
plateau, at validation Pearson 0.20 to 0.27 and epochs 46 to 105, where round 117's
seeds sat on it at $-0.02$; the sixth (seed 2704) was selected on the plateau at epoch
149 and its pinned predictions are rank-identical to round 117's seed 2704 (Spearman 1.0
between the two arms, largest difference 0.004), which is what a plateau cell is: the
affine's read of the store and nothing from the graph. The top-50 enrichment is 4.93 on
single seeds against 3.49 for the store on the old graph, 3.39 for the deletion-only
graph alone and 4.06 for the control, and 5.59 ensembled over six seeds, the highest of
any arm; the six-seed rank is 0.131. So the strain represented as built is the first
cell where the store lifts both the rank and the call, and it is the arm the width and
block rounds are now measured against.

The betaxanthin half of round 122 is read (jobs 2762 and 2763, 7 h 11 min and 7 h 23
min for three cells each at a 16.5 GB peak): the affine-free control on 027's backbone,
width 90, nine heads, two blocks, on the old graph. It reads 0.109 (sd 0.053) against
the width-12 control's 0.079, $+0.030$ paired (sd 0.079), above on four of six, with a
top-50 enrichment of 4.73 against 4.06; ensembled over six seeds 0.134 and 5.59, where
027's own three-seed cell of this form read 0.115. Every wide seed leaves the plateau,
selected at epochs 9 to 107 with validation Pearson 0.16 to 0.36, where the width-12
arms lose a third of their seeds to it; the validation loss bottoms at a median epoch of
42 and then rises by 0.01 to 0.11 to epoch 199, and two seeds fall back to a constant
predictor by the end. So width buys the escape and the early peak, and 200 epochs is
four times what these cells use. Against the width-12 store affine on the corrected
graph (0.121, 4.93, ensembled 0.131 and 5.59), the seven-times-wider backbone without
the store reads the same on every measure, which is the size of the store's
contribution at width 12. Whether the two add is round 128 (w145), the store on the
corrected graph at width 90.

The wide half of round 123 is read (jobs 2766 and 2767, 7 h 25 min and 7 h 20 min):
the store affine on 027's backbone, old graph. It reads 0.134 (sd 0.020) against the
wide control's 0.109, $+0.025$ paired (sd 0.051), above on four of six, and ensembled
over six seeds 0.153, the highest rank of any cell on this split, above 027's precursor
readout at 0.131 and the cosine kNN at 0.145. The call moves the other way, as in round
117: top-50 enrichment 4.09 against the wide control's 4.73 on single seeds and 5.19
against 5.59 ensembled. The seeds show why. Four of the six were selected on the plateau,
at validation Pearson $-0.06$ to $0.06$ and epochs 9 to 196, where the wide control had
no plateau seed at all; the two that escaped were selected at 0.29 and 0.30. So at width
90 the affine's read of the store carries the rank on its own while the backbone stays
constant on most seeds, and the top-50 call, which the escaped backbone supplies,
follows the escape rate. On the old graph the width-12 store had three plateau seeds of
six and the corrected graph one; round 128 puts the store on the corrected graph at
width 90.

Round 125 is read (jobs 2768 and 2769, 4 h 29 min and 4 h 38 min): the whole vector's
two blocks read alone, at width 12 on the old graph, each paired against the whole
vector of round 117 and against the control. The growth block alone, 4,178 columns,
reads 0.033 (sd 0.021): $-0.088$ paired against the whole vector (sd 0.029), above on
no seed, and $-0.046$ against the control (sd 0.064), above on one of six, with a top-50
enrichment of 3.06; ensembled 0.059. The production block alone, 4,177 columns, reads
0.091 (sd 0.040): $-0.030$ against the whole vector (sd 0.034), above on one of six, and
$+0.013$ against the control (sd 0.021), above on five of six, top-50 3.63; ensembled
0.112. So the rank the affine carries lives in the production half, the parsimonious
solve at 90 percent of the knockout's own growth with the betaxanthin demand as the
objective, and the growth half carries none of it on its own and costs the control
0.046 when read alone; yet the whole vector beats the production half by 0.030 on five
of six seeds, so the growth columns add to the production columns what they subtract
from the backbone. Three seeds of each half were selected on the plateau, as with the
whole vector, and the validation loss bottoms at a median epoch of 53 on both.

Round 127 is read (job 2771, 3 h 40 min): six more seeds, 2706 to 2711, of the store on
the corrected graph, so the best betaxanthin cell has twelve. The new six read 0.058 to
0.138, mean 0.111, every one selected off the plateau at validation Pearson 0.15 to
0.22, so over twelve seeds the arm reads 0.116 (sd 0.027, standard error 0.008) with a
top-50 enrichment of 4.86. Averaging all twelve reads 0.129 and 5.39 against 0.131 and
5.59 for the first six, so the ensemble has saturated at six on this cell: the second
six seeds add nothing to the rank or the call, and the ensemble numbers of the transfer
arms, one draw each, are read as sitting within about 0.02 and 0.2 of where more seeds
would put them.

Round 128 is read (jobs 2772 and 2773, 7 h 04 min and 7 h 31 min for three cells each):
the store affine on 027's backbone, width 90, on the deletion-only graph, the fourth cell
of the width-90 two-by-two. It reads 0.120 (sd 0.024) against the wide control on the old
graph at 0.109, $+0.011$ paired (sd 0.067), above on three of six, and against the
width-12 store on the same graph at 0.121, $-0.001$ paired (sd 0.035), above on three
of six; top-50 enrichment 4.43 against 4.73 and 4.93. Ensembled over six seeds it reads
0.147 and 4.99, under the old-graph wide store's 0.153 and the width-12 corrected-graph
store's 5.59. What the graph correction changes at width 90 is the plateau, as it did at
width 12: every seed is selected off it, at epochs 21 to 50 with validation Pearson 0.22
to 0.28, where the old-graph wide store had four plateau seeds of six. The rank does not
follow. Width buys the escape (round 122), the store buys the rank at width 12 (round
123), and put together on the strain as built they read what either reads alone, so the
two contributions do not add, and the seed spread (sd 0.024) is the smallest of the four
wide cells. The validation loss bottoms at epochs 8 to 42 and rises 0.09 to 0.15 by epoch
199 on every seed, the round-122 pattern, so these seven-hour cells spend six hours past
their minimum. Round 132 (w149), the wide control on this graph, completes the square.

Rounds 130 and 131 are read (jobs 2782 and 2783, 3 h 52 min and 3 h 46 min), the two
schedules for the store affine on the deletion-only graph. Round 130 holds the affine at
zero until epoch 40: it reads 0.074 (sd 0.046) against the store arm's 0.121 ($-0.047$
paired, sd 0.053, above on two of six) and against the affine-free control's 0.058
($+0.016$, sd 0.068, four of six), top-50 3.86 against 4.93 and 3.39, and ensembled over
six seeds 0.129 with a top-50 of 5.79 against 0.131 and 5.59. The affine started at
epoch 40 in every seed, and five of the six seeds select their epoch before it (10, 32,
34, 25 and 38; the sixth at 76), so the selected cell is the affine-free trajectory in
five seeds of six: the validation peak the affine-free model reaches before epoch 40 is
not overtaken once the affine arrives. Round 131 releases the affine from epoch 5 once
the trailing validation Pearson reaches 0.15: the release came at epochs 5, 6, 25, 31, 6
and 40, and the seeds select at 2, 57, 35, 32, 78 and 38, four of them after the
release. It reads 0.110 (sd 0.050), $-0.010$ against the store arm (sd 0.052, one of
six) and $+0.052$ against the control (sd 0.035, six of six), top-50 4.66, ensembled
0.120 with a top-50 of 6.19, the highest ensemble call of any betaxanthin arm. The seed
whose release fell at epoch 40 is the same trajectory in both rounds (0.016 at epoch 38
in each), a check that the schedule and not the seed is what separates them. So the
store's gain does not come from the first epochs: the affine released after the model
leaves the constant predictor reads the store arm's level and its best call, and the
affine held to epoch 40 loses the level because the selected epoch comes first. A
release rule on the validation curve costs nothing and keeps the gain; a fixed late
start does not.
