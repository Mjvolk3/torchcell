The recommended set carries roughly $5.8\times10^7$ genotype-by-environment instances and
$9.0\times10^7$ individual measurements. Both are lower bounds: rows whose axes could not
be pinned down contribute nothing to either total, and the instance figures marked
$\dagger$ are products of the two axes rather than counts the papers report.

Two numbers in that total deserve a discount before they are quoted anywhere. Turco 2023
supplies $3.8\times10^7$ of the instances and is a meta-aggregation of screens this table
lists separately, so its net-new contribution is smaller than its row and currently
unmeasured. Lee 2014 supplies $1.3\times10^7$ and is a single primary experiment, so that
one stands.
