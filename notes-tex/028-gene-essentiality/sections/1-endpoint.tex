\subsection{Vocabulary\secstatus{todo}}

\begin{description}
  \item[AUROC] Area under the receiver operating characteristic, computed as the
    Mann-Whitney rank statistic with mid-ranks on ties: the probability that a randomly
    drawn essential gene scores above a randomly drawn nonessential one.
  \item[backbone] The cell graph transformer over the nine-graph perturbed gene graph with
    \texttt{prot\_T5\_all} node features unless another encoding is named, read at the
    pooled perturbed tokens. The \emph{pooled} arm is this backbone with a scalar head and
    no metabolic module.
  \item[chromosome-pathway indicator] The 197-dimensional per-gene encoding
    \texttt{normalized\_chrom\_pathways} (\texttt{sgd\_gene\_graph\_hot.py}): five SGD
    numeric gene features scaled to $[0, 1]$ (protein length, molecular weight,
    isoelectric point, the median and the median absolute deviation of the gene's
    expression), a one-hot of the gene's chromosome, and a multi-hot over the SGD
    pathways it is annotated to. \emph{chrom-pathway} in figure labels; the name
    understates it, since five of its columns are continuous gene properties and two of
    those are expression.
  \item[FBA affine] The readout that adds $w^\top r + b$ to the backbone's logit, where $r$
    holds the deleted gene's knockout-to-wild-type growth ratio on each of four media from
    one FBA solve per gene; the \emph{fba} arm.
  \item[fold ensemble] Five models trained on the five cross-validation files (each a
    different fifth of the 787 labeled non-test genes held for validation), their logits
    averaged on the held-out genes; Flux Cone Learning's own test protocol.
  \item[gene bootstrap] Resampling the scored genes with replacement (2,000 draws) and
    reporting the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles of the statistic.
  \item[held-out genes] The 223 genes Merzbacher 2025 held out of its own training
    (\texttt{test = 1} in the released split), of which 195 resolve as single-deletion
    genotypes in the build. Every model number in this document is on those 195 unless
    the gene set is named.
  \item[layer that solves] The metabolic module with its flux replaced by the growth
    optimum of the linear program under the layer's own bounds, one solve per genotype
    in the forward pass, the bound duals as its gradient. Its \emph{elastic} form lets
    the model's one forced flux (ATP maintenance) go unmet at a price, so the program is
    feasible for every genotype by construction.
  \item[message passing] The update of a node's representation from an aggregate of its
    neighbors' representations, $h_i' = \gamma(h_i, \bigoplus_{j \in \mathcal{N}(i)}
    \phi(h_i, h_j))$; graph convolution (GCN), graph attention (GAT, GATv2), GraphSAGE
    and the graph isomorphism network (GIN) are choices of $\phi$, $\bigoplus$ and
    $\gamma$. \emph{Relational} message passing (RGCN, RGAT) keeps one weight or one
    attention per edge type over a multigraph.
  \item[metabolic module] The enzyme-constrained thermodynamic flux layer over yeast-GEM
    9.0.2 that maps the perturbed representation to a flux vector by $K$ alternating
    projections onto the mass balance $Sv = 0$ and the box of bounds; here it is read at
    the objective column \texttt{r\_2111} (growth). The \emph{anchored} layer tightens the
    GEM's box by the enzyme-capacity ceilings and the second-law directions; the
    \emph{stoichiometry-only} layer keeps the GEM's box.
  \item[parsimonious FBA] Two linear programs: the growth optimum over the polytope
    $\{Sv = 0,\ \ell \le v \le u\}$ with the deleted gene's reactions closed through the
    gene-protein-reaction rules, then the flux of least total absolute value at that
    optimum. \emph{pFBA}; \emph{the solve} when the object is the optimum rather than the
    method.
  \item[released labels] The 1,121 essentiality calls in the released split. The column
    named \texttt{essential} is a viability flag (1.0 marks a nonessential gene); it is
    inverted once and the four class counts are asserted against the paper's Methods.
  \item[selected epoch] The argmax of a centered five-epoch moving average of the
    validation AUROC. The held-out genes are scored once, at that epoch.
  \item[solve-derived encodings] Per-gene vectors computed from pFBA rather than from
    sequence: the deletion's own flux vector, its difference from the wild-type vector,
    and eight statistics of the gene's reactions under the wild-type solve and its flux
    variability (\texttt{ess\_reference\_flux\_table.py}).
  \item[warm start] The flux layer's proposal written as a reference flux plus a
    null-space residual of relative size $\alpha$, $v = v_{\text{ref}} + \alpha N z$,
    before the alternations; with a per-genotype table the reference is the deletion's
    own pFBA flux.
  \item[Weisfeiler-Leman test] Color refinement: every node starts with a color, and
    each iteration replaces it by a hash of its own color and the multiset of its
    neighbors' colors; the multiset of colors after $k$ iterations is the graph's 1-WL
    signature. Different signatures prove two graphs non-isomorphic; equal signatures
    prove nothing. A message-passing network is at most as expressive as the test.
\end{description}

\subsection{Where the labels come from\secstatus{todo}}

The build is a union of the SGD essential-gene list (1,140 genotypes, all essential) and
the single-mutant fitness screens (Costanzo 2016, Kuzmin 2018; 4,540 genotypes), 5,656
single-deletion genotypes in all. No fitness value enters any objective: the head is one
logit scored by binary cross entropy against a 0/1 label, and every genotype whose gene

\subsection{The protocol inherited from 027, and what it got wrong\secstatus{todo}}

The wave-9 protocol pinned the held-out genes and let the data module draw train and
validation at 80/10/10 over the remaining 5,461 records. Of those, 926 carry a label, so
the validation set held 75 labeled genes with about 12 essential, on which a single AUROC
read has a Hanley-McNeil standard error near 0.07; 10 percent of the labeled genes sat in
a random test block nothing reports; and 4,535 unlabeled records were forwarded every epoch
under a False mask. A split file replaces the draw: it names every record's split, is
class-stratified, carries the labels it was stratified on, and the trainer drops every
record it does not name.
