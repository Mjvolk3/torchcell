## 2026.10.22 - The data-level pass condition

Reads 25 records of every source straight from a store, builds the vocabulary as the training script does, prints every source's distinct profiles and checks (1) the two Kuzmin screens of one measurement type annotate identically, (2) Costanzo and Kuzmin interaction rows differ only by the uracil dropout and the temperature, (3) fitness and interaction rows of one screen differ in their classes, (4) the encoder returns equal tokens for equal annotations. First run on the S3Q store on GilaHyper, 2026.10.22: PASS, 147 nodes, depth 4, 375 records over 15 sources, 13,008 encoder parameters at state 8 and token 8. See [[torchcell.data.experiment_ontology]].
