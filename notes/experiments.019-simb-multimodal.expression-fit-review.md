## 2026.09.17 - Ten-agent review: how to improve the expression fit, and what comes after the queued rounds

Ten read-only review agents (Opus, one question each) read every 019 note, the config headers of every round, the weekly logs since July, the memory files, the model and trainer source, and the W&B histories of 175 to 410 runs, and ran CPU diagnostics on the six best-validation prediction dumps written by [[experiments.019-simb-multimodal.scripts.gh_eval_ckpt_predictions]]. The ten reports, with every number's source, are under `experiments/019-simb-multimodal/results/expression_fit_review_2026_09_17/` (agent-01 ledger, 02 curves, 03 architecture, 04 masking, 05 optimization, 06 data, 07 metabolic, 08 inputs, 09 literature, 10 methodology). The queue already decided before the review, and not re-litigated here: 1) v16 joint proteome plus expression heads ([[experiments.019-simb-multimodal.conf.cgt_expr_v16_joint]]), 2) proteome trunk size and weight decay ladder at 500 epochs, 3) reliability-weighted per-protein loss.

Statistic conventions in this note: `roll_max` is the campaign's rule (max of a centered 5-epoch rolling mean of `val/<pheno>/pearson_per_feature`), an upward-biased order statistic; "level" is the rolling mean AT an epoch; "dump" values are one checkpoint scored on the full split. Anything unmeasured is labeled a hypothesis.

### The four questions, answered

**Does masking need a warm-up, and is that why the runs are slow? No.** `mask_schedule` is a static list and `train/mask/n_revealed@k` takes one value per k for the whole run in every run checked; there is no ramp. The conditioned branches are the FAST part: in v13 the validation Pearson at k1 / k2 / k3 peaks at epochs 214 / 606 / 1,099 and then decays, while k0 peaks at about 3,500. The v9 round already holds the dose response: eight arms to 9,999 epochs with the fraction of gradient steps at k0 from 1.00 (`M_off`, no masking) to 0.17 (`M_fine`), and epoch to 90% of each arm's own max does not order by dose (`M_off` 1,809, mid-pack; `M_fine` ends highest at 0.233; one seed per arm). The same objective plateaus at epoch 57 on the proteome and 825 on expression, so what is slow is the label, not the objective. What masking does cost is measured: `M_off` and `M_sched` were co-resident on one card for 9,999 epochs, 222,666 s against 276,815 s, +24%. And the masked objective goes inert: on the TRAINING set the k3 minus k0 gap is +0.245 at epoch 100, +0.034 at 500, +0.002 at 1,000, 0.000 after; on validation the imputation capability peaks at 0.48 at epoch about 1,200 and decays to 0.32 by the k0-selected checkpoint, where revealing 10 true genes buys +0.007 against a ridge oracle's +0.408. No no-mask control exists at the current trunk (166 runs across v10 to v15 all carry `[0, 10, 100, 1000]`). Reports 02, 04.

**Would the metabolic module help? No, not as a layer, and the cheap form has already failed its first gate.** The flux layer has produced no measured gain on any readout (026 betaxanthin: no arm clears the permutation null; 027 amino acids: -0.039, 3 of 3 seeds negative; 028 essentiality: learned layer 0.771 against pooled 0.774, shuffled kcat table 0.779, and the layer adds nothing on top of the offline FBA solve, 0.821 against 0.821). The one gain in that line, +0.047 AUROC from offline FBA growth ratios as an INPUT on essentiality, works because essentiality is the FBA objective. For expression: 98.9% of the essentiality genes are in yeast-GEM against 8.1% of the Kemmeren deletions, so an FBA feature is the wild-type constant for 92% of expression strains; the exact 197-d pathway encoding that carries the 028 gain scores B2 0.053 / B3 0.033 on this task against the random-1024 control's 0.049 / 0.073; and every measured expression-to-metabolite number runs expression to metabolite, none the other way. The target side is real but small: the loud reporters ARE small-molecule metabolism (top decile by sd: OR 3.4, amino-acid biosynthesis 4.4, respiration 4.4; translation and transcription depleted), but they are 8.9% of the metric's terms, so a perfect +0.3 on all of them moves the unweighted metric by about +0.027. Report 07.

**Are genes allowed to interact enough? They interact plenty, but strain-invariantly, and interaction is not the binding constraint.** As configured for v13, 96.4% of the 6.67M parameters are strain-invariant: 5.85M in the gene-embedding preprocessor (3,328 to 1,709 to 90) and 590K in a six-layer encoder that runs at batch 1 on the wild-type graph before the deletion exists (five of six layers unmasked, dense over all 6,608 tokens). The whole genotype-conditional path is one 98K cross-attention block (1.5%); at one deletion (95.4% of the build) its softmax is over a single key, so the deletion is one 90-d vector added identically to every gene token, followed by a LayerNorm that removes the `<h_i, c_b>` inner product. The only post-deletion gene-gene channel is a 32-latent Perceiver; the readout is a 9,919-parameter MLP shared across genes. Every pair-term mechanism in the file (propagation, null sink, Hadamard, response basis, bilinear, FiLM, GEARS row, State row) is OFF in the round that produced the headline. Two measurements say rank and routing are not what is costing points today: the validation prediction matrix has effective rank 4.8 (a rank-5 truncation reproduces the full score, 0.228 against 0.223) while a rank-1 oracle on the same validation matrix scores 0.317; and the model is at chance on the largest signal in every profile, the deleted gene's own value (true mean -2.42, 8.3 sd below its column mean; predicted +0.036; rank 49.5th percentile of the strain's predicted changes). Substituting a constant -1.0 at that one cell per strain, no retraining, moves val 0.2227 to 0.2366 and test 0.1095 to 0.1241. The hop-0 self-indicator that supplies this exists in the code and is disabled; its one prior test (wave 1 `A2_self`) ran with a ReZero gate closed at init, n = 1. The field's answer agrees: only about 3% of TF-bound genes respond to that TF's deletion and regulation "is not propagated appreciably via extended cascades" (Hu et al. 2007, in Hughes and de Boer 2013); GEARS, the one graph-based readout in the Perturb-seq literature, beat no additive or mean baseline in five independent benchmarks. Reports 03, 09.

**Why are the runs slow, then?** Four measured contributions. (a) The readout family: in v12 (matched budget, pinned split, four seeds) the per-gene-output-row arms (`H_state`, `H_pergene`, `H_gears`, `H_pergene_basis64`) reach 90% of their plateau at mean epoch 330 against 860 for the shared-MLP family, with the same ordering in the train fit, so it is optimization speed; at the long v9 budget `R_pergene` peaks at 0.219 at epoch 1,767 while `R_ref` is still climbing at 8,414. The incumbent `V_ref` / `V_concat` is one of the two slowest readouts in the panel. (b) Arithmetic: 1,244 training strains at batch 32 is 39 steps per epoch, so 4,000 epochs is 156k steps; the level at epoch 1,000 is already 89 to 91% of the level at 3,754 (paired +0.014 to +0.020 from 1,000 to 3,754; +0.0045, t 1.6, from 2,000). (c) Optimizer: AdamW at a constant 3e-4, no scheduler, no warmup, decay 1e-8 applied to LayerNorm gains and ReZero gates too, clipping inert (max clip fraction 0.02); the pinball gradient is a sign, so the gradient noise floor never falls (grad norm 0.032 to 0.053 from epoch 100 to 4,000 while train Pearson goes 0.04 to 0.77), and the first ~100 epochs sit at the constant predictor (pred_sd_ratio exceeds 0.05 at a median epoch 100). Score is monotone DECREASING in lr across 289 `_007` runs (Spearman -0.51), lr 3e-3 died at 55 epochs, but every high-lr arm started at full lr with post-LN, so "high lr fails" and "high lr without warmup fails" are unseparated. (d) Mask cost, +24%. Reports 02, 03, 05.

### Corrections to standing claims

Each of these was carried in the working summary or a note and is wrong as stated; the corrected statement and its source follow.

- "Batch 64 collapsed Pearson." Inverted. Batch 32 under the PURE-Pearson objective collapsed 3 of 3 seeds; both batch-64 runs survived 9,899 epochs at 0.194 / 0.186. Four-way confounded (lr unscaled, budgets unmatched, packing differs, n 2 against 3). No batch-size effect is measured for the incumbent pinball objective. Reports 01, 05.
- "v12: per-gene head +0.041 on seed 0." No such number exists. v12 `H_pergene` reads -0.0085 at the matched 1,399 epochs and +0.0319 (4 of 4 positive) at 500; `H_state` +0.0356 at 500 and -0.0007 at 1,399. The per-gene row is a SPEED lever, not a ceiling lever. The nearest real +0.04 is the never-read per-gene replicate stage (cabbi 2389901 + 2392370, tag `round-pergene-rep`, 3 pairs at 999 epochs): +0.0751 / +0.0243 / +0.0167, 3 of 3 positive, on the `calm` input. Pooled with the mechanism round the per-gene row is 5 of 5 positive on `calm` and negative on `E_full`. Reports 01, 02, 03, 10.
- "v11: embedding content +0.068." The +0.068 is v10's ProtT5-against-random effect (t 7.8). v11 (Delta 21948711, 12 runs, 1,399 epochs) was never read out; read now: `E_full` minus `E_calm` +0.0012 and `E_ptt5` minus `E_calm` -0.0288 on the two clean seeds. v12 through v16 pin `E_full` on the strength of that unread round, and the linear embedding study says the four-embedding stack is not better than ProtT5 alone. Report 01.
- "Wave 6 was a 174-epoch liveness check; FiLM was never trained at a resolving budget." All twelve wave-6 runs finished at 4,102 to 4,375 epochs. The pair-rank ladder is flat at n = 1: rank 0 0.2126, rank 9 0.2033, 16 0.2072, 32 0.1954, 64 0.2304, FiLM 0.2055, Hadamard 0.2165; dropout 0.2 -0.025 and 0.3 -0.067 (collapsed), the opposite of the wave-6 recommendation. Report 01.
- "The model is still rising at epoch 4,000." The rise from 1,000 to 3,700 is real (paired within run, +0.0156, sd 0.0164, 18 of 23 positive, sign-test p 0.011, on all four partitions) and small; from 2,000 it is +0.0045 (t 1.6) and the run with the test read is flat at 0.20 from epoch 2,000. Reports 02, 03, 05.
- "The first grid reached per-gene 0.48." That was the TRAIN metric; the project maximum on validation across 496 runs is 0.1184. `experimental-plans.md` still carries it. Report 01.
- "Topology is the channel that matters." The nine-graph attention mask was frozen on speed with accuracy explicitly unmeasured (`_010` header), and the proximity prior it encodes is at chance on all nine graphs (AUC 0.496 to 0.506 against degree-rewired controls); the one directional signal (TFLink TF to target 0.551) is symmetrized away by the mask builder. Reports 01, 03.
- "Split 0 is +0.089 easier." Half right. For the parameter-free B2 baseline over twelve partitions, split 0's VALIDATION draw is +1.72 sd above the mean and its TEST draw -1.38 sd below; B3's split-0 test is the minimum of 24 draws. The pinned partition is the luckiest 155-strain validation draw available. Reports 06, 10.
- "No test number." True until today, and the reason is worth naming: v13 configured `run_test: true` but died on the wall at epoch ~4,080 of 6,000 after 111.5 h, so `trainer.test` never ran, and the offline sync stamped every run `finished`. A `finished` state with epoch below `max_epochs` is a killed run and nothing checks for it. Report 10.
- "H_concat is the readout to adopt." +0.0135 (4 of 4) on split 0 at 1,399 epochs; +0.0056 (t 0.94, 5 of 11) across four partitions in v13; -0.0002 on the proteome. On the first test read the ordering reverses: `V_ref_s0` val 0.223 / test 0.110, `V_concat_s0` val 0.193 / test 0.139 (one pair, strain-bootstrap sd about 0.018). Reports 01, 10.
- The 019 metabolism configs (`gh_metabolism_00*`) never attached a flux layer; they are the readout-head track. Metabolism has never been attached to the expression model in any form. Report 07.

### What the literature says we have not applied (report 09)

No published work predicts the genome-wide Kemmeren profile for held-out strains. The Perturb-seq field's 2024 to 2025 benchmarks (Ahlmann-Eltze et al. 2025, Kernfeld et al. 2025, Csendes et al. 2025, Wong et al. 2025, PerturBench, Systema, the Virtual Cell Challenge) found that mean and linear baselines match deep models and that control-referenced Pearson credits the shared response axis. Our metric already gives the mean baseline exactly 0 and removes that axis, so 0.20 is on the harder side of every axis they found inflated; the honest caveats are that our NMSE above 1 means we lose to the mean on their L2 metric, and that a random 1024-d embedding scores 0.05 to 0.07 under B2/B3, a null we compute and never quote. The one intervention that consistently won in Ahlmann-Eltze was pretraining the perturbation embedding on OTHER perturbation data; our embedding study contains zero perturbation-derived embeddings (SGA interaction profiles, the Messner proteome, morphology, chemogenomics are all in the repo keyed by deleted gene).

### Plan for the rounds after the queued 1 to 3

Design template for every round (report 10, adopted here): one reference plus at most two candidates; four split seeds (0 to 3); three init seeds; 1,200 fixed epochs that the wall can reach; decision statistic the mean of `val/<pheno>/pearson_per_feature` over epochs 1,000 to 1,200 (no max; `roll_max` kept as a descriptive column); paired t on the 12 (split, seed) differences; the closing step is the existing checkpoint-evaluation path scoring every arm's best-val checkpoint on that partition's test split. Adopt an arm only if the paired mean exceeds +0.02 with a Bonferroni-corrected CI excluding 0, at least 3 of 4 partitions positive, and the test-side sign agrees. 36 runs, about 12 card-days at four per card, MDE 0.011 to 0.014, against v13's 28 card-days for 9 pairs. Report a failing arm as "measured, not resolved, MDE 0.013", never as "did not help".

Round 4, hygiene and speed (config only; runs first because it prices everything after it):

- `S_self`: hop-0 self-indicator with the gate forced on (`model.perturbation_propagation.enabled=true hops=0 gate_mode=on`). Measured lower bound +0.014 val / +0.015 test from the constant substitution. Hypothesis (untested): a trained indicator recovers at least that.
- `S_k0`: `multitask.mask_schedule=[0]`, the no-mask control the strand never had at this trunk, scored at matched epochs AND matched wall clock (+24% epochs for the same GPU time). `[0]` isolates the objective from the plumbing (the probe forward, the label encoder's constant offset and the k-sweep validation all stay). Hypothesis (untested): no k0 cost, 24% wall saved.
- Reference: v13 `V_ref` at 1,200 epochs.

Round 5, readout speed and the strain-conditional block (config only):

- `R_state`: `multitask.context_readout=true` (the per-gene row over strain context). Prediction from the existing curves (untested at these splits): reaches its peak by epoch about 250 against about 950 for the reference, at a peak within 0.01. If it replicates, every later round's budget drops from 1,200 to about 600.
- `R_pert`: `model.perturbation_head.dropout=0.0` plus a 50-epoch warmup one-cycle cosine to peak lr 1e-3 (`regression_task.lr_scheduler.type=CosineAnnealingWarmupRestarts first_cycle_steps=1200 warmup_steps=50 max_lr=1e-3 min_lr=1e-6`, both counts in EPOCHS because the scheduler interval is `epoch`). Two hypotheses: at one deletion the attention weight is exactly 1.0, so attention dropout 0.1 deletes the only strain-conditional signal for 10% of gene tokens per step; and every high-lr arm ever run started at full lr with post-LN (1 dead run in 24 in v13, 3 collapses in 16 among the v12 arms that add zero-gated branches).

Round 6, inputs (CPU gate first, then one GPU arm; details in the inputs section below):

- CPU, before any GPU: the retrieval-alignment gate (rho between embedding cosine and deletion-profile correlation over the training pairs) and B2/B3 on four split seeds for perturbation-derived embeddings of the deleted gene (Costanzo SGA interaction profile; Messner proteome profile for the expression task and vice versa; Ohya morphology; chemogenomics). Pre-registered rule: rho above 0.02 (ProtT5 is +0.017) and B2/B3 above ProtT5 on 4 of 4 seeds to earn a GPU arm. Same gate for the FBA descriptor (already failed once as `normalized_chrom_pathways`).
- Free correction: retire `E_full` to `E_calm_ptt5` (v11 read out: -0.002 for the two cis-regulatory windows at 5.5M extra preprocessor parameters), and `model.learnable_embedding.preprocessor.num_layers=1`. Hypothesis (untested): the generalization gap is dominated by an unconstrained multi-million-parameter map from a fixed input fitted at 1,244 points.
- GPU: the directed mask (un-symmetrize `tflink` and `regulatory_interaction`) paired against the mask-off control, the one graph-channel ablation the campaign never ran; then the SGA-profile representation if it passes the gate.

Round 7, target side (after the CPU pre-check):

- Optionally a within-batch per-gene correlation term added to the pinball loss (State's second axis), as a small weight ladder, never a switch: pure Pearson at batch 32 collapsed 3 of 3.

Evaluation changes, no GPU, do before round 4:

If it survives, it replaces the B2 ridge as the baseline every arm is read against, the document's baseline section changes, and the highest-value GPU arm becomes graph adjacency as a node feature (the union adjacency row or a 128-d SVD of it beside the sequence blocks), or a graph-kNN blend of the existing prediction dumps, which is near free.

### Inputs (report 08)

What the model sees per gene is a 3,328-d concatenation of four frozen sequence embeddings (`fudt_upstream` 768, `calm` 768, `prot_T5_all` 1,024, `fudt_downstream` 768) and nothing else; the nine graphs enter as one symmetric hard attention mask on one of six encoder layers (KL regularizer off since `_009`; three of the nine graphs have median degree 0); the deletion is never routed along any graph. The model file's own docstring describes it: a permutation-equivariant set encoder over frozen sequence embeddings with one strain-level 90-d vector added to every token.

- v11 read out (roll_max at 1,395 epochs, three seeds): `E_calm_ptt5` minus `E_ptt5` +0.030 (3 of 3); `E_full` minus `E_calm_ptt5` -0.002 (the two cis-regulatory windows, 1,536 dimensions and 5.5M preprocessor parameters, buy nothing); on the two healthy seeds ProtT5 alone is 0.029 BELOW CaLM alone. The parameter-free study agrees: the incumbent `model_stack` is a worse perturbation representation than ProtT5 alone under B3 on both val (0.101 against 0.120) and test (0.091 against 0.119).
- The graph channel has never been ablated in the expression campaign (every v9 to v15 run carries the nine-head mask; `P_free` and `graphreg_off` were never launched); the KL-vs-mask comparison was n = 1 and inside the noise; the mask was frozen on throughput.
- THE HEADLINE OF THE INPUTS REPORT, a scratchpad measurement that must be promoted to a committed script before it is quoted anywhere else: a parameter-free neighbor average that retrieves training strains by the deleted gene's interaction-graph ADJACENCY ROW matches the trained transformer. On all four v13 partitions with k chosen on validation: union of the nine graphs 0.189 val / 0.201 test, `string12_0_experimental` 0.196 / 0.175, `string12_0_coexpression` 0.188 / 0.181, `physical_interaction` 0.149 / 0.190, against ProtT5 kNN 0.105 / 0.127 and the incumbent CGT's 0.1965 roll_max (n = 8, itself a max over 9,900 epochs). Positive against ProtT5 on 4 of 4 seeds on both splits; against each graph's own degree-preserving rewiring +0.13 to +0.18 (the TF-target graphs +0.02), so it is topology, not degree. The expression-independent graphs (SGD physical, STRING experimental and database) carry it, so it does not rest on the co-expression channel's provenance. The model is handed these nine graphs and consumes them only as a symmetric boolean mask on one of six encoder layers, on a module that never carries the deletion. Consequence: the expression document cannot currently claim the transformer beats its baselines, because the strongest baseline was never run.
- The retrieval oracle: a neighbor average that retrieves training strains by the held-out strain's OWN measured profile scores 0.629 val / 0.646 test across the four v13 partitions, against 0.107 / 0.116 for the same rule on ProtT5 and 0.1965 for the trained model. The gap between the best possible gene representation and the current one is about 0.52 Pearson, and the oracle works as well on the quietest quartile of deletions (0.55) as on the loudest (0.65), where sequence embeddings score 0.01 and 0.13. Measured directly: over 703,230 pairs of training deletions the Spearman between embedding cosine and deletion-profile correlation is +0.017 for ProtT5, +0.014 CaLM, +0.012 chromatin pathways, +0.018 for a train-split co-expression column, against a profile-correlation distribution whose 99th percentile is 0.457. No gene representation we have is aligned with co-response structure.
- A leakage-free train-split co-expression column (the gene's reporter column over the 1,186 training strains) carries real signal as a perturbation representation (B3 0.071 val / 0.075 test against a matched-width random floor of 0.00) but is not complementary to ProtT5 under neighbor averaging (fusion -0.006 val, -0.019 test); it does help a ridge onto the train gene basis (rank-8 reconstruction 0.140 to 0.153 val, one split). The naive GEARS-style co-expression input is a measured null; the reporter-side version (each gene token carrying its own response column) is untested.
- The graphs are not silent on the relation a neighbor readout needs. Over 1,097,421 pairs of single-deletion strains (base rate of profile r above 0.3 is 5.2%), genes joined on `string12_0_cooccurence` have mean profile r +0.157 and 4.8x the base rate (8.5x at r above 0.5); on `string12_0_database`, STRING's curated pathway and complex channel, +0.107 and 3.6x (13x at r above 0.5); physical interaction 1.7x; the two TF-target graphs 0.98x and 0.85x, at chance. So TF-target edges say which reporters move when the TF is deleted (the earlier probe) and nothing about whether two deletions give the same profile; complex and pathway co-membership does the opposite. Those two informative channels are the ones with the lowest coverage (39% and 61% of genes with any edge), so at layer 1 the informative heads are dark for most genes while the at-chance TF heads are dense. Hypothesis (untested): they belong as a retrieval prior on the readout, not as a sparsity mask on one attention layer.
- Only two or three response directions are predictable from any representation: ridge from the deleted gene's embedding to the train-basis coefficients resolves PC1 and PC2 at r 0.3 to 0.45 and nothing beyond PC3, while a perfect rank-2 placement alone would score 0.412.

The input-side arms, in order: (A1, CPU, first) the retrieval-alignment gate, rho between embedding cosine and profile correlation, run on the SGA interaction profile (`dmi_costanzo2016`), the chemical-genomic fitness profiles and GO semantic similarity, against ProtT5's +0.017; nothing below rho 0.02 gets a GPU arm; (A2, CPU) the same oracle on the proteome panel; (B3, a correction, free) retire `E_full` to `E_calm_ptt5`; (B1) stop symmetrizing `tflink` and `regulatory_interaction` in the mask builder with a forward and a reverse head each; (B2) the mask-off control; (C1) the SGA profile as the perturbation representation, gated on A1; (C2) the reporter-side co-expression token; (C3) a SPELL-compendium embedding with Kemmeren and Sameith excluded (a week of engineering); (D2) learn the retrieval metric itself from the 703k labeled training pairs.
