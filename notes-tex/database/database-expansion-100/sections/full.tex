\section{The full ranking to 200\secstatus{todo}}\label{sec:full}

Table~\ref{tab:candidates} is the whole ranking the sixty were cut from, every row the
generating script holds, in rank order with its class, both axes, instance and measurement
counts, phenotype, sequence basis and a clickable link. The first 149 rows are the
recommended set: 51 supported datasets plus 149 reaches 200. The rows past 149 are the
reserve, ranked by the same rule. Table~\ref{tab:sources-all} gives every row's citation,
link, the reason for its position and where the per-record values live, in the same
order; for the first sixty rows that reason is also the \emph{Why} column of
Table~\ref{tab:final}. The ranking is produced by
\texttt{experiments/database/scripts/build\_candidate\_datasets\_table.py} and is also
written to \texttt{experiments/database/results/candidates/candidate\_datasets.json}.
