\section{What the parameter tables are worth\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:parameters}

Six published $k_{cat}$ predictors (DLKcat, UniKP, EITLEM, TurNuP, DeepEnzyme, EZKinetics)
and four $K_M$ predictors were run from hash-pinned mirrors over 95.3 percent of yeast-GEM's
3{,}728 catalytic units, the ceiling set by substrate structure availability. Per gene, the
unit the capacity bound reads (Figure~\ref{fig:agreement}), the median gene moves 1.28
orders of magnitude between the highest and lowest predictor and 71 percent move by more
than one order, against a consensus range across genes of 0.87. On the 57 measured yeast
pairs, EZKinetics leads on rank at Spearman
0.79 and EITLEM on magnitude at 0.23 orders median error, an upper bound: every predictor
trained on the literature those measurements come from.

\textbf{Most of that plausibility is coarse-class knowledge} (Figure~\ref{fig:plausibility}).
Against the EC4 band every predictor beats the EC-median lookup, 56 to 84 percent against
53; at EC1 the lookup already reaches 79 percent, and for $K_M$ no predictor beats the
lookup at all. On the nine unmeasured betaxanthin supply genes, four of six $k_{cat}$
predictors are class-plausible and no $K_M$ predictor is.

\textbf{Where the kinetic values come from.} The resolved $k_{cat}$ and $K_M$ tables cover
all 3{,}728 catalytic units on four tiers, tried in order: 97 units, 2.6 percent, carry a
measurement of yeast's own protein on a substrate the reaction consumes; 782, 21.0 percent,
a measurement of the same EC number and substrate in another organism; 2{,}831, 75.9
percent, the median over the predictors an admission rule keeps, which is EZKinetics, EITLEM
and UniKP for $k_{cat}$ and EZKinetics alone for $K_M$; and 18 the organism default. The
mix is therefore about a quarter measured and three quarters estimated, and the homolog tier
is counted with the estimates wherever an experimental fraction is quoted, because a
measurement of another organism's enzyme is not a measurement of this one. The 18 defaults
are a ceiling rather than a backlog: 14 of the 28 substrate rows behind them carry an
R-group placeholder, 7 are macromolecular species with no formula, and the structure writer
was run on the rest and refused each.

\textbf{Where the thermodynamic values come from.} The table is recomputed with eQuilibrator
component contribution, which returns a covariance the shipped point values cannot, and it
now prices \textbf{3{,}801 of 4{,}131 reactions}, 3{,}535 of the 3{,}847 chemical ones and
\textbf{2{,}724 of the 2{,}845 that can carry flux}. Every value names one of nine routes
and every gap one of seven failure classes (Figure~\ref{fig:routes}). The mix is dominated
by one evidence class: 2{,}494 of the 2{,}559 priced species are estimated by component
contribution, 59 read from a table, 4 fixed by a curation record and 2 gauged from a
measured midpoint potential. \textbf{Coverage is not what the eight recovery routes past the
plain accession lookup bought}; between them they price 153 species. What they bought is a
table in which every value names its own method.

\textbf{The shipped file is in kilocalories and labeled kilojoules}, copied verbatim from
yETFL by yeast-GEM's curation script: regressing our table on yETFL's over 1{,}927 shared
metabolites gives a slope of 4.187 at $r = 0.987$. Correcting that and matching compartment
conditions closes the transport arm of the disagreement, median 17.4 to 5.6 kJ\,mol$^{-1}$,
leaving the single-compartment cloud at 17.6, the genuine estimator difference.

\textbf{Two recovery routes are worth naming because they are error sources as much as
coverage.} Structures written from the metabolite's own name price 347 lipid rows, and 48 of
them need a compound eQuilibrator has never seen, created through a p$K_a$ cascade selected
against the compound cache's own constants on 2{,}100 held-out compounds. That cascade's
ladder error runs to a 90th percentile of 8.33 kJ\,mol$^{-1}$ at the worst compartment pH,
while writing the same structure as its neutral parent rather than its pH 7 major
microspecies moves the answer by a median of 1{,}585 kJ\,mol$^{-1}$, so the reference
convention and not the predictor is what has to be got right. A composition check over the
2{,}414 accession-priced species also found 8 priced as a different molecule, 6 of which are
withdrawn.
