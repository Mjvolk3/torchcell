\section{Decisions the owner holds\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:decisions}

Each item names the choice and the test that settles it. Items 1 to 11 are the open questions
of \dsec{7}, shortened, with where the plan places their test; items 12 to 21 are new.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Heterozygous IUPAC codes: typed gap or the encoder's unknown token.} \emph{Test:}
re-embed the gapped rows through the unknown token and compare the two arms on Caudal and
Muenzner per-gene Pearson, six seeds. Not in the grid; a candidate Aurora stage-1 contrast.
\item \textbf{Soft-masked bases.} \emph{Test:} count and re-embed the windows an uppercase slice
recovers, then the recovered against the strict-gap arm on the isolate statistics. Not in the
grid.
\item \textbf{Route B and the consistency test.} \emph{Test:} C8 against R, six seeds, with the
consistency readout of Eq.~\ref{eq:routecons}.
\item \textbf{Attended-token head or head-only tokens.} \emph{Test:} the attended arm against
head-only on Kemmeren, the doubles and the isolates, six seeds; not implemented, not in the grid.
\item \textbf{Flat-type and six-heads controls.} \emph{Test:} \texttt{exp\_type\_mode: flat} and
six heads against the type path on every dataset; the flat control is a config switch and fits
a later Polaris job.
\item \textbf{Dataset weighting extremes.} \emph{Test:} C2, C3 and C4 against R
(Sec.~\ref{sec:sampler}); under the balanced sampler the weighting contrast is degenerate.
\item \textbf{A clade-disjoint isolate split.} \emph{Test:} a second fold file disjoint by Peter
2018 clade; GBLUP and the stage-1 reference on both, the drop from random to clade split being
the quantity of interest.
\item \textbf{The Addgene 127546 window gate.} \emph{Test:} deposit the plasmid's GenBank file by
the manual recipe, compare its window with the reconstruction base by base, re-embed only on a
difference; until then, whether Vaishnav numbers may be quoted.
\item \textbf{Retiring Sameith 2015 Sm.} \emph{Test:} repeat the identity check on the served
graph's records and record it with the retirement decision.
\item \textbf{The 188 dropped records.} \emph{Test:} re-query with the \texttt{SIZE = 0 OR ANY}
shape and confirm the store gains exactly 188 records; \texttt{all} would then hold them. Hu,
Nadal-Ribelles and Ohya account for 111 of the 188.
\item \textbf{The mark cap.} \emph{Test:} G6 for memory, then C9 against R on the segregant
statistics.
\item \textbf{Background marks.} On or off, how the mating type enters, and whether a
complemented auxotrophy is two marks on one gene or one under the precedence rule
(Sec.~\ref{sec:background}). \emph{Test:} C7 against R, six seeds, per dataset; and, within
Kemmeren, the MATa and MAT$\alpha$ strata read separately, since only a mixed-background dataset
can separate the marks from the dataset token.
\item \textbf{Graph placement.} Mask at one layer, readout gather or none, and whether the soft
edge bias is worth an arm. \emph{Test:} C5 and C6 against R, six seeds, on every dataset.
\item \textbf{Operator residual.} Plain is the main arm by the owner's preference. \emph{Test:}
C1 against R, with the $\zeta$ trajectories; if ReZero wins under the rule, whether it replaces
plain or stays a control is the owner's call.
\item \textbf{The stage-1 reference sampler.} Balanced, records or $\alpha = 0.5$. \emph{Test:} C2
and C4 against R. The winner becomes the reference for any later stage-1 job; stage 2 starts from
the winner's checkpoints.
\item \textbf{Fine-tune learning rate and freezing policy.} Main $\mathrm{lr}_2 = 10^{-4}$, nothing
frozen. \emph{Test:} A2's three rates by three policies, chosen on Kemmeren's validation fold
only, never on the doubles.
\item \textbf{Fine-tune length.} $k = 31{,}710$ for Kemmeren and the segregants, 1{,}100 for the
singles. \emph{Test:} $k = 7{,}550$ (50 Kemmeren epochs) beside 31{,}710 for the main setting,
six seeds, $\Delta^{\mathrm{ft}}_{k}$ at each; one extra short run per seed.
\item \textbf{Which datasets fine-tune second.} The Sameith singles, then the segregant panels,
is the proposed order; the isolates (Caudal, Muenzner) are the alternative second target.
\emph{Test:} A2 reads every target's $\Delta^{\mathrm{ft}}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ on the same stage-1
checkpoints; an isolate target (Caudal and Muenzner) adds its fine-tunes and 24 control runs, about 230 tile-hours at the segregant rate (an estimate).
\item \textbf{A longer stage 1.} Whether 200 stage-1 epochs make a better starting point than 50.
\emph{Test:} A3 against R at matched seeds, read on stage 1 and through the Kemmeren fine-tune;
64 node-hours.
\item \textbf{Hu, Jackson, Nadal-Ribelles in \texttt{all}, and the Boocock de novo cells (P1).}
The three were left out of \texttt{joint\_9} on 2026-10-21; ``all data'' brings them back.
\emph{Test:} C11 (\texttt{joint\_9} at $k_1$ steps) against R on the datasets the two share. The de
novo cells, 79{,}123 records beyond the pooled 393, would make Boocock the largest gene-level
dataset; their arm waits on P1.
\item \textbf{A leave-target-out pretraining control.} \texttt{all} minus Kemmeren in stage 1,
then the Kemmeren fine-tune, separates transfer from the other datasets from a second pass over
the target. \emph{Test:} six seeds of that stage-1 arm and its fine-tunes against R's; about 50
Polaris card-hours, not in the budget.
\end{enumerate}
