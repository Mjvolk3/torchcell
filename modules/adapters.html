torchcell.adapters

BioCypher adapters that turn a built torchcell dataset into knowledge-graph nodes and edges. There is one adapter class per registered dataset (for example SmfCostanzo2016Adapter or BetaxanthinCachera2023Adapter), and most inherit from CellAdapter, which reads the dataset’s LMDB records and yields BioCypherNode and BioCypherEdge objects through get_nodes and get_edges. The knowledge-graph builders in torchcell.knowledge_graphs pair each dataset class with its adapter through dataset_adapter_map.
