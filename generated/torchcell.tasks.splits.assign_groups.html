torchcell.tasks.splits.assign_groups

assign_groups(groups: Sequence[str], seed: int, fractions: tuple[float, float, float] = (0.8, 0.1, 0.1)) → NDArray[str_][source]

Assign every member to a part so that no group is divided.

The distinct groups are sorted, shuffled with seed and dealt in that order: to train until train holds its fraction of the members, then to validation until train plus validation hold theirs, then to test. A group that crosses a boundary stays whole on the earlier side, so a part can exceed its fraction by at most one group.

Parameters:

  • groups – The group of each member, one entry per member.

  • seed – Seed of the shuffle.

  • fractions – Target fractions of members in train, validation and test.

Returns:

The part of each member, aligned with groups.
