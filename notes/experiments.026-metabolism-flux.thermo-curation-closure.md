## 2026.09.11 - Curated records enter the pipeline, and the coverage denominator becomes honest

Fifteen curated thermodynamic records were written for the species that block the last
reactions of yeast-GEM 9.0.2, one per species or per family, each with its sources, its
searches and its decomposition check. This note is what happened when the pipeline read
them, and it is also the record of three pricing rules that landed with them and change
more reactions than the records do.

Code:

- `torchcell/metabolism/thermo_curation.py`: the typed record, the loader, the
  `CuratedResolver` stage and the `CuratedReactionPricer`.
- `torchcell/metabolism/thermo_pipeline.py`: cancellation, the non-chemical rule, the
  reaction-pricer protocol and the elemental balance check.
- `experiments/026-metabolism-flux/scripts/thermo_curation_price.py`: the structure route
  under `equilibrator-ext`.
- `experiments/026-metabolism-flux/scripts/thermo_pipeline_yeast9.py --curation-dir`.
- `experiments/026-metabolism-flux/scripts/thermo_verified_tables.py`: the new versioned
  parquets.
- `experiments/026-metabolism-flux/scripts/thermo_curation_closure_summary.py`: every table
  below.
- `tests/torchcell/metabolism/test_thermo_curation.py`: 40 tests.

### Admission decides what may be trained on

A record carries an admission, and only `measured` and `tabulated` reach the table a model
reads. `computed` and `provisional` records are loaded, validated and reported, and never
priced. The reason to keep them is that "we looked, and what we found is a value we do not
trust enough to train on" is a finding; the reason not to admit them is that a computed
value is indistinguishable from a measured one once it is a float in a parquet. Of the 15
records, 10 are admitted and 5 are not (`thermo_curation_closure.json`).

### Three rules that are chemistry, not relaxations

**Cancellation.** A species on both sides of a reaction with coefficients summing to zero
contributes nothing to the formation sum, so whether the pipeline has a number for it
cannot change the reaction's chemical energy. Twenty-two reactions are priced this way and
none of them existed as a gap: they are transports of a species the pipeline cannot price,
`mannan[c] <=> mannan[er]` and its kind. Identity is (name, formula, charge), which is what
separates `Ferricytochrome b5` from `Ferrocytochrome b5` when both carry the formula `Fe`.
Only groups holding an unpriced member cancel, which is a compatibility guarantee rather
than a chemical one: it is what keeps every already priced reaction's value bit for bit
unchanged. Verified on the yeast-GEM run: 0 of 3,779 previously priced reactions changed
value and 0 of 2,561 metabolite values changed.

**The non-chemical bucket.** An exchange, a demand, a sink and a biomass or pool
pseudoreaction are not elementally balanced and have no standard reaction energy to find.
284 of yeast-GEM's 4,131 reactions are one of these, and counting them in the denominator
made every coverage fraction a different number from the one it claimed to be. The rule is
a config field (cobra's `boundary`, a name token, an SBO term), and yeast-GEM needs all
three tests: its 274 exchanges match none of the name or SBO tests, its carbohydrate and
cofactor pseudoreactions match only the name test, and its biomass pseudoreaction matches
name and SBO.

**A measured decomposition failure outranks a later diagnosis.** The pipeline's last two
stages diagnose a species from its namespace, and they had been overwriting the curated
finding. A curated reason now carries `reason_is_final` and survives. This is what moved
heme a, heme o and siroheme out of `no_structure_available`, which said the work was
retrieval, into `group_decomposition_failed`, which says it is not.

### Findings

**The curated structures exist and do not decompose, which is the opposite of what the
coverage report said before.** heme a, heme o and siroheme all HAVE a tabulated structure,
from ChEBI, with a verbatim quote and a sha256 in each record; component contribution
refuses all three, measured by the curators with
`equilibrator_assets.group_decompose.GroupDecomposer` and recorded in each record's
`decomposition_check`. Twelve species carried `no_structure_available` before this round
and one does now (`thermo_exclusions.json`, two runs of `thermo_pipeline_yeast9.py`). The
work these species need is a decomposition or a measured value, and no amount of retrieval
will produce it.

**The structure route priced none of the eight structures it was offered, and each refusal
has a different remedy.** Run by `thermo_curation_price.py` under `equilibrator-ext`
against `~/.cache/equilibrator/compounds.sqlite`: 3 requests after the measured refusals
are skipped, 0 priced, outcomes `no_formation_energy` 2 and `no_pka_ladder` 1. Nitrophenol
and ethylnitronate resolve to a cache compound whose stored group vector is EMPTY, so
component contribution returns nothing; chitobiosyldiphosphodolichol has no cache compound
and no pKa ladder in a record field, and the compound bridge will not create a molecule
with ionizable centers without one. The chitobiosyl record does carry a pKa set, in the
prose of its `microspecies_note` rather than in a field, so that one is a field away.

**Mannan is the only record that priced anything, and it priced itself.** The repeat unit
carries attachment points and the decomposer refuses it, so the curator priced the residue
as half the difference between a measured dimer and water, and recorded the number per
compartment: s_1107 -248.704 and s_1108 -254.505 kJ/mol with sigma 1.439, at pH 7.2 and
7.1. The resolver checks those conditions against the pipeline's own before using the value
and refuses it on a mismatch, because a transformed formation energy is not portable.

**The carrier route is implemented and closed nothing, for one missing field.** The
cytochrome c record is measured (272 mV vs SHE, n = 1, iso-1-cytochrome c, cyclic
voltammetry) and carries no partner couple potential, and `-nF dE'` is a difference. Seven
reactions (r_0001, r_0002, r_0004, r_0437, r_0438, r_0439, r_4382) are listed with that
reason in `thermo_curation_closure.md`. The cytochrome b5 record does carry a partner
(NAD+/NADH at -320 mV) and is provisional, so it is reported and not priced.

**The sign of a carrier reaction is decided by chemistry or not at all.** Three rules, in
order: the record names which species is oxidized; the declared charges differ, and the
lower charge is the reduced form; the charges are equal and the formulas differ in hydrogen
only, so the extra hydrogen atom is a proton plus an electron and the species with more
hydrogen is reduced. Where none applies the reaction is left unpriced and says so. Rule
three is what would settle ferricytochrome c against ferrocytochrome c, which yeast-GEM
gives the same charge and formulas differing by one H.

**205 chemical reactions do not balance elementally or in charge.** Diagnostic only, no
price depends on it, and the list with the offending metabolites per reaction is in
`reaction_thermo_coverage_final.json`. The pectin record found one of them by hand
(r_0365 is charge imbalanced by -1 as shipped) before the check existed.

**The restated Legendre transform agrees with eQuilibrator's to 1.7e-13 kJ/mol.** Checked
over 1,008 points of pH, ionic strength, temperature, charge and proton count by
`thermo_curation_price.py`, which refuses to write its output if the deviation exceeds
1e-9. The check found a real disagreement first: eQuilibrator uses R = 0.00831 rather than
the CODATA 8.314462618e-3, which is 0.29 kJ/mol on a twelve-proton species at pH 7.8, so
the rounded constant is copied deliberately.

## 2026.09.11 - Round two: the carrier as a pseudo pair, the conserved moiety, and the prices the accession route got wrong

The first round priced from the records and left three things on the table: the carrier
route needed a field no record carried, the porphyrin macrocycle had no route at all, and
nobody had checked whether the accession route's structures are the molecules the GEM
declares. All three are now measured. Same scripts as above, rerun end to end.

### The carrier is a pseudo PAIR, not a reaction-level potential difference

A midpoint potential is a property of a couple, and the two forms have no separate
formation energy, but they do have a definite DIFFERENCE. Fixing the oxidized form at zero
and the reduced form at `-nFE'0` makes the ordinary stoichiometric sum right for every
reaction that CONSERVES the carrier, because the arbitrary gauge cancels there. No partner
couple is needed and no electron chemical potential has to be pinned down: the partner is
priced by its own metabolites like anything else.

Cytochrome c (s_0709 at 0, s_0710 at -26.244 kJ/mol from 272 mV, n = 1) closes r_0001,
r_0002, r_0004, r_0437, r_0438, r_0439 and r_4382, which is 7 reactions from one record and
one field fewer than the round-one route asked for. The oxidized form is identified from
chemistry rather than from a name: at equal charge, the form with one more hydrogen ATOM is
the reduced one, because a hydrogen atom at constant net charge is a proton plus an
electron.

The gauge is arbitrary, so a reaction that MAKES or destroys the carrier must not be priced
from it. `carrier_conservation_violations` refuses those with
`UnpricedReason.CARRIER_NOT_CONSERVED`. Holocytochrome c synthase is the case the rule
exists for; in yeast-GEM r_4152 and r_4192 carry different species ids (s_3748, s_3816) and
never reach the carrier prices at all, so both remain blocked for their own reasons.

**A disagreement the route makes visible.** r_0439, ubiquinol-6 + 2 ferricytochrome c,
comes out at +8.15 kJ/mol. The textbook two-potential estimate for complex III is about
-30 kJ/mol. The gap is not in the route: it is a disagreement of roughly 0.2 V between the
measured cytochrome c potential and eQuilibrator's ubiquinone-6 and ubiquinol-6 formation
energies, and the pseudo pair puts the two on one scale where it can be seen. Hypothesis
(untested): the quinone-6 pair is the side that is off, since its long isoprenoid tail is
group contribution far outside the training set.

### The conserved-moiety route, and what it measured

The porphyrin macrocycle has no group cover. Decomposing every participant in the
decomposer's LENIENT mode leaves it unassigned; where the unassigned atoms match as a
multiset across the two sides they cancel exactly, and the reaction is the group difference
of everything else. Sigma comes from the same component-contribution covariance the bulk
route uses (`L_c`), and the estimability test is the same one:
`norm(L_inf @ x) > 1e-8` means the difference has an infinite-variance component.

Of 12 requested reactions, 1 is priced: r_0530 (heme O monooxygenase) at -440.539 kJ/mol,
standard -396.829 plus a compartment transform of -43.709, sigma 3.169, `sigma_inf`
1.3e-15. The other 11 fail, and each failure is a different fact:

| outcome | reactions | what it means |
|---|---:|---|
| untrusted_structure | 5 | the only structure available is not the GEM's molecule |
| moiety_does_not_cancel | 5 | the two sides leave different atoms unassigned |
| group_in_null_space | 1 | r_4273, the nitro group |

**Protonation is what decides estimability, and it is measurable.** As curated, heme a
carries one propionate as the neutral acid and one as the carboxylate, while heme o carries
both deprotonated. The difference vector then contains `-COO [H1 Z0 Mg0]`, a group that
**zero of the 631 training compounds** carry, and the estimate has infinite variance. Moving
that count to its conjugate base on the group vector, which is the microspecies convention
the rest of the table already uses, takes `sigma_inf` from 1.0 to 6.5e-16 and moves the
heme a minus heme o difference from +201.206 to -117.463 kJ/mol. The route applies that
normalization to any zero-coverage protonated group whose conjugate base IS covered, and
records every count it moved.

The same test names why chitobiosyldiphosphodolichol still cannot be priced. With its pKa
ladder moved out of prose into a field, the compound bridge now CREATES the compound, it
decomposes into 0 unassigned atoms, and one group in it is
`-OPO3-OPO2- [H2 Z0 Mg0]`, again 0 of 631 training compounds. The record itself says the
pH 7 species is the DIANION; writing that microspecies is the one-line remedy.

### The accession route had no composition check, and the shipped table carries the result

`thermo_curation_price.py sweep_accessions` compared every accession-priced metabolite's
structure against the GEM's formula, 2,414 species:

| class | species | what it is |
|---|---:|---|
| match | 2087 | the structure is the GEM's molecule |
| repeat-unit multiple | 12 | every dolichol species: structure at n = 1, GEM formula at n = 0 |
| other | 8 | priced from a different molecule |
| no compound | 307 | no accession resolved to a compound with a definite formula |

The 12 dolichol species all carry the SAME offset, +1 isoprene, so the family is consistent
and the offset cancels in every reaction that conserves the carrier. They are kept, with

the -1127.2 and -1370.9 the shipped table carries, a factor of 4.6.

Withdrawing a wrong price makes the coverage count go DOWN, and that is the point.

### Upstream yeast-GEM defects this round found or confirmed

Each of these is a property of yeast-GEM 9.0.2 rather than of our pipeline, and each one is
visible in the run above.

- **heme a charge -3 is chemically unreachable.** No microspecies of C49H55FeN4O6 carries
  it; the ferrous dianion is C49H54FeN4O6 at -2. Every curated structure for it therefore
  fails a strict charge comparison.
- **r_0530's stoichiometry disagrees with EC 1.17.99.9**, which is worth knowing next to the
  -440.539 kJ/mol this round gives it.
- **pectin s_1309 is charge 0 and should be -1**, which is why r_0365 fails the charge
  balance. The record now states it as a charge-only correction.
- **s_3934 "Ethylnitronate" is nitroethane.** The GEM's own mass balance on r_4273 only
  closes with C2H5NO2 at charge 0, which is the neutral nitroalkane, not the nitronate.
- **s_0332's GPI formula was invented by a solver** (commit 2eb8e907, 2018), and r_0520,
  r_0522 and s_4002 carry the consequences.
- **The dolichol family formulas are the n = 0 core while every accession structure is
  n = 1**, measured above on all 12 species.
- **Glycogen is priced from a four-residue oligomer**, measured above.

**The shipped table every 026 and 027 run has trained on carries the glycogen and dolichol
defects.** `metabolite_dgf_prime_with_external.parquet` is unchanged and still holds
-1127.2 and -1370.9 for glycogen and the n = 1 dolichol energies; the corrected values are
in the verified table only.
