\section{Where the module stands on this target\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:standing}

The amino-acid endpoint sits at its noise floor. Its own strain-level retest is 0.20, the
second screen agrees with it at 0.03, and every model, transformer or ladder, at either
width, with or without a store, reads between 0.00 and 0.06. Four forms of the store
affine sit at or under their control; the solve head recovers the affine's loss and
lands at the control; width, 100 more epochs and the rule counts change nothing. What
the campaign fixes is the target's position rather than the module's: a strain-level
rank with a 0.20 ceiling has no headroom to show an arm effect against. The physically
stated stores read in the transformer confirm it from the other side: the adjustment
store and the driving-force concentrations each sit between the parsimonious store and
the control inside both spreads, so the parsimonious store's small loss is specific to
that build and nothing metabolic rises above the control. The one read above the
transformer band is annotation with sequence through a perceptron, 0.065, and the flux
row lowers every read it joins. The sampled cone (round 143, 0.019) and the polytope
mean (148, 0.032) read under every other store; width 6 (146, 0.025) brings the
collapse back; dropout 0.3 on the store arm (157, 0.045) returns half of what the store
costs and stays under the control. GO in the affine slot (153, 0.058) is the one
transformer arm above its control on five seeds of six, at the GO perceptron's level;
the HiGHS vector appended to it in the same affine takes 0.003 from it on every seed
(160), where on betaxanthin it added 0.009. Cells queued: GO with the HiGHS vector at
dropout 0.3 (round 162) and the GO affine at dropout 0.3 (165).
