\section{The multi-measurement build\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:multi-measurement}

The missing record of Section~\ref{sec:deletion-query} is now a record. Build 030
(\texttt{experiments/030-solid-growth-multi/}, build 001, 2026-09-24) runs the 025 query,
every allele, on the 029 store shape, every source entry kept, and both Kuzmin
double-mutant fitness loaders emit the query strain's own fitness from the released query
strain tables as of commit 4c4a4f950: 172 Kuzmin 2018 and 200 Kuzmin 2020 double-mutant
query strains, each carrying the strain identifier \texttt{GENE1+GENE2\_tmNNNN} that the
triples of that screen carry on their query perturbations. Because each query strain was
screened against hundreds of array genes, those 372 records cover 86{,}111 of the 91{,}111
Kuzmin 2018 triples and 255{,}296 of the 300{,}187 Kuzmin 2020 triples. The store holds
43{,}820{,}352 raw records in 13{,}525{,}088 genotype groups; roles resolve on all
376{,}732 triples.

\notesec{The same identity, once with the record and once without}
Table~\ref{tab:multi-measurement} scores the published form on identical records under
the same Kuzmin-first policy with the triple's own year promoted, with $f_{ij}$ chosen
two ways: by \texttt{LabelPolicy.select\_double}, which prefers the entry whose strain
token equals the triple's query strain and otherwise takes the pair's digenic array
screen, and with the query-strain entries excluded, which is what 029 could do. The
second reading reproduces 029 to three decimals on both screens on the deletion-array
stratum, $0.511$ and $0.325$, so the extra alleles of the 025 query and the larger
store change nothing on that reading, and the two builds are comparable term by term.

With the record, the 2018 screen closes. On the 86{,}111 triples whose query double is
in the build, $r$ moves from $0.435$ to $0.951$, the rmse from $0.102$ to $0.016$, and
the median absolute residual from $0.046$ to $3.6 \times 10^{-5}$: for most of those
triples the build reproduces the released score exactly, and the distance to the
$0.985$ of the raw-table recompute is a tail of residuals rather than a shift, the slope
being $0.98$. Over all 91{,}111 triples, where the 5{,}000 without a query double fall
back to the array screen, $r$ is $0.729$; on the deletion-array stratum of 029, $0.778$
against 029's $0.511$.

The 2020 screen moves but does not close: $0.338$ to $0.660$ on the 255{,}064 covered
triples, the rmse halved to $0.068$, but a median absolute residual of $0.032$ says that
most 2020 triples are still not reproduced exactly, so a term other than the query
double differs from what the source computed with. One candidate, untested: the control
terms $\epsilon_{ik}$, $\epsilon_{jk}$ and $f_k$ are selected from the pair's pooled
entries whatever the array strain, because a double's two perturbations carry two strain
identifiers and the policy matches on the query token only, whereas the raw-table
recompute of Section~\ref{sec:strength} matched the same array strain identifier; the
2020 screen mixes deletion and temperature-sensitive array alleles of one gene and both
years' screens of one pair. Testing it needs the scan to keep both identifiers of a
double and an array-strain match for the control terms.
