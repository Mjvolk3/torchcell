torchcell.models

Model implementations, one module per architecture. They range from sequence language-model wrappers (Nucleotide Transformer, ESM-2, ProtT5, the fungal up/down-stream transformer) through set and graph models (DeepSet, GCN and GAT encoders, DiffPool and SAGPool variants) to the cell models used in the experiments: DCell, the DANGO family (for example GeneInteractionDango in hetero_cell_bipartite_dango_gi), and the Cell Graph Transformer (CellGraphTransformer, with its equivariant variant CellGraphTransformer). The package namespace re-exports a small subset (torchcell.models.__all__); every class below is listed under the module that defines it.
