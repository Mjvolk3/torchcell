The functional dose takes the two values the plan expected and no others
(Table~\ref{tab:genotype}). 1{,}826{,}549 cells sit at zero, a full deletion, and

\notesec{The two arms never share an environment}
Hoepfner is the only source with both arms, and the plan rests the learned scale factor
$\gamma$ on its 648{,}977 pairs. The store confirms the count and adds a condition the plan
did not state (Table~\ref{tab:ploidy}). Matched on the compound alone, 648{,}977 pairs are
measured in both arms. Matched on compound and concentration, 1{,}140{,}517 cells are.
Matched on the environment, none is. Every heterozygous cell ran for 20 generations with
no duration in hours stated, and every homozygous cell ran for 5 generations over 16
hours (Table~\ref{tab:physical}). Duration is part of an environment's identity, so the
two arms are disjoint sets of environments, and in this source ploidy and duration are one
variable.

\notesec{The arms agree weakly}
On the 1{,}140{,}517 cells matched on compound and concentration, the two arms correlate
at Pearson 0.127 and Spearman 0.101. The repeat reliabilities of the arms, 0.576 and
0.639, put the limit for two measurements of the same quantity at 0.607, so the arms share
0.21 of what noise alone would allow. Hits overlap more than the correlation suggests:
8{,}000 of the 57{,}026 homozygous hits are also heterozygous hits, against 2{,}851
expected by chance. Figure~\ref{fig:reliability}c shows the shape behind both numbers. The
mass lies along the two axes, cells that respond in one arm and sit at zero in the other,
with a thin lower-left quadrant where both respond.

\notesec{What this does and does not say about $\gamma$}
The plan's hypothesis is that a heterozygote attenuates the deletion response, which a
scalar $\gamma$ multiplying the operator can express. The measurement bounds that reading
and cannot test it. A pure attenuation would make the two arms proportional and would put
their correlation at the limit, and it sits at a fifth of the limit. Three things other
than ploidy could produce the gap, and the store cannot separate them. The arms differ in
duration, 20 generations against 5. Each screen is normalized to its own pool, so a score
is relative to the other strains of its arm. And the heterozygous pool contains the
essential genes the homozygous pool cannot, which changes what a median strain is.
\textit{Hypothesis (untested):} most of the gap is biology, because a heterozygous
deletion exposes the drug's target by dosage while a homozygous deletion exposes the
pathways that buffer it. If so, $\gamma$ as a scalar is the wrong form and the arm needs
its own interaction with the compound.
