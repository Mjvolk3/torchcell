\section{What these tables cannot support\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:limits}

\subsection{A predicted turnover number is not a measured one\secstatus{todo}}

Every row is a model output, recorded as one in a provenance column. Two predictors trained
on overlapping data, applied to the same 7{,}351 pairs, disagree by a median factor of about
three, which is wider than either model's own reported error and is the honest uncertainty
on any single value.

The accuracy of Section~\ref{sec:accuracy} does not narrow it, being an upper bound under
training leakage. The join also has to be on the \emph{pair}:
joining on enzyme alone compares a prediction for one substrate against a measurement for
another, and doing so moves one of these correlations from 0.98 to 0.82 and another from
0.62 to 0.12. The looser join is the one that is easy to write by accident.

\subsection{$K_M$ is built and deliberately not consumed\secstatus{todo}}

$K_M$ would enter the flux layer only through a saturation factor
$\eta_{\mathrm{sat}} = \prod_i c_i/(K_{M,i} + c_i)$, whose intracellular concentrations are
measured for 191 of 2{,}806 metabolites, 6.8 percent, so adding it today adds a parameter
and a free variable per reaction with no anchor for either.

The measured argument for adding it anyway is promiscuity: underground reactions show
roughly twice the $K_M$ at indistinguishable $k_{cat}$, so a $k_{cat}$-only model cannot
tell a promiscuous side activity from a native one. The measured argument against is that no
$K_M$ predictor beats the EC-median lookup (Section~\ref{sec:accuracy}), so these values are
order-of-magnitude priors, not constants.

Four, in order of how much they bind.

\textbf{Novel compounds needed a licensed tool, and no longer do.} Uni-pKa on the simple
enumeration template supplies the constants to 0.91 kJ\,mol$^{-1}$ through
\texttt{pkas\_provided\_externally} (Section~\ref{sec:pka}). \textbf{What binds is reach
rather than accuracy}: 347 of the 665 unpriced metabolites now carry a written structure and
a price (Section~\ref{sec:lipids}), and 185 of the remaining 318 never can. The 48 values
that came through the external route were written as neutral structures and priced with
MolGpKa constants, so they still carry the 777.5\,kJ\,mol$^{-1}$ protonation-convention
offset until they are resubmitted as their pH 7 major microspecies, which closes it to
0.13\,kJ\,mol$^{-1}$.

\textbf{Coverage is 66.4 percent against the shipped table's 84.9}, up from 42.5 before the
proton convention of Section~\ref{sec:thermo}, and 86.3 with the written lipid structures,
which are at a single pH and not in the built table yet.

\textbf{Proton translocation still carries zero in every arm reported here.}
\texttt{transport\_delta\_g\_source: computed} is off by default, so the 183
proton-translocating reactions get none of the term Section~\ref{sec:transport-term}
computes and checks, and the protonation contribution to $N_H$, worth 613 reactions, is
still unwritten.

\textbf{Compartment pH is assumed, and nothing in the library can settle it.} The values are
textbook organellar figures recorded as \texttt{assumed} in the build output; the two
in-library sources tabulate rather than measure them and disagree by 1.2 units on the
peroxisome (Section~\ref{sec:compartments}).

\subsection{What the mirror records when a predictor cannot run\secstatus{todo}}

All six now run. Where a predictor could not be run at all, the mirror records
\texttt{runnable: false} with the specific blocker, so that a gap in coverage is never
mistaken for a modeling choice. Boost\_KM carried that flag wrongly
(Section~\ref{sec:mirrors}); the real obstacle was that UniRep is a TensorFlow~1 mLSTM stepping one residue at a time on CPU, which on a first attempt
occupied roughly 85 cores, and the ESM-1b variant Wu et al.\ used embeds all 887 enzymes on
one GPU in four minutes.
