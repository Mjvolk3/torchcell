\section{The rounds\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:rounds}

Round 118 (jobs 2743 and 2744, w135) reads the essentiality store's growth vector on
the amino-acid medium into the 19-output affine on the mean channel. Round 121 (w138) is
the amino-acid production store, its 19 ratios and the growth ratio alone, and the whole
vector with all 42 summary statistics. Round 122 (w139) is the affine-free control on
the backbone 020 and 027 trained, width 90, nine attention heads, two blocks, where the
graph regularizer is evaluated. Round 124 (w141) is the deletion-only graph; round 126
(w143) the whole-vector store affine on it, paired against round 124's control on that
graph and against round 118's affine on the old one. Round 129 (w146) runs the
deletion-only control for 300 epochs. Rounds 136 to 143 screen the essentiality
finalists and the physically constrained stores of Section~\ref{sec:physics}, every one
at width 12 on the deletion-only graph over six seeds, paired against round 124's
control and round 126's store: round 136 (w150) the vector with the six rule-count
columns beside it, the essentiality study's best single model; round 137 (w151) the
cached solve head, a hidden-256 perceptron over the store, in place of the affine;
round 138 (w155) the linear minimization-of-metabolic-adjustment store; round 141 (w158)
the max-min driving-force concentration store; round 143 (w160) the mean of six samples
of each deletion's flux cone. Round 146 (w163) reads the size question, width 6 with two
heads against width 12, on the store arm. The stores are written on the prototroph over
the source store's genes and reaction order, with the growth-ratio column unchanged, so
each pair isolates the vector.

Round 118 is read. On the amino acids the whole-vector affine into the 19-output mean
reads 0.025 (sd 0.015) against the control's 0.034 (sd 0.025): $-0.009$ (sd 0.018),
above on two of six, and both sit under every 027 arm on the same split (0.068, 0.061,
0.048 at width 90). Two seeds of each arm stayed near a constant predictor (validation
Pearson 0.01 to 0.02 at selection; one control selected at epoch 2 and scores 0), so the
width-12 backbone that the essentiality endpoint tuned is under-sized or unstable for
19 targets at once, and the width arm of round 122 is the test that has to be read
before any amino-acid affine is. Averaged over seeds the control reads 0.058 and the
store arm 0.035, so the affine costs a little on this target rather than adding to it.

Round 124 is read (job 2761, 3 h 38 min): the amino-acid control on the deletion-only
graph, the M\"ulleder strain without the two alleles and the BTS1 copy it never carried.
It reads 0.049 (sd 0.007) against the old-graph control's 0.034 (sd 0.025), $+0.015$
paired (sd 0.026), above on five of six. The level is modest; the spread is the finding.
Every seed of the corrected graph escapes the constant predictor and reads 0.042 to
0.059, where two seeds of the old graph sat at 0.000 and 0.010, so the collapse round
118 attributed to the width-12 backbone was in part the graph carrying three genes the
strain does not have. Ensembled over six seeds it reads 0.062 against 0.058, under 027's
best at 0.068 and above its context-only cell at 0.061, both at width 90. The
amino-acid affine arms of rounds 118 and 121 are paired against the old graph; the
corrected control is the one any further affine on this target is paired against.

Round 121 is read (jobs 2755 and 2756, 4 h 34 min and 4 h 35 min): the amino-acid
production store, on the old graph. The 19 synthesis ratios with the growth ratio read
0.031 (sd 0.004) against the control's 0.034, $-0.003$ paired (sd 0.025), above on two
of six; the growth vector with all 42 summary columns reads 0.023 (sd 0.009), $-0.011$
paired (sd 0.027), above on two of six. Four of the 20-column arm's seeds were selected
at validation Pearson 0.005 to 0.025 and still read 0.025 to 0.037 on test, the
selection blindness of round 117 on a target where the affine has 19 outputs to fill
from 20 columns. Ensembled over six seeds the two arms read 0.030 and 0.026 against the
control's 0.058, so neither production summary carries rank the control does not, and
the amino-acid affine has now been tried three ways on the old graph (the growth vector,
20 summaries, the vector with 42 summaries) without a gain. Round 126 is the same
whole-vector affine on the corrected graph, against round 124's control.

Round 126 is read (job 2770, 3 h 40 min): the amino-acid whole-vector affine on the
corrected graph, the first amino-acid affine paired on the strain as built. It reads
0.040 (sd 0.005) against the corrected-graph control's 0.049 (sd 0.007), $-0.009$ paired
(sd 0.011), above on one of six, and ensembled 0.043 against 0.062. Against the old-graph
affine of round 118 it reads $+0.015$ (sd 0.014), above on six of six, which is the size
of the graph correction round 124 measured on the control, so the graph and not the
store is what moved. The affine does on this target what it did on betaxanthin (the sibling
document) to the seed spread, sd 0.005 against 0.007, and nothing to the level. Four forms of the
amino-acid store have now been read, the growth vector on either graph and the two
production summaries, against two controls, and none is above its control; the
amino-acid module has no gain to report at width 12, and round 122's wide control is
what says whether width changes that.

The amino-acid half of round 122 is read (jobs 2774 and 2775, 7 h 36 min and 7 h 48
min): the affine-free control on 027's backbone, width 90, on the old graph, the cell
round 118 said had to be read before any amino-acid affine. It reads 0.034 (sd 0.010)
against the width-12 control's 0.034 (sd 0.025), $+0.000$ paired (sd 0.031), above on
three of six, and ensembled over six seeds 0.050 against 0.058, under the corrected-graph
control's 0.062 at width 12 and under every 027 arm at this width (0.068, 0.061, 0.048).
Every wide seed leaves the constant predictor, selected at epochs 10 to 59 with
validation Pearson 0.09 to 0.13, and the validation loss bottoms at epochs 6 to 21 and
rises 0.05 to 0.07 by epoch 199. So width does for the amino acids what the graph
correction did (round 124): it removes the collapsed seeds and tightens the spread (sd
0.010 against 0.025), and it does nothing to the level. The 027 cells of this form read
0.061 to 0.068 over three seeds on the same split; the six seeds here read 0.016 to
0.043, and the difference between the two runs of one architecture is as large as any
arm effect on this target. With the second amino-acid screen agreeing with the first at
0.03 and the first's own retest at 0.20 (Section~\ref{sec:physics}), the
amino-acid endpoint's strain-level rank has no headroom to show an arm effect against,
and the width read closes the amino-acid backbone question rather than opening one.

Round 129 is read (job 2781, 5 h 34 min): the deletion-only amino-acid control run for
300 epochs instead of 200, the check that the 200-epoch budget is not cutting the
amino-acid arms short. It reads 0.049 (sd 0.009) against the 200-epoch control's 0.049
(sd 0.007), $-0.001$ paired (sd 0.003), above on one of six, and ensembled over six
seeds 0.060 against 0.062. Four of the six seeds select the same epoch with the same
score in both runs (148, 88, 75 and 37), since the first 200 epochs of one seed are one
trajectory; the two whose validation peak moved past epoch 200 (to 287 and 217) read
0.036 against 0.042 and 0.055 against 0.054. The validation loss bottoms at epochs 20
to 169 and drifts up by at most 0.014 over the remaining epochs. A hundred more epochs
change nothing on this target, and the 200-epoch budget stands.

Round 136 is read (job 2786, 3 h 40 min): the essentiality study's best single store,
the parsimonious vector with the six rule counts (0.934 there), in the affine slot on
the amino-acid deletion-only graph. It reads 0.033 (sd 0.009) against the affine-free
control's 0.049 ($-0.016$ paired, sd 0.012, above on one of six) and against the plain
store affine's 0.040 ($-0.007$, sd 0.013, two of six), ensembled over six seeds 0.037
against 0.062 and 0.043. The seeds select late (epochs 47 to 186, five of six past
100) on validation Pearson 0.06 to 0.09, so the arm neither collapses nor escapes
further than the control does. The rule counts that separate essential from
non-essential genes carry nothing for the amino-acid rank, and the store affine's small
loss on this target (round 125) is not recovered by them.

Round 137 is read (job 2787, 3 h 38 min): the cached solve head, a hidden layer of 256
on the store vector in place of the zero-initialized affine, on the amino-acid
deletion-only graph. It reads 0.053 (sd 0.008) against the affine-free control's 0.049
($+0.004$ paired, sd 0.011, above on four of six) and against the store affine's 0.040
($+0.013$, sd 0.008, six of six), ensembled over six seeds 0.063 against 0.062 and
0.043. The seeds select at epochs 24 to 192 on validation Pearson 0.07 to 0.10 with the
validation loss bottoming at 25 to 105. So the head recovers on every seed what the
affine lost on this target and lands at the control, the highest single amino-acid arm
of this study (0.033 to 0.049 for the others) and inside the 027 band (0.049 to 0.062).
The store is not harmful on the amino acids when it is read nonlinearly; it is also not
helpful, and the ensemble says the same.

Round 138 is read (job 2793, 3 h 40 min): the linear minimization-of-metabolic-adjustment
store, the nearest feasible flux to the parent's parsimonious flux under each knockout,
in the affine slot on the deletion-only graph. It reads 0.052 (sd 0.006) against the
affine-free control's 0.049 ($+0.003$ paired, sd 0.011, above on four of six) and
against the parsimonious store's 0.040 ($+0.012$, sd 0.008, five of six), ensembled
over six seeds 0.056 against 0.062 and 0.043. The seeds select at epochs 86 to 197 on
validation Pearson 0.07 to 0.09, none collapsed. So a store anchored to one wild-type
vector, which Section~\ref{sec:physics} measures as almost free of the vertex
degeneracy (11 no-effect rows differ from the wild type's against the parsimonious
store's 324), does not carry the parsimonious store's loss on this target: the third
form of the flux vector read here, after the cached solve head (round 137), that
undoes it, and none rises above the control.
