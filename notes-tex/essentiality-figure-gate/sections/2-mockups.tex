\section{The mockups\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:mockups}

Result files are under \file{experiments/028-gene-essentiality/results/}. The proposed
main-text sheet is Figure~\ref{fig:sheet}, two rows of three, drawn from the summary
figure's readers of \file{028-gene-essentiality} so every number is the same committed
file; the two original summary sheets follow it for the panels it leaves out. The
held-out set everywhere is the FCL held-out set: the \gnHeldOut{} genes Merzbacher 2025
held out of its own training that the cell graph scores, \gnEssentialHeldOut{} of them
essential; the statistic is the AUROC on those genes under the fold protocol (five
models trained on five cross-validation files, logits averaged on the held-out genes)
unless the caption says otherwise, and intervals are gene bootstraps. Two learned
families carry the figure, named by what they read. CGT is the cell graph transformer:
attention over the perturbed cell graph, reading ProtT5 and the pathway multi-hot of
every gene and, in the form kept, the metabolic solve (a pFBA solve done offline, its
log(1+|v|) flux vector through a zero-initialized affine on the logit). GCN is two-layer
graph convolution over the union of the nine gene graphs (physical, regulatory, TFLink
and the six STRING channels), reading the GO multi-hot. They differ in both the
message-passing form and the inputs, which is why they misrank different genes and why
their equal-weight average of standardized logits is the best row. A gene's misrank
under a model is the share of the other class scored on the wrong side of it: for an
essential gene, the fraction of nonessential genes scored above it. The two marks after
each panel letter are the panel's section status and figure status, keyed below.

\pfstep{What each panel decides} Panel b is the claim: on the genes the published
method held out, the average of the two families reads above Flux Cone Learning and
above FBA, with the comparators rescored on exactly these genes. Panel c is the
guard against that number being a post hoc choice: the three were fixed before the
repeated splits ran, and the average wins on nine of ten. Panel d is the reach of the
metabolic column: it helps on the metabolic genes and has nothing to say off them, where
the gene graphs alone read \ggengcnDegree{} with nothing trained. Panels e and f are
the open question the figure hands the reader: the essential genes every model still
misses, by name, and the one feature they share across both families, a deletion FBA
calls viable, which is where a model of the cell is still missing something a yeast
needs.

\pfstep{What the sheet leaves to the supplement} The module forms on one backbone and
their cost (Figure~\ref{fig:goal2}a and b), the store's scale (c), the forms by media and
columns (d), the gains on the repeated splits (e and f) and the columns handed to GCN (g).
The families explainer of \file{028-gene-essentiality} is a draw.io composition and enters
as a first panel once it is re-exported into this document's figures. Both summary sheets
below still name CGT "transformer" in their labels; the gate's sheet above is the one
with unified names, and the two will be regenerated from one script before any panel
enters the paper.
