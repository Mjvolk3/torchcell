\section{Chemical similarity across the datasets\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:chemistry}

Exact overlap is two compounds per arm (Section~\ref{sec:axes}). The softer question
is whether Vanacloig's inhibitors have chemical neighbors in Hillenmeyer, and whether
chemical similarity carries any information about response similarity, because that is
the route by which a model could use Hillenmeyer on inhibitors it never dosed
(\file{chemical\_similarity.py}). Both are measured for every encoder: Tanimoto
similarity for the fingerprints, cosine similarity for the dense embeddings.

\notesec{Nearest neighbors}
Under count ECFP4 the median Vanacloig compound's nearest HOM compound is at Tanimoto
0.33, and only three Vanacloig compounds have a HOM neighbor above 0.5: the two exact
matches and cinnamic acid against benzaldehyde at 0.54. Isobutanol's nearest HOM
compound is sorbitol at 0.14, which is no neighbor at all. HET is richer for the
phenolics because it dosed ferulic acid: ferulamide (0.77), caffeic acid (0.66),
vanillin (0.62), vanillic acid (0.59), sinapic acid (0.56), acetovanillone (0.52) and
p-coumaric acid (0.51) all have ferulic acid as their nearest HET compound, and
4-hydroxybenzoic acid has 4-aminobenzoic acid at 0.64. The cosine nearest-neighbor
similarities of the dense encoders (0.8 to 0.95) are not comparable to Tanimoto: cosine
between mean-pooled transformer states is high for any two molecules, so only the
fingerprint numbers say how close the chemistry is.

\notesec{Chemistry against response}
Over the 4{,}674 Vanacloig-by-HOM condition pairs, chemical similarity and response
similarity correlate at Spearman 0.08 to 0.09 for count and bit ECFP4, MoLFormer-XL and
Mol2Vec ($p < 10^{-7}$), at 0.05 to 0.06 for the ChemBERTa-2 heads, and not at all for
MolE, the RDKit descriptors and the ZINC RoBERTa (Table~\ref{tab:chemsim},
Figure~\ref{fig:chemsim}). The effect is real and it is small: the top decile of pairs
by chemistry has a median response Spearman of 0.011 to 0.022 against 0.011 for the
rest. Against HET every correlation is below 0.04. Figure~\ref{fig:chemsim} shows why
the number is small: under ECFP4 almost every pair sits at a Tanimoto below 0.25, where
the response similarity is centered on zero with a spread of $\pm 0.1$, and the few pairs
above 0.5 do not stand out from it.

\notesec{What this says for transfer}
A model that represents compounds with ECFP4 or a SMILES transformer gets a weak prior
from Hillenmeyer about which genes an unseen inhibitor will hurt, weak because
chemically close pairs are barely more response-similar than distant ones. That is the
expected outcome for a dataset whose compounds are drugs and metabolic poisons chosen
for coverage of mechanism, against a panel of small hydrolysate molecules: the two
compound sets occupy different regions of chemical space, and the pairs that would test
the prior at high similarity do not exist. For the phenolic inhibitors the HET arm
offers ferulic acid as a neighbor at Tanimoto 0.5 to 0.77, and ferulic acid itself did
not transfer at the doses used (Section~\ref{sec:structure}). For isobutanol there is
no neighbor in either arm. The transfer test of Section~\ref{sec:next} therefore has a
prior of a small positive effect for the fingerprint and transformer encoders and no
effect for MolE, and it should be read against that prior rather than against the hope
that chemistry alone bridges the two datasets.
