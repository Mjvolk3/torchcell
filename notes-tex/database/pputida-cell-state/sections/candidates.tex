\section{The fifteen rows\secstatus{todo}}\label{sec:candidates}

Table~\ref{tab:pcsrows} is the row table and Table~\ref{tab:pcscounts} its totals. Two
distinctions decide whether a row answers a given question, and neither is a stored
field, so both are stated here rather than read off the table.

\notesec{Catabolism is not tolerance} In the transposon compendia the alcohol arms are
carbon-source perturbations: the alcohol is the sole carbon source at 10 mM, and the
fitness value says whether disrupting a gene impairs growth \emph{on} that alcohol. In
Lim 2022 and Lim 2025 the alcohol is a stressor added to a glucose medium, and the
readout says whether the strain tolerates it. A query that filters on the compound
alone returns both and they answer different questions.

\notesec{Scale is not information} Almost every record in
Table~\ref{tab:pcscounts} is in the two pooled compendia, which that table reports as
a share, because a pooled screen multiplies one condition by several thousand genes. The engineering rows at the bottom of the table hold single
digits of records and carry the phenotype the strain-design work actually targets. The
record count is a cost estimate, not a ranking.

\notesec{The environment axis is the narrow one} Fifteen datasets, and the distinct
environment counts in Table~\ref{tab:pcsenv} are dominated by the two compendia's
carbon and nitrogen source panels. Outside them the condition axis is one or two
environments per dataset, so almost every row is a genotype series measured in a single
condition.
