\section{The decision\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:decision}

Figure 3 covers single-gene deletions read out in three modalities: the transcriptome
(Kemmeren 2014, 1,484 deletions), the proteome (Messner 2023, 4,476) and morphology (Ohya
2005, CalMorph, 4,718). Two framings are open and they send the next compute to different
places. Framing A is a conditioning figure: one modality is measured and another is
predicted from it, which is an imputation claim and needs the conditioning measurement for
every strain the model is applied to (\cref{fig:mockup-a}). Framing B is a genotype-only
figure: what the deletion alone predicts against each replicate ceiling and against linear
and nearest-neighbor baselines (\cref{fig:mockup-b}). The choice is A, B, or neither;
\cref{fig:mockup-si-1,fig:mockup-si-2} hold the supporting panels, none of which repeats a
main panel, and
\cref{tab:remaining} lists what each panel lacks. The joint-training round v19 is a null on
both registered tests. The conditioned round v20 is in flight at 2 of 12 split seeds and
about 160 of 1,200 epochs, so framing A currently rests on the ridge triangle and on a
partial run. On file, the best nearest-neighbor baseline is within $0.01$ of the best model
score on expression and above it on the proteome and on morphology, each a maximum over
keys selected on validation (\cref{fig:mockup-a}b).
