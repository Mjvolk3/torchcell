\section{The decision\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:decision}

Gene essentiality is to be its own main-text figure, separated from fitness and
interactions, because the study behind it is closed on its two goals and its figure can
carry a claim the others cannot yet. Every model number here is on the \gnHeldOut{} genes
of the \gnReleasedHeldOut{} that Merzbacher 2025 held out of its own training and that
resolve as single-deletion genotypes in the build, \gnEssentialHeldOut{} of them
essential, with the epoch chosen on validation folds that never contain them. Nothing here
is approved. The sheets are the summary figures of \file{028-gene-essentiality}, and the
symbol after each panel letter says only whether the values behind it are on file.

\pfstep{Where the full record is} \file{028-gene-essentiality} holds the study: the
endpoint, its labels and splits; the comparators and how Flux Cone Learning's published
numbers were reproduced (five-fold mean \gfclPublishedCv{} published, \gfclRecomputedCv{}
recomputed); the backbone rounds; every form of the metabolic module and why the layer
was retired; the two model families and their average; the hard genes; and the summary
on one page. This document takes its result files and asks what a figure can claim.

\pfstep{Three statements are on file} First, the held-out score. The best method is an
equal-weight average of two families, \gbestAverageNames{}, at \gbestAverage{} AUROC
[\gbestAverageLo{}, \gbestAverageHi{}] on the \gnHeldOut{} genes; the best single model
reads \gbestSingle{} [\gbestSingleLo{}, \gbestSingleHi{}], against \gfclAuroc{} for Flux
Cone Learning and \gfbaAuroc{} [\gfbaAurocLo{}, \gfbaAurocHi{}] for FBA on the same
genes. On \gnResplits{} other held-out fifths of the labeled genes, with the average fixed
in advance, it beats the better single model on
\gresplitDiffaverageMinusthebettersinglemodelUp{} of
\gresplitDiffaverageMinusthebettersinglemodelOf{}
(\gresplitDiffaverageMinusthebettersinglemodel{}). Second, the module form that carries.
A single-medium parsimonious solve per genotype, run once offline and read as an affine
on the logit beside six gene-rule counts, lifts the same cell with no metabolism by
\ggainOneMediumOverNone{} on the \gnResplits{} repeated splits, higher on
\ggainOneMediumOverNoneUp{} of \ggainOneMediumOverNoneOf{} splits and on
\ggainOneMediumOverNonePairsUp{} of \ggainOneMediumOverNonePairs{} split-and-seed pairs,
and it matches the four-media form (\goneMinusFour{}, \goneMinusFourUp{} of
\goneMinusFourOf{} splits). Every form that kept a linear program inside the training
loop scored lower, so the layer is retired and the solve is kept as data. Third, the
limit. Off the metabolic genes every deletion reads one constant row, and on the
\ggenomeN{} genome-wide genes that carry no GEM reaction (\ggenomeEssential{} essential)
CGT with the solve reads \ggenlayerFree{} [\ggenlayerFreeLo{},
\ggenlayerFreeHi{}] where GCN on ProtT5 over the union of nine gene graphs
reads \ggengcnProt{} [\ggengcnProtLo{}, \ggengcnProtHi{}].

\pfstep{The regime question, settled by naming it} The highest single cell in the
study is transductive: the one-hop propagation of the training and validation genes'
calls through the gene graphs. It is not on the proposed main-text sheet, because its
number holds only inside a called screen. Every bar proposed for the main text is
inductive, reading the gene and the metabolic model and nothing measured on the screen's
other genes, and the sheet's key says so. \regimekey{}

\pfstep{The claims the main-text figure would make} First, that on the released held-out
genes the method beats the published Flux Cone Learning and FBA by a margin the gene
bootstrap separates: on file. Second, that the metabolic model enters best as data, one
offline solve per genotype and medium read through an affine, and that this form carries
across the two model families: on file. Third, that the metabolic module covers only
the genes with a reaction and the gene graphs cover the rest, so a model over the whole
database needs both inputs: on file. Fourth, that the hard genes share an annotation
(isozyme redundancy): on file in \file{028-gene-essentiality}, not yet read into this
document's numbers. The figure enters the paper when the composition of
\ref{sec:mockups} is drawn at Nature size from the same result files and the author
approves each claim's sentence.
