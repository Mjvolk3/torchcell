\section{What is established and what remains\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:remaining}

\pfstep{Established} The held-out score against two comparators on the same genes, with
the margin held on \gnResplits{} repeated splits; the module form (an offline solve read
as data through an affine) and that it carries across both families and inside one
medium; the genome-wide limit of the metabolic columns and the gene graphs' coverage of
the rest; the hard genes and their shared annotation (in \file{028-gene-essentiality}).

\pfstep{Not established} The main-text composition at Nature size from the same result
files (R1). Seeds and intervals on every bar of that composition drawn from one script,
not from the two summary sheets (R1). Whether the best average holds on a split that is
not the released one when the choice of average is made on the repeated splits
themselves rather than fixed in advance (R2). The link to the solid-growth model: the
030 holdout arms serve 698 essentiality singles as a second validation loader, and no
AUROC from them is on file (R3). Whether the essentiality call carries to a second host
with a genome-scale model and no screen, which is the generality claim the figure cannot
yet make (R4).

\pfstep{Corrections recorded} The 028 summary quotes the best single model at 0.934; the
fold-ensemble file this document reads gives \gbestSingle{} for \gbestSingleName{}, and
the 0.934 is the one-seed fold ensemble of the four-media form in the simplification
ladder (\gladfourSolveCountsEns{}). Both are on file; the figure uses the fold-ensemble
table, and the caption says which.
