\section{The metabolic module: three ways to read essentiality off it\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:module}

The pooled backbone reads its logit from an MLP on the perturbed context
(Figure~\ref{fig:readout-pooled}). A flux arm builds the enzyme-constrained thermodynamic
flux layer on the same context and exposes one physically named quantity, the flux through
the growth reaction \texttt{r\_2111}. Three readouts put that quantity to different uses,
and they differ in what the backbone's gradient has to pass through.

\begin{description}
  \item[biomass (forced)] $\ell = a\,v_{\text{growth}} + b$. The growth column is the
    only path from the context to the logit, so the module carries the whole prediction or
    the cell scores chance (Figure~\ref{fig:readout-biomass}). This is FBA's own reading of
    essentiality with a learned threshold, and it is the wave-9 design.
  \item[context + growth] $\ell = \mathrm{MLP}(h) + a\,v_{\text{growth}} + b$. Both paths
    from the first step; the per-module gradient norms say how much of each update the
    module carries when it is not forced to carry all of it.
  \item[gated] $\ell = \mathrm{MLP}(h) + g\,(a\,v_{\text{growth}} + b)$ with $g_0 = 0$
    (Figure~\ref{fig:readout-gated}). At initialization the cell is the pooled model
    exactly; the gate's trajectory, logged every epoch, says whether the optimizer chose
    the flux path, and the \texttt{flux\_layer} gradient line says whether gradient reached
    the layer, which at $g = 0$ it cannot.
\end{description}

  MLP from the perturbed context to a logit, binary cross entropy against the released
  label. No stoichiometry, no kinetic parameters, no flux. Rendered from

\tcfigfit[1.0]{figures/readout_2.pdf}{\textbf{The forced biomass readout.} The context sets

\subsection{Round 2: the module on the stable backbone, with and without the penalties\secstatus{todo}}

Round 2 (jobs 2182 to 2208) puts the three readouts on the width-12 cell of
Section~\ref{sec:backbone}, on the released-label split, at three seeds each: the forced
and gated readouts at constraint scale 0.1 and 0, the context readout at both scales, the
forced readout at scale 0 with the $k_{cat}$ vector permuted across catalytic units (same
architecture, no unit-to-value correspondence), and the stoichiometry-only layer
(\texttt{flux\_off}: no thermodynamics, no capacity) with the forced and context readouts.
Every flux arm carries the wave-9 flux configuration otherwise: the predicted $k_{cat}$
tier, the union-external thermodynamic source, twenty warmup epochs, the dataset medium,
the mixture concentration space. The round's table and figure enter here when its readout
runs.

The reads are fixed before the numbers: \emph{not hurt} is the gated arm against the
pooled cell paired by seed, with the gate's trajectory beside it; \emph{help} is the forced
arm above the pooled cell on three of three seeds and above its shuffled-$k_{cat}$ control;
the \emph{collapse's cause} is the forced arm at scale 0.1 against scale 0, since on the
betaxanthin targets the penalty scale was the dose that starved the layer's input.
