<CardBlurb>

Each record is one *Escherichia coli* K-12 double mutant, made by conjugation between a
donor strain carrying one mutation and an arrayed recipient strain carrying another
(eSGA). The colony size of the double mutant was scored against the expectation from
its two single mutants. The dataset holds the 42,705 pairs the publication released,
those that passed its significance cut.

</CardBlurb>

<CardNotation>

$$
g = \{p_i, p_j\}, \quad i, j \in \mathcal{G}
\qquad\qquad
(g, e) \mapsto y \in \mathbb{R}
$$

The genotype holds two perturbations: $p_i$ from the donor and $p_j$ from the
recipient. Most are deletions; the perturbation of an essential gene is a hypomorphic
allele. The phenotype $y$ is the interaction score (S-score) of the pair, one number
per record, so $T = 1$. It is negative when the double mutant grows worse than
expected and positive when it grows better.

</CardNotation>
