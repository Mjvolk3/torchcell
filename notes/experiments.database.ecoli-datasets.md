## 2026.10.21 - The E. coli datasets document and the gene-encoding benchmark short list

Paired typeset document: `notes-tex/database/ecoli-datasets/` (`make` there builds
`ecoli-datasets.pdf`). It holds the table of every served E. coli dataset, read from the
pre-build snapshot that `build_bacteria_datasets_table.py --organisms ecoli` writes
([[experiments.database.scripts.build_bacteria_datasets_table]]; the two-host view of the same
probe is [[experiments.database.bacteria-datasets-table]]), and the short list of datasets that
can benchmark a gene-sequence encoding such as GenSLM ([[torchcell.datasets.genslm]],
[[torchcell.models.genslm]]). The tables and every number in the prose are generated by
[[experiments.database.scripts.build_ecoli_genslm_benchmark_table]]; the pick list is that
script's `PICKS` registry.

**The question.** A gene-sequence encoding is one vector per coding sequence and nothing else.
Benchmarking it means holding out genes, fitting a small head on the vectors of the rest, and
scoring the held-out genes against two same-width controls: a one-hot or random vector (gene
identity only) and a codon-frequency vector (the one sequence feature a codon model is sure to
have learned).

**The rule.** The label must sit on one gene or one gene pair (sequence-only fit *best*, or
*good* only through a gene x condition cross); enough genes must carry a label for a
held-out-gene split; one primary per task family. Guide-level CRISPRi stores are excluded
because the label depends on the 20-nt protospacer and knockdown strength, not on the gene's
coding sequence; whole-strain readouts (titers, engineered-strain growth, wild-type omics
profiles) have no single gene to attach to.

**The core four, one per task family:** Goodall 2018 essentiality (binary call per gene),
Nichols 2011 chemical genomics (S-score per deletion and condition, the widest condition panel
on one gene set), Schmidt 2016 proteome (absolute copies per cell of the gene's own product,
across growth conditions), Babu 2014 gene interaction (a signed score per deletion pair, the one
pairwise label at scale). Second tier: Li 2014 synthesis rates, PRECISE-1K expression, Campos
2018 morphology, Wang 2018 gene-level CRISPRi fitness. The measured columns are in the generated
block below.

**Caveats a score does not remove.** Gene identifiers are the released ones (b-numbers, BW25113
locus tags, ECK accessions, symbols) and resolve to the pinned assembly's coding sequences only
at modeling time, so coverage is measured before any score is read; paralogs leak a held-out
gene's sequence into the training fold under a random split; GenSLM's BV-BRC pretraining corpus
is expected (unverified per gene) to contain the K-12 genes being scored, which is what the
one-hot control is for. No encoding has been scored on any of these rows yet; every expectation
is labeled as one.
