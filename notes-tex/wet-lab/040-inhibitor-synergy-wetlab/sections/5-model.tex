\subsection{The bars are model-free and they are already on file\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:bars}

The wet-lab data are a clean validation target, and the reference rules set the level a
trained model has to reach before it has said anything (Table~\ref{tab:bars}). Growth
over the 63 combinations is separated by Loewe additivity at AUROC 0.958, with one false
positive, and the ordering of the 21 grown combinations is recovered by Bliss from the
observed singles at Spearman 0.573. Both come from the single-agent curves alone, so a
model that reaches them has matched something that needs no training data beyond the six
titrations in the same run.

What the chemogenomic representation adds to that is, so far, nothing measurable.
Compound-cold ridge profiles reach the published bar on the gene-level task
(Section~\ref{sec:claim4}) and still carry only the generic stress component; profile
similarity does not anticipate which pairs deviate once a measured profile is in the
matrix (Section~\ref{sec:claim2}); and the one composition rule that is measurable is
measurable on public data for compounds that are not these
(Section~\ref{sec:claim3}). The honest preliminary claim is the model-free one, which is
also why the run order put these four claims before any training.

\subsection{Round 1, which has not run\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:round1}

The adaptation keeps experiment 035 round 11's recipe, the best on record: a compound
token in front of the 6,607 gene tokens, one further layer, a one-layer encoder, prior
weight zero, 50 epochs keeping the last, fit on the non-test pool and stacked with nested
ridge. Three things change, each an ablation: a set of compound tokens, one per inhibitor
in the medium, in place of the single token; a log-molar dose scalar modulating each
token, with the public IC30 doses and dose zero as the anchors; and a host-growth head
read from the mean of the cell-in-medium state and the compound tokens after they read
the genome, the empty-genotype path, fit on the ex21 curves and scored on the 63
combinations and the isoboles against the references of Section~\ref{sec:claim1}. The
gene-level head stays on the public task so the compound representation is shared.

Hypothesis (untested), and it is the only statement this section can make: the trainer is
being written, no run exists, and nothing above comes from a model. On the record from
experiment 035 the transformer encoder ties nested ridge on the gene-level task, and the
compound-cold profiles of Section~\ref{sec:claim4} are generic, so the expectation is
that the encoder's profiles behave the same way on claim 2 and that the host head has to
earn its place from the dose anchors and the ex21 curves rather than from the profile
representation. Neither half of that has been measured. The next number this document
can carry is a growth AUROC and a fitness Spearman from a finished run, set beside the
0.958 and the 0.573 of Table~\ref{tab:bars}.
