\newcommand{\regimekey}{Regime is what a method reads when it scores a held-out gene:
\emph{inductive}, the gene's own sequence, annotation, graph position and the metabolic
model's solve for its deletion, none of which needs the screen; \emph{transductive}, a
measured call or phenotype of other genes in the same screen (the label-propagation
store), which holds only inside a called screen; \emph{comparator}, a published method
scored on the same genes with nothing learned here.}
