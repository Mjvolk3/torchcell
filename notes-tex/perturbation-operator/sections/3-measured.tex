At a real budget the sigmoid gate alone showed no gain in its one run. That is weak
evidence, and it agrees with \cref{sec:capacity}: nine dials are a small change. Not yet
run: the gate together with a rank-$d$ product (\cref{eq:both} in full), either with the
flag, and any of these on more than one seed or split.
