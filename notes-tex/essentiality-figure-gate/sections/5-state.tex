\section{The decision rules, fixed now\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:state}

\pfstep{What a panel needs to enter the paper} Every drawn value in a committed result
file (\fstat{file}); every learned bar a fold ensemble or a seed mean with its interval
or spread drawn; the regime of every bar named in the key; the comparators on the same
genes; and the claim the panel carries written in one sentence in this document before
the panel is composed.

\pfstep{The claims and their rules} The score claim stands if the best average's gene
bootstrap interval excludes the Flux Cone Learning point and the average beats the
better single model on at least eight of the \gnResplits{} repeated splits; on file it is
\gresplitDiffaverageMinusthebettersinglemodelUp{} of
\gresplitDiffaverageMinusthebettersinglemodelOf{}. The module claim stands if the
single-medium form beats no metabolism on at least eight of \gnResplits{} splits and
matches the four-media form within 0.01; on file \ggainOneMediumOverNoneUp{} of
\ggainOneMediumOverNoneOf{} and \goneMinusFour{}. The coverage claim stands as a
statement of the data (which genes carry a reaction) and needs no rule. The generality
claim is not made.

\pfstep{Order of work} R1 first, since it is composition from files that exist. R3 is one
readout script over 030 runs that are queued already. R2 is CPU. R4 is a campaign and
is not this figure's.
