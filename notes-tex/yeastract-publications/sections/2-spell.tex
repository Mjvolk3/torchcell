\section{Overlap with the SPELL archive\secstatus{todo}}\label{sec:spell}

The SPELL archive distributed by the \emph{Saccharomyces} Genome Database holds
\ytNSpellStudies{} expression studies, one PMID each. \ytNSpellPapers{} of the \ytNPapers{}
YEASTRACT+ papers have a PMID in that archive. They carry \ytNSpellRows{} rows,
\ytPctSpellRows{} percent of the file, and \ytNSpellIndirectRows{} of those rows
are labeled \texttt{Indirect} against \ytNSpellDirectRows{} labeled
\texttt{Direct}. For these papers the archive holds an expression matrix from
the same publication. \ytNSpellNoGeo{} of the \ytNSpellPapers{} SPELL studies
name no GEO series.

A PMID match misses a paper that analyzes another paper's measurements, because
the file credits the analysis. One such paper has been identified. Reimand 2010
(PMID 20385592) carries \ytNReanalysisRows{} rows, \ytPctReanalysisRows{}
percent of the file, and its abstract states the source: ``Recently, Hu and
colleagues published a comprehensive study covering 269 TF knockout mutants for
the yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae. However, the information that can be
extracted from this valuable dataset is limited by the method employed to
process the microarray data. Here, we present a reanalysis of the original data
using improved statistical techniques freely available from the BioConductor
project.'' The SPELL study Hu 2007 (PMID 17417638, \ytReanalysisSourceGeo{}) has
\ytReanalysisSourceConditions{} conditions.
Table~\ref{tab:all} marks the Reimand 2010 row \emph{via Hu 2007}.

The remaining \ytNAbsentPapers{} papers were not read for the origin of their
data, so other reanalyses may be among them. They carry \ytNAbsentRows{} rows,
\ytPctAbsentRows{} percent of the file.
