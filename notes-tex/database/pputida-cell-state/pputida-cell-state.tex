\noindent
KG 4.0 serves 32 \org{P. putida} datasets. Fifteen of them carry an alcohol growth or
tolerance arm, or a perturbation of cell state, and Table~\ref{tab:pcsrows} gives each
one's genotype, environment, phenotype and genome as the served store holds them, with
the environment axis broken out in Table~\ref{tab:pcsenv}. Every count in both tables
is read off the store rather than off a loader docstring (Sec.~\ref{sec:method}).

Two findings come before the table. First, no served \org{P. putida} dataset measures
isobutanol. A search of every loader module under \file{torchcell/datasets/pputida/}
returns no match, and the two isobutanol datasets in the release are Lopez 2024, which
is \org{S. cerevisiae}
(Sec.~\ref{sec:isobutanol}). Second, the alcohol arms that do exist split into two
kinds that a query must not merge: the transposon compendia feed an alcohol as the sole
carbon source, which measures catabolism, while Lim 2022 and Lim 2025 apply one as a
stressor (Sec.~\ref{sec:candidates}).

Four of the fifteen rows are transposon screens, and they are the largest by three
orders of magnitude. Sec.~\ref{sec:transposon} sets out what a transposon record means
here, since the genotype is deliberately a gene-level aggregate rather than a clone and
the phenotype deliberately avoids the fitness class.

Sec.~\ref{sec:isoprenol} turns to the task these rows were surveyed for: predicting the
final design-build-test-learn round of the Carruthers isoprenol campaign from its
earlier rounds. It names the one non-Carruthers dataset that transfers, and it records
a blocker, which is that the cycle label is absent from every served Carruthers record.
Sec.~\ref{sec:gaps} closes with what the schema cannot yet hold.
