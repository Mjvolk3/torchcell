<CardBlurb>

Each record is one strain of the Keio collection, *Escherichia coli* K-12 BW25113 with
one gene deleted. The strain was grown in one medium in a plate reader, and its growth
curve was fit for the maximal growth rate. The dataset holds 3,664 records.

</CardBlurb>

<CardNotation>

$$
g = \{\Delta i\}, \quad i \in \mathcal{G}
\qquad\qquad
(g, e) \mapsto y \in \mathbb{R}
$$

The genotype holds one deletion $\Delta i$. The environment $e$ is the same for every
record. The phenotype $y$ is the maximal growth rate of the strain relative to the
parent strain, one number per record, so $T = 1$.

</CardNotation>

<CardDetails>

Source: Campos et al. 2018, *Mol Syst Biol* 14:e7573,
[doi:10.15252/msb.20177573](https://doi.org/10.15252/msb.20177573).

| Detail | Value | Read from |

| Perturbation type | Single-gene deletion: in-frame replacement of the gene by a kanamycin-resistance cassette | Results, "Genes, functions, and pathways associated with cell size and shape" |
| Medium | M9 with 0.1% casamino acids and 0.2% glucose, liquid | Results, same section as the strain |
| Temperature | 30 °C | Results, same section |
| Culture | 150 µl in 96-well plates, continuous shaking in a plate reader | Materials and Methods, "Screening setup and microscopy" |
| What $y$ is | Maximal growth rate from a Gompertz fit of the growth curve, divided by that of the parent strain | Results, same section; the ratio is how the served store holds it |
| Unit of $y$ | None (a ratio). The publication releases the rate in min<sup>-1</sup> | Dataset EV2, column `alpha_max (min-1)` |
| Replicates | One culture per record. The reference is 240 cultures of the parent strain | Results, same section; Figure 4 legend |
| Uncertainty reported | None per strain | one culture per record |
