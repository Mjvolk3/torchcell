description: Planned query mode that starts from a gene.
---

<StatusBadge status="planned" />

Planned: start from a yeast gene and retrieve the experiments that perturb that gene
or genes similar to it. Gene similarity here means a similarity score between two
genes, for example between their [encodings](/benchmark/encodings). The intended use
is to collect the measurements around a gene of interest when the gene itself was
perturbed in few experiments. Which encodings or other similarity sources are offered
is not decided.
