\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Pooled genetic screens read out by single-cell RNA sequencing turn a library of
perturbations into a dense map from each perturbation to its transcriptional
consequence. This document works out what that costs in \org{S.\ cerevisiae},
what sets the ceiling on how many genes can be perturbed in one cell, and which
parameter is actually binding. It is a planning document for method selection,
not a manuscript: the intended readers are collaborators deciding what to build.
Numbers are regenerated from committed scripts rather than transcribed, and
every claim that is not backed by a hash-pinned artifact in our library mirror
is flagged in the text.
\end{abstract}
