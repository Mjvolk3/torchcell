A **baseline** is a simple, standard model whose score gives every other row on a
board something to be compared with. The same baseline procedure is run over every
dataset:

- **kNN**: k-nearest-neighbors regression over a gene encoding.
- **Linear regression** over a gene encoding.

A **gene encoding** is a fixed numeric vector per gene; the encodings used are listed
under [Encodings](./encodings.mdx).

Baseline rows are marked with a "baseline" chip and a tinted background on the
[leaderboard](./leaderboard.mdx), and they are drawn as open gray markers in the
charts.

The baseline runs are planned, and so are the details of the procedure: which
hyperparameters are searched and how the encodings are combined with each model.
Baselines are meant to follow the same [training protocol](./training-protocol.mdx)
as any submission. Until they are uploaded, a board has no baseline rows.
