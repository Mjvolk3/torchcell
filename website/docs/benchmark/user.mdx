Everyone with a scored submission, most recent submitter first. A name opens that
person's public record: every scored submission, with validation and test over time for
each dataset. Names on the [leaderboard](./leaderboard.mdx) open the same record, and
your own is linked from the [account page](./account.mdx). [Integrity](./integrity.mdx)
explains why this history is public.
