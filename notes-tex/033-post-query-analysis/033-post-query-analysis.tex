\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The 031 plan for inhibitor-tolerance modeling was measured on dev-tree records. The pooled
store the model trains on, build 001 of experiment 033, holds 6{,}394{,}540 served
measurements as 6{,}042{,}771 cells, a cell being a strain in one environment, over
5{,}863 perturbed genes, 6{,}229 environments and 5{,}463 compounds. Every number here is
read from that store.

The plan's data claims hold. All served records are present, the 648{,}977 Hoepfner pairs
measured in both ploidy arms are there, the 41 Vanacloig reliability indexes match the
plan's to floating-point error with a median ceiling of 0.84, the 71 repeated Hoepfner
screen pairs give the plan's median reliability of 0.611, every dosed compound has a
fingerprint vector, and the cross-source correlations are reproduced. Only Hillenmeyer
needs its sign flipped.

Five facts of the store are new. The store's row is not the plan's row: the plan's
statistics describe 3.08 million (gene, compound) pairs, and the store holds twice as many
cells because a cell keeps dose, duration and ploidy arm. The label table holds the last
measurement of each of 278{,}581 repeated cells, an accident of insertion order. No
Hoepfner cell is paired across ploidy inside one environment, because the heterozygous arm
ran 20 generations and the homozygous arm 5, so the data that would fit the heterozygosity
factor changes duration with ploidy, and the two arms correlate at 0.13 against a noise
limit of 0.61. 80{,}332 Hillenmeyer cells have no complete compound vector. And after
per-source standardizing, 2 to 3 percent of each source's cells carry 41 to 80 percent of
its squared error. Section~\ref{sec:next} turns each into a decision with a
recommendation, none of which has been run.
\end{abstract}
