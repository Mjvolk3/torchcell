\subsection{Time\secstatus{todo}}\label{sec:time}

A review comment on the previous version, anchored at Hackett 2020's time points, raised
the concern that nothing in the substrate carries a time component, and that one is needed
eventually for concentration-over-time modeling of the ordinary-differential-equation
kind. Both halves are right. Seven of the sixty rows carry a time dimension
(Table~\ref{tab:final}, \emph{Time} column): Hackett 2020's induction series, Jariani 2020's
lag-phase course, Jackson 2023's continuous course, Wang 2022's three ages, Airoldi 2016's
upshift course, and the two rate rows, Sun 2013 and Martin-Perez 2017, whose rates are
derived from a labeling series that is not itself released. The steady-state chemostat
rows (Brauer 2008, Boer 2010, Hackett 2016, Yu 2021) vary dilution rate, which is a growth
rate, not a time axis, and are not counted.

What the schema holds today is \texttt{Environment.duration\_hours} and
\texttt{Environment.duration\_generations}, treatment durations that make two exposure
lengths two distinct environments. A time series can therefore be ingested now, as one
environment per time point, and that is how Hackett 2020's roughly 200 records per strain
are counted in Table~\ref{tab:final}. It is a flattening: a model sees time as a
categorical condition, and nothing on the record says that eight environments are one
series in order. A first-class time field on the record and a series identity that groups
its points are the two schema additions a $c(t)$ model needs, and the seven rows above are
the ones that would exercise them. They are recorded here as an open schema item rather
than folded into this list's ranking, since the ranking rule does not yet score a time
axis.

\subsection{Sun 2013 against the other deletion-strain expression sets\secstatus{todo}}\label{sec:sun}

A second comment asked whether Sun 2013 (row 19) overlaps the other deletion-strain
expression datasets. The overlap is on the strain axis and not on the measurement. The
built Kemmeren 2014 (1,484 deletions) and the candidates Hughes 2000, Hu 2007 and Lenstra
2011 each store a steady-state transcript level per gene and per deletion; Sun 2013 stores
the synthesis rate and the decay rate that produce that level, across 46 deletions of mRNA
degradation and metabolism genes, and none of the others stores a rate. It is therefore a
second layer on shared strains, the join named in Table~\ref{tab:synergies}, rather than a
second copy of a layer already held. What is not settled is the exact shared-strain count:
the 46 are single deletions expected to be inside the Kemmeren set by gene identity, but
the GEO strain list was not fetched, and the abstract does not say the strains came from
the deletion collection. The row's figures are marked \emph{recall} for that reason.
