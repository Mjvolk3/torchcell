\noindent
The database holds 50 schematized and L0--L4-verified \org{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}
datasets, of which 36 are served and 14 are built and waiting on the next full graph
rebuild. Table~\ref{tab:final} is the final selection for datasets 51 to 100: the fifty
recommended builds in rank order, then ten extra in rank order so that a row which proves
unreachable has a named replacement. Every figure the ranking used sits in that table
beside the reason the row is there. Table~\ref{tab:sources} gives each row's citation, link,
data location and the reason for its position; Table~\ref{tab:synergies} names the joins
each row opens against a built dataset or another row; Table~\ref{tab:excluded} records
what was considered and dropped; Sec.~\ref{sec:summary} closes with the summary
statistics and the two review points from the previous version.

The order is a rule, not a taste (Sec.~\ref{sec:rule}): the rows a planned yeast
Perturb-seq is designed against come first, then the rows that put two molecular layers on
one joinable axis, then scale; inside a band, tier, then measurements. The fifty are exactly
the first two bands, 30 rows and 20. The ten extra are the last three molecular-layers rows
and the first seven scale rows. The 108 rows ranked below them are kept in the generating
script and its JSON and are not printed here.
