\section{The environment: fields, dose and compound vectors\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:environment}

\notesec{Physical and medium fields}
The plan gives the physical fields no channel, on the ground that each is constant inside
a source and the dataset token can carry it. Table~\ref{tab:physical} reads the fields
from the store. The ground holds for Vanacloig and Wildenhain, where every field takes one
value. It does not hold for the other two. Hillenmeyer varies three fields: 28{,}828 cells
are in SD or YP rather than YPD, 28{,}560 state a temperature of 20, 23 or 37~C, and
183{,}702, which is 7.1 percent of the source, ran for a generation count other than 20.
The first two are confined to the ten environments that dose no compound
(Table~\ref{tab:vectorless}), where the medium or the temperature is itself the
condition; every Hillenmeyer cell that doses a compound is in YPD with no temperature
stated. The generation count varies among the dosed cells as well. Hoepfner varies
duration, and the variation is the ploidy arm (Section~\ref{sec:genotype}). A token per
source assigns one vector to all of these.

\notesec{Dose}
The store confirms the three dose regimes (Table~\ref{tab:genotype}). 5{,}585{,}863 cells,
92 percent of the pool, state a concentration that converts to molar. Vanacloig states
none. The basis token is close to a second dataset token: every Hoepfner cell is on a
target-inhibition basis, every Wildenhain cell states no basis, and Hillenmeyer states
none on 2{,}380{,}154 of its single-compound cells and a fixed basis on 5{,}704. Only
Vanacloig splits, with 136{,}237 cells dosed to a target inhibition and 6{,}981 at a fixed
concentration, which are its two compounds benomyl and methyl methanesulfonate.

\notesec{Cells a compound vector cannot describe}
The plan's processor resolves each dosed compound against the embedding table and raises
when a vector is missing. Table~\ref{tab:embedding} counts what that does on the store.
Eleven encoders embed all 5{,}463 compounds, and under each of them 80{,}332 cells still
have no complete vector. All are Hillenmeyer cells, in fourteen environments
(Table~\ref{tab:vectorless}): 57{,}388 cells in ten environments dose no compound, and
22{,}944 in four dose tunicamycin, a homologue mixture that carries no InChIKey. The
147{,}953 cells that dose two compounds are covered, since both vectors exist and the
representation pools a set. The three-dimensional encoder leaves 406{,}318 cells
uncovered, 6.7 percent of the pool, because 21 compounds have no conformer.
