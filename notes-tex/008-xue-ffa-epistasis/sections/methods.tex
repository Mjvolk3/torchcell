\section{Methods\secstatus{todo}}

\subsection*{Data and normalization}

Titers for five fatty acid species and their sum were taken from the source study's
supplementary tables for the base strain and for all 175 deletion combinations. Every titer
is divided by the base strain's titer for the same species in the same experiment, so all
values below are ratios and the base strain is 1 by construction. The sum, referred to as
total titer, is formed per replicate before averaging rather than by summing the five
published species means, so it is a measured quantity and not a sixth derived one. Loading
and normalization are in \file{experiments/008-xue-ffa/scripts/free_fatty_acid_interactions.py}.

\subsection*{Epistasis models}

Four nulls were fit on the same normalized strain means.

\notesec{Multiplicative} The expected phenotype of a combination is the product of its
parts. Digenic $\varepsilon_{ij} = f_{ij} - f_i f_j$; trigenic as in Eq.~\ref{eq:tau}. This
is the form used by the yeast genetic interaction maps and is the model every figure uses
unless it says otherwise
(\file{free_fatty_acid_interactions.py}).

\notesec{Additive} The same construction with sums in place of products
(\file{additive_free_fatty_acid_interactions.py}).

\notesec{GLM log-link} A generalized linear model with a Gamma family and a log link, whose
three-way coefficient is the interaction
(\file{glm_log_link_epistatic_interactions.py}).

\notesec{Log-OLS} Ordinary least squares on $\log$ titer differenced against the base strain
(\file{log_ols_wt_differencing_epistatic_interactions.py}).

The equivalence used in Fig.~\ref{fig:models}c,d, that the saturated three-way interaction
on log titer reproduces the shipped GLM and log-OLS coefficients, is asserted in code rather
than assumed: \file{linear_vs_log_scale_sign_test.py} recomputes it from the strain means
and raises unless it agrees with both fitted models to $r > 0.99$.

\subsection*{Uncertainty and multiple testing}

Standard errors propagate through the interaction expression by the delta method, using the
per-strain standard errors of the replicate means. The resulting $t$ statistic is referred
to the Welch-Satterthwaite effective degrees of freedom of the linear combination, computed
from each term's contribution to the combined standard error. This matters more than it
usually does. A trigenic score combines seven independently measured strains, and referring
its $t$ statistic to the degrees of freedom of the smallest single input, as an earlier
version of this analysis did, gives 1 or 2 rather than a median of 4.28, and suppresses
nearly every call. Across the 990 multiplicative tests of the whole run, none was
significant at a 5\% false discovery rate before the correction and 456 are after it
(audit record: \file{notes/plan.008-xue-ffa-epistasis-audit.2026.08.15.md}).

Benjamini-Hochberg controls the false discovery rate. The family matters and is stated
wherever a count appears. Counts in Figs.~\ref{fig:landscape} and \ref{fig:models} correct
over the tests of a single readout, since that is what those figures claim about. The
enrichment analysis in Fig.~\ref{fig:models}b uses the stored per-test $P < 0.05$ over all
six readouts pooled, which is the family the enrichment scripts were written against.

\subsection*{Path enumeration and monotonicity}

For each triple, all six orders of introducing its three deletions were expanded into a
four-rung trajectory: base strain, one deletion, two deletions, three deletions, taking the
measured strain at each rung. A route is monotone when every step is strictly positive, and
monotone within one standard error when every step exceeds the negative of that step's
propagated standard error. Valley depth is $1 - \min(\text{rungs})$ on a route, and a
triple's valley depth is the minimum over its six routes, meaning its shallowest available
approach. The greedy campaign in Fig.~\ref{fig:inaccessible}b starts at the base strain,
evaluates every combination reachable by one further deletion, takes the highest if it
exceeds the current strain, and stops otherwise. Enumeration and monotonicity are in
\file{ffa_epistatic_path_panels.py}; the greedy simulation is in
\file{perspective_figure_panels.py}.

\subsection*{Interaction graph enrichment}

Ten interaction graphs were queried for each triple: physical, regulatory and genetic
interactions, TFLink, and the neighborhood, fusion, co-occurrence, coexpression,
experimental and database channels of STRING 12.0. A triple counts as overlapping a graph
when its three genes induce a connected subgraph of that graph. Fold enrichment is the ratio
of the overlapping fraction among significant triples to the overlapping fraction among the
rest, and significance is Fisher's exact test on the corresponding two-by-two table. Three
of the ten graphs contain no connected triple among these ten genes at all and are omitted
from Fig.~\ref{fig:models}b, which is a statement about those graphs' coverage of this gene
set and not about epistasis
(\file{interaction_graph_enrichment_analysis.py}).

\subsection*{Figures}

Every panel is produced by a committed script and written as a true-size SVG at one of the
standard panel widths, then converted to PDF for this document. Panels come from
\file{perspective_figure_panels.py}, \file{notable_interaction_selection.py} and
\file{ffa_epistatic_path_panels.py}; the network overlay comes from
\file{create_ffa_multigraph_overlays.py}. The multi-panel arrangements are assembled from
the same SVGs by \file{build_perspective_drawio.py} into editable draw.io sources under
\file{notes/assets/drawio/}.

\subsection*{Code and data availability}

All analysis scripts are in \file{experiments/008-xue-ffa/scripts/} and all derived tables
in \file{experiments/008-xue-ffa/results/} of the torchcell repository. Selection of which
interactions appear in a figure is itself a committed artifact rather than a post-hoc
choice: \file{notable_interaction_selection.py} states the rule, applies it, and writes the
selected set to \file{results/notable_interaction_selection.csv}.
