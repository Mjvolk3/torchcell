\section{What follows\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:next}

\notesec{Baselines before models}
The ceilings of Section~\ref{sec:noise} become scores through the same ladder the
trigenic work used: a per-gene additive ridge and a nearest-neighbor model over genes,
fit on Vanacloig alone and then on Vanacloig with HOM, under a split that holds out
whole compounds. A held-out compound is the case a strain-design model faces, a stress
it has never seen, and it is the split that isolates the model from the dataset
offsets of Section~\ref{sec:axes}. Every per-compound score is reported beside that
compound's ceiling, because a score of 0.3 on a compound with a ceiling of 0.9 and a
score of 0.3 on one with a ceiling of 0.35 are different results.

\notesec{Two transfer tests}
Section~\ref{sec:structure} found one exact match that transfers. The model question
is whether a partner dataset helps on held-out inhibitors through exact matches only,
or through chemical similarity as well. Both are testable on the same split: add HOM
restricted to the exact-match compounds, then add all of HOM with compounds encoded by
one of the encoders of Section~\ref{sec:encoders}, and compare the held-out Vanacloig
score against Vanacloig alone. Section~\ref{sec:chemistry} gives the prior for the
second arm.

\notesec{The environment encoding}
The first environment representation is the dosed compound alone, one vector per record
from a chosen encoder. The second adds the medium: SynBase, YPD and SC dropouts enter as
sets of compound vectors rather than as names, which is the representation the
metabolism arm will share. Whether adding the metabolic model degrades the held-out
compound score is the ablation that decides whether the integrated representation
costs anything.

\notesec{Lian 2019 as the external furfural check}
Lian et al.\ 2019 (\texttt{10.1038/s41467-019-13621-4}) selected a genome-wide
CRISPRa, CRISPRi and CRISPRd library on furfural. Round 1 of the CRISPRd arm, about
21{,}000 guides over about 5{,}200 genes in the unmodified host, is the nearest thing
to a deletion screen; the activation and interference arms have no deletion analog
and rounds 2 and 3 carry integrated backgrounds. Furfural's ceiling in Vanacloig is
0.88, so there is signal to transfer, and the environments differ (aerobic SED-URA
with G418 at 5\,mM against anaerobic SynBase at IC30), so a weak score would not
separate the model from the modality.

That paper also validated its hits as genome-integrated strains, and the per-replicate
values behind those figures are released as a Source Data workbook now held in the raw
mirror. One sheet crosses three integrated edits fully: interference of \emph{SIZ1},
activation of \emph{NAT1} and interference of \emph{PDR1}, as seven strains plus the wild
type, at three furfural concentrations in biological triplicate. At 17.5\,mM the mean
relative biomass is 1.03 for the wild type, 1.95, 0.77 and 0.48 for the three single
edits, 55.7 and 25.7 for the two doubles containing the \emph{SIZ1} edit, 0.53 for the
double without it, and 142.2 for the triple. Two edits that do nothing alone become large
only together with the third, so the set scores an interaction rather than a ranking, and
it is the external check with the most stringent form available on any inhibitor here.

\notesec{The isobutanol validator, and what is missing from it}
Isobutanol has no equivalent. It is the inhibitor the strain-design work targets and the
served graph carries it as a stress only in Vanacloig, at a ceiling of 0.786. The nearest
external check is a set of engineered strains from the laboratory whose single-deletion
biosensor screen the graph already serves: 23 strains that each combine one
mitochondrial-morphology deletion with a five-cassette mitochondrial isobutanol pathway,
and two double deletions, \emph{gln3} with \emph{gcn4} and \emph{gln3} with \emph{gnp1},
whose combined tolerance is reported as less than the sum of the two singles. The
genotypes are released and machine-readable; the titers and tolerance factors appear only
in figures, with no per-strain table and no deposit, so nothing can be ingested from them
without the numbers themselves.

The prediction is therefore recorded before the check exists. A model fit on Vanacloig
with HOM as a gene prior should place deletion of \emph{GLN3} and of \emph{GNP1} among the
strongest tolerance gains on isobutanol, and should place both double deletions below the
sum of their singles. The first two are reachable by the additive baseline of this section;
the third needs an interaction, which makes it the informative one.
