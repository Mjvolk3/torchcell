  {\large What the four chemogenomic datasets can share as input, which molecular encoders
   carry the compounds, and how one decoder reads four response scales}}

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Five served datasets hold 7.48 million chemogenomic records, and training one model across
them requires a single input representation and a single decoder. They disagree on four axes.
Ploidy runs from haploid through homozygous diploid to heterozygous diploid. Compound panels
run from 41 to 5{,}170. Dose runs from an unquantified target inhibition through a
nine-order-of-magnitude molar range to a single fixed concentration. And the response is a
log ratio, a z score or a sensitivity score depending on the source. Every number here is
measured from the served records through their loaders.

The genotype axis resolves cleanly. The schema already carries heterozygosity as a
genome-wide ploidy baseline and a per-locus copy-number deviation, and a per-gene functional
dose, copies present over copies in the reference, is lossless for these datasets because
every perturbation is a full deletion or a one-of-two variant. It is learnable: Hoepfner
supplies 648{,}977 gene-by-compound cells measured both heterozygous and homozygous. The gap
is entirely in the tensor layer, which reads only a gene name and no environment field at
all. The dose axis does not resolve, so dose enters as a basis token and a masked log molar
value rather than one number. The physical fields, temperature, aerobicity, duration and pH,
are constant inside every dataset, so they need no channel and the dataset token carries
them. Chemically, pooling buys the phenolic inhibitors and not the small alcohols, and
Wildenhain supplies essentially all of the gain; twelve molecular encoders cover all 5{,}472
dosed compounds, the 37 medium components and 894 of the 1{,}378 Yeast9 species from one
embedding table.

Two decisions are taken. The Hillenmeyer homozygous arm is dropped, leaving four datasets and
6.39 million records. And the functional dose scales the existing cross-attention
perturbation operator through a factor that equals one for a deletion, so every current
dataset's model is unchanged and heterozygosity is the only new behavior. The decoder is one
scalar head with a per-source dataset token at the readout, the mechanism experiment 030
smoke-tested. The first round is four arms on a fixed compound-cold evaluation within
Vanacloig, scored against the reliability ceiling of every compound.
\end{abstract}
