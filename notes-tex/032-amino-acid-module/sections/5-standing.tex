\section{Where the module stands on this target\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:standing}

The amino-acid endpoint is noisy, and every model reads at a tenth of what the noise
allows. Its own strain-level retest is 0.20, the second screen agrees with it at 0.03,
and every model, transformer or ladder, at either width, with or without a store, reads
between 0.00 and 0.06. Four forms of the store affine sit at or under their control; the
solve head recovers the affine's loss and lands at the control; width, 100 more epochs
and the rule counts change nothing. What the campaign fixes is the target's position
rather than the module's: the retest of 0.20 bounds a second screen, the bound on a
model against one measurement is its square root, about 0.45 on the Spearman scale and
0.62 on the Pearson (Section~\ref{sec:concordance}), and every arm reads at a tenth of
it, so the arms differ within a band that is small against the ceiling and against the
seed spread alike. The physically
stated stores read in the transformer confirm it from the other side: the adjustment
store and the driving-force concentrations each sit between the parsimonious store and
the control inside both spreads, so the parsimonious store's small loss is specific to
that build and nothing metabolic rises above the control. The one read above the
transformer band is annotation with sequence through a perceptron, 0.065, and the flux
row lowers every read it joins. The sampled cone (round 143, 0.019) and the polytope
mean (148, 0.032) read under every other store; width 6 (146, 0.025) brings the
collapse back; dropout 0.3 on the store arm (157, 0.045) returns half of what the store
costs and stays under the control. GO in the affine slot (153, 0.058) is the one
transformer arm above its control on five seeds of six, at the GO perceptron's level;
the HiGHS vector appended to it in the same affine takes 0.003 from it on every seed
(160), where on betaxanthin it added 0.009, and dropout 0.3 around that affine moves it
by less than a thousandth (162), as does dropout around the GO affine alone (165): as
on betaxanthin, the annotation arm is the affine alone. On twelve seeds the
annotation's gain holds at 0.009, above the control on ten of twelve, but averaging
the control's twelve seeds reads 0.067, above the annotation's twelve-seed ensemble and
above every single cell of either arm (168): the gain is real and smaller than what the
control's own seed noise is worth when averaged away. The flux vector beside GO on
twelve seeds is $-0.002$, above the annotation alone on two of twelve (174): what adds
0.012 on betaxanthin takes on this target, at any seed count read. The one input that
moves the target is measured, not inferred: the strain's own betaxanthin score, a single
column, reads 0.071 by forest and 0.078 appended to GO, the highest read of any model on
this target (Section~\ref{sec:concordance}). In the transformer's affine the same
column beside GO reads 0.053, under the GO affine on four of six (183), with the pair
confounded by the store's encoding; the column alone reads 0.060 with the highest
validation correlation and, ensembled, the highest test rank of any arm on this target,
0.073 (184). The measured score is the one input the transformer reads at the
annotation's level from a single column. The encoding control settles the pair:
standardizing GO costs the affine 0.005 and its early selection, and beside GO under
that encoding the score is $+0.001$ (189), so the score adds to the annotation in the
forest and not in the affine. On twelve seeds the score alone holds the annotation's
single-seed level (0.056 against 0.057) with six times its spread, and its twelve-seed
ensemble, 0.072, stays the highest of any arm on this target (195).
