torchcell.sga

A colony-fitness pipeline adapted from SGAtools for single CRISPR knockouts dispensed onto plates by an ECHO acoustic liquid handler. It reads the gitter colony-size file and the ECHO picklist (io), corrects positional plate artifacts (normalize), and scores each knockout as fitness relative to the on-plate BY4741 wild type (score). image and cellpose_seg measure colony sizes from plate photographs, and viz draws plate heatmaps and histograms. The typical call sequence is read_gitter_dat(), read_echo_picklist(), merge_layout(), normalize_plate(), then score_plate().
