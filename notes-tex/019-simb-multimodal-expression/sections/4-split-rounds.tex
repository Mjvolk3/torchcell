\section{The split rounds, 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-27: partition dominates every lever\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:split-rounds}

The rounds after the readout round share one template, registered in the expression-fit
review of 2026-09-17: the arms of a contrast run co-resident on one card for one partition
(a split seed, which fixes the held-out strains) and one initialization seed, so every
contrast is paired within card; the score is the mean of the per-gene validation Pearson
over the last sixth of the budget (the fixed window), reported beside the rolling maximum,
which is an upward-biased order statistic; a lever is adopted only above $+0.02$ with the
paired confidence interval excluding zero, at least three of four partitions positive, and
the test score at the best-validation checkpoint agreeing in sign. Six rounds ran under it,
on the IGB \file{gpu} A40s and the \file{cabbi} RTX~6000 cards, three or four runs per card.
Every number below is the fixed-window contrast at the matched epoch unless it says
otherwise; ``pairs'' counts partition by seed.

\pfstep{The partition is the largest effect in every round} Holding the arm fixed, the
partition mean of the reference arm spans $0.128$ to $0.164$ over the four v13 partitions
at epoch 5{,}179, $0.116$ to $0.182$ in v17 at 1{,}199, $0.112$ to $0.163$ in v18 at
1{,}121, and $0.064$ to $0.089$ on the proteome in v14 at 1{,}352. Folding the test strains
into training on split 0 lifts the same validation strains from $0.164$ to $0.213$
(\texttt{s0\_90}). No arm effect in any round reaches half of the partition spread, so a
contrast read on one partition is a statement about that partition.
\src{experiments/019-simb-multimodal/results/v13_split_readout.json, v14_proteome_readout.json, v17_locality_readout.json, v18_hygiene_readout.json}

\pfstep{v13 and v14, the readout concatenation, a tie on both labels} \texttt{V\_concat}
hands the per-gene readout the concatenation of the perturbed gene state, the gene's own
embedding and the strain context in place of the perturbed state alone. On expression
(v13, 24 runs, four partitions plus the 90/10 fold, two seeds, canceled at epoch
5{,}179 of 6{,}000 once the run mean had moved $+0.001$ since epoch 4{,}000) the contrast
is $+0.025$ with a paired sd of $0.052$ over twelve pairs ($t = 1.6$, seven of twelve
positive); the sd is one collapsed reference run (\texttt{825on260}, zero from epoch
1{,}344), and the eleven clean pairs read $+0.007$. On the Messner knockout proteome (v14,
16 runs, four partitions, two seeds, canceled at 1{,}352 of 2{,}000 with every run past
its peak) it is $+0.007$, sd $0.015$, eight pairs, five positive. Neither approaches the
bar. Both rounds forfeited the end-of-run test pass; the best-validation checkpoints are
pulled to GilaHyper and unread.
\src{experiments/019-simb-multimodal/results/v13_split_readout.json, v14_proteome_readout.json, eval_ckpt_manifest_v13.tsv, eval_ckpt_manifest_v14.tsv}

\pfstep{v15, weight decay, cut short by the five-day rule} AdamW decay $10^{-2}$ and
$10^{-1}$ against the reference $10^{-8}$ on split seeds 1 and 2, two seeds, 6{,}000
epochs budgeted. Four of twelve runs died on 2026-09-20 of a 60~s file-lock timeout in a
respawned data-loader worker (the trainer reads \file{experiment_types.json} under an
exclusive lock at every worker start, and \file{persistent_workers} was off), the two
orphaned cards were canceled, and the survivor was stopped at the cabbi five-day limit at
epoch 3{,}770. At the last shared epoch, 1{,}094, over four pairs: $10^{-2}$ reads
$+0.011$ ($t = 1.7$, three of four), $10^{-1}$ reads $-0.010$ ($t = -1.6$, one of four).
Neither is a result at that epoch; the rounds that followed run with persistent workers.
\src{experiments/019-simb-multimodal/results/v15_wd_readout.json}

\pfstep{v17, where the deletion enters, twelve pairs, not adopted} The reference adds one
deletion vector to every gene token after the graph attention layers. \texttt{L\_self} adds
the hop-0 indicator ``this token is the deleted gene''; \texttt{L\_prop2} adds the one- and
two-hop reachability of the deletion along each of the nine gene-gene graphs, nineteen
features in all, gate forced open. Four partitions, three seeds, 1{,}200 epochs, 36 runs
on the A40s. Propagation reads $+0.012$, sd $0.033$, nine of twelve positive, with the
confidence interval running from $-0.009$ to $+0.033$, and $+0.003$ on the test set at the
best-validation checkpoint (seven of twelve). The self indicator reads $+0.010$ ($t = 1.4$)
and $+0.004$ on test. At eleven pairs propagation had read $+0.020$ with nine positive; the
twelfth (\texttt{sylu3gsw}, split 3, seed 2) peaked at $0.062$ by epoch 175 and decayed to
$0.009$ while its two card mates reached $0.10$ and $0.14$, and the template scores all
twelve. The layer chain cannot carry the deletion's reach on its own because no layer
sees the deletion; whether injecting it before the graph layers recovers the eleven-pair
number is untested.
\src{experiments/019-simb-multimodal/results/v17_locality_readout.json}

\pfstep{v18, the masked objective off and a per-gene context row, twelve pairs with one
partial} \texttt{Y\_k0} sets \file{mask_schedule} to $[0]$, removing the masked-label
objective that every run since v10 carried, with the plumbing left in place;
\texttt{Y\_ctx} gives every gene an affine row over the strain context, zero-gated so step
0 is the shared MLP. Four partitions, three seeds, 1{,}200 epochs, 36 runs on cabbi at
one to three cards, the last task at epoch 1{,}121 when read (window 921 to 1{,}121). The
no-mask arm reads $-0.012$, sd $0.017$, four of twelve positive, negative on every
partition ($-0.003$, $-0.008$, $-0.010$, $-0.028$), and runs 21 to 25\,\% more epochs per
hour than its card mates (759 against 629 epochs at one moment on one card, 360 against
289 and 272 against 219 on two others). The context row reads $+0.005$, sd $0.015$, nine
of twelve positive, $-0.017$ on split 0 and $+0.009$ to $+0.015$ on the other three.
Neither clears the bar. The masked objective is dropped from the trunk for its cost, not
for a gain, by decision of 2026-09-25; the imputation capability it served returns as its
own round if that application returns.
\src{experiments/019-simb-multimodal/results/v18_hygiene_readout.json}

\pfstep{v16, the joint round at 500 epochs, partial on both sides} Three arms on the
\file{fig3_proteome} partition, where every strain carries both readouts: \texttt{J\_ref},
the proteome head alone (v14's reference at this budget); \texttt{J\_expr}, the expression
head alone on the same strains; \texttt{J\_joint}, both heads on one trunk, the proteome
head the checkpoint monitor. Three partitions, two seeds, 500 epochs, eighteen runs, three
per A40. On the proteome, joint minus proteome-only reads $-0.008$, sd $0.013$, three of
six positive ($-0.016$ on the rolling maximum, none of six). On expression, four of the six
expression-only runs collapsed to exactly zero validation Pearson (peaks below $0.03$ by
epochs 39 to 388), while every joint expression head trained, to $0.08$ to $0.12$, and every
one was still rising at epoch 499 with a slope of $+0.003$ to $+0.023$ per hundred epochs;
on the two clean pairs joint minus expression-only is $+0.007$. The proteome-only reference
peaked between epochs 93 and 463 with its validation loss rising from epoch 180 to 230 on
all six runs; two of the six joint proteome heads peaked at 437 and 497. So the joint arm is
scored before its own peak on both heads while the reference is scored past its peak, and
the 500-epoch reads are partial. Every run left \file{last.ckpt}; on 2026-09-23 all
eighteen were continued to epoch 1{,}200 as new W\&B runs carrying \file{wandb.resumed_from}
(IGB 2410399, \file{gpu}, three cards, launcher stage \file{resume_many}), and the readout
stitches each continuation onto its source. The continuation started on 2026-09-26 and is
read when it finishes.
\src{experiments/019-simb-multimodal/results/v16_joint_readout.json, v16_joint_expr_readout.json, v16_resume_map.tsv}

\pfstep{Collapse is the recurring phenomenon, and it decides calls} A run whose validation
Pearson decays to near zero and stays there: one of 24 in v13, four of six expression-only
runs in v16, two of 36 in v17 (\texttt{tnv3dbe4}, self indicator, split 1 seed 0, and
\texttt{sylu3gsw}), none of 36 in v18 so far, none of the 24 joint or proteome-only runs in
v16. Which arm collapses shows no pattern across rounds, and a single collapsed pair moved
v17's twelve-pair contrast from above the bar to below it. Any claim built on a paired
contrast has to state in advance how a collapsed run is scored; the template currently
scores it as it stands. What causes the collapse is not measured.

\pfstep{Cluster rules that shaped these rounds} Every \file{cabbi} submission carries a
walltime of at most five days; the scheduler accepted a ten-day job and the rule is
enforced at the submit line. The \file{gpu} partition has six A40s in all and, by the IGB
administrator's instruction of 2026-09-21, at most three of ours run at once
(\texttt{--array=<range>\%3}), with arrays chained by dependency when two are pending so
the two throttles do not add. Both rules are written into the launcher's header.
