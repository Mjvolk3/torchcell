\input{tables/counts}

The recommended set carries roughly $1.8\times10^8$ genotype-by-environment instances and
$5.7\times10^8$ individual measurements. Both are lower bounds: rows whose axes could not
be pinned down contribute nothing to either total, and the instance figures marked
$\dagger$ are products of the two axes rather than counts the papers report.

The totals are dominated by a handful of rows, so quoting them without naming those rows
would mislead. de Boer 2020 alone supplies $10^8$ instances, Turco 2023 $3.8\times10^7$ and
Lee 2014 $1.3\times10^7$. Of those three, Turco's contribution is not net new, since it is a
meta-aggregation of screens this table lists separately, and Lee's is not currently reachable
(Sec.~\ref{sec:status}). de Boer's stands, and it is a different kind of instance from the
rest: an 80-base promoter with an expression readout, not a strain with a phenotype.

The honest summary is that this list contains one dataset larger than everything already
built, one larger than everything else here, and about $2\times10^7$ instances spread across
the remaining 148 rows.
