## 2026.10.12 - The three yeast cells of Figure 1 as assets

Figure 1 of the manuscript embeds its raster images as base64 PNG inside
`notes/assets/drawio/Fig1-torchcell-overview.drawio.svg`; three of them are one yeast cell
drawing in three tints. This script writes them to
`notes/assets/images/028-gene-essentiality/yeast_cell_{orange,rose,sand}.png`, found by
the x and y the SVG places them at, for the gate sheet's label panel
([[experiments.028-gene-essentiality.scripts.essentiality_gate_sheet]]). If Figure 1 is
re-exported and the cells move, the position table here is the one thing to update.
