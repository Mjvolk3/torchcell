## 2026.09.27 - Published counts for the yeast metabolome, each with its quote

Nothing here is measured. Every row carries the citation key of the mirrored paper and the
verbatim sentence, so a figure plotting these can mark them reported and a reader can check.

**The finding that changes the plan.** [[wuSystematicallyExploringYeast2026]], "Systematically
exploring yeast metabolism through retrobiosynthesis and deep learning" (Nielsen and Kerkhoven
are authors), ALREADY DID the retrobiosynthetic expansion of yeast metabolism in 2026:

- YMDB holds 16,042 entries with a SMILES; 14,882 are missing from Yeast9 (14,310 lipids, 572 non-lipids)
- 21,921 enzyme-associated reaction rules + 213 spontaneous, from MetaNetX and MetaCyc; 95% regenerate their originals
- connected network of 1,092,946 reactions after removing dead ends
- Yeast-MetaTwin: 16,244 metabolites, 1,976 genes, 59,865 reactions
- **coverage raised from 7% to 92%** of the yeast metabolome (non-lipid 54% -> 75%)
- residual: 267 non-lipid metabolites (1.7%) could not be connected with enzyme-annotated reactions

So proposing a retrobiosynthetic enumeration of yeast metabolism as new work would duplicate a
published result from the group that maintains the reconstruction.

**The gap that is real.** Yeast dry mass is published as 40-50% protein, ~10% RNA, ~10% lipid,
~25% cell wall ([[miloCellBiologyNumbers2016]], [[feldmannYeastMolecularCell2012]]). The
fraction that is **small molecules is NEVER stated for yeast** in the mirror. Only the E. coli
figure exists, a soluble / metabolite-and-cofactor pool of 3 to 3.9% of dry weight, and it must
not be quoted as a yeast number. DNA is likewise unstated for yeast.

**Also not recoverable:** the YMDB experimentally-detected versus predicted split. The YMDB 2.0
paper is not in the mirror and the relevant supplementary tables are not mirrored either.
Closing that is a curation decision, not an agent action.
