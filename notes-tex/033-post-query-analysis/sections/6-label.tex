\section{The label of a repeated cell, and what repeats say about noise\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:label}

\notesec{The label table holds the last measurement}
278{,}581 cells fold more than one measurement, and the build left the choice among them
to read time. The label table has already made it. The loop that fills the table in
\file{torchcell/data/neo4j_cell.py} visits the measurements of a cell in order and
overwrites the label with each one, so the value that remains is the last. Table~\ref{tab:policy}
confirms it on every repeated cell of the store: the label equals the last measurement in
all of them, the first in none, and the mean in one cell of 170{,}966 by coincidence. The
order of measurements inside a cell is the order the query returned them, which carries no
meaning.

The choice is not small where it applies. The measurements of a repeated cell differ by a
median of 0.49 standard deviations in Hillenmeyer and 0.36 in Hoepfner, and the label
correlates with the mean of the cell's measurements at 0.81 and 0.85. Repeated cells are
4.6 percent of the pool. They are spread over 585 environments and concentrated in 50 of
them, the ones where two screens share at least 100 genes, which hold 265{,}023 of the
278{,}581 (\file{results/pool_totals.csv}).

\notesec{Repeat agreement recovers the plan's ceilings}
Two screens of one environment are parallel measurements, so their correlation over genes
estimates the reliability of one served value. The plan computed this for Hoepfner from
the raw score matrices. Table~\ref{tab:reliability} computes it from the store's folded
measurements and finds the same 71 screen pairs and the same median of 0.611 over both
arms, with ceilings of 0.759 and 0.799 against the plan's 0.758 and 0.797. For Hillenmeyer
the store holds 19 repeated environments, with a median Pearson of 0.352 and a median
Spearman of 0.248. The plan's raw replicate agreement for the same source is a Spearman of
0.255 over 108 conditions, read from the raw arrays. The two estimates agree, and both sit
far below the reliability of 0.84 that the served standard errors imply, which is the
plan's reason for not using the served errors for this source.

\notesec{The evaluation folds are in the store as planned}
The first-round evaluation holds out one Vanacloig compound at a time. Table~\ref{tab:folds}
lists the 41 folds as the store holds them: between 3{,}388 and 3{,}514 cells each, every
cell with a served standard error, and a reliability index identical to the plan's. The
fold table the evaluation needs can therefore be built from the store alone.
