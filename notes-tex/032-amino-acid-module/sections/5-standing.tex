\section{Where the module stands on this target\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:standing}

The amino-acid endpoint sits at its noise floor. Its own strain-level retest is 0.20, the
second screen agrees with it at 0.03, and every model, transformer or ladder, at either
width, with or without a store, reads between 0.00 and 0.06. Four forms of the store
affine sit at or under their control; the solve head recovers the affine's loss and
lands at the control; width, 100 more epochs and the rule counts change nothing. What
the campaign fixes is the target's position rather than the module's: a strain-level
rank with a 0.20 ceiling has no headroom to show an arm effect against, and the
physically constrained stores (rounds 138, 141, 143) and the size question (round 146)
are read here for completeness beside their betaxanthin reads (the sibling document).
