\section{The endpoint, the protocol and the stores\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:endpoint}

Cachera's betaxanthin screen measures 4,930 deletion strains of BY4741 carrying the
pigment cassette on YPD; the target is the strain's betaxanthin level, and the metric
the task is about is the top-50 enrichment of the highest producers beside the rank
correlation. Every cell is scored under the 027 protocol: Merzbacher's 639 test genes
pinned to test, the epoch selected on validation Pearson (centered five-epoch moving
average), the pinned-test Spearman reported with the top-50 enrichment of high producers
(a 10 percent base rate); the comparators are the 027 rounds' own numbers on the same
split, read from their result files (Table~\ref{tab:target-transfer-comparators}). The
backbone is the essentiality one (pathway multi-hot with ProtT5, width 12, one block,
four attention heads, CRPS on the head, 200 epochs), a fifth of the width 027 trained.
The graph regularizer is requested on every cell and never evaluated at width 12: it is
pinned to the second block, and these cells have one, so \texttt{graph\_reg\_loss} is 0
at all 200 epochs. It is inert in the control and in the arm alike, so the pairing is
unaffected, and every arm is paired by seed over six seeds against the same backbone with
no affine.

The study began inside the essentiality campaign (rounds 117 onward there) and has been
its own study, 031, since 2026-09-23, with its own results tree, W\&B project and round
series (B1 onward; the map from the round numbers used here to the B-series is in the
study's note). The amino-acid half of the same campaign is the sibling document,
\texttt{notes-tex/032-amino-acid-module}, and the essentiality endpoint the backbone and
the store were built on is \texttt{notes-tex/028-gene-essentiality}.

The store represents the strain, not the wild type. For betaxanthin
(\texttt{bx\_reference\_flux\_table.py}, job 2740) the model is yeast-GEM with the
cassette's 46 reactions and 20 demands, the four BY4741 markers deleted through the
rules, on the corrected YPD; per GEM gene two parsimonious solves, growth and
betaxanthin at 90 percent of the knockout's own growth, 8,355 columns with the total
demand last; 1,155 genes, 93 lethal, 180 that move the production potential. ARO4-K229L
and ARO7-G141S relieve feedback inhibition a stoichiometric model does not carry, so
they change no bound. On the graph side the processor had removed the two allele genes
from every Cachera cell graph, since the cassette records them as perturbations, and
pooled them with the deletion; round 120's \texttt{--deletion-only-graph} keeps them in
the graph and out of the pooled set, which is what the strain carries.
