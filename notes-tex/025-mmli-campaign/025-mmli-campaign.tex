\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Two claims rest on this campaign. Training on fitness records merged across datasets
improves prediction of trigenic interactions that the added records do not contain, and a
fixed sequence-derived gene representation, which transfers to genes and organisms a
learned table has never seen, costs nothing where the table is at its best. One seed of the
first claim is measured: adding 739{,}219 double-mutant records to the 376{,}732 triples
raises validation Pearson from 0.439 (three seeds, sd 0.003) to 0.478. The second claim
has no measurement on the random split. Table~\ref{tab:campaign} holds every cell of the
design and Table~\ref{tab:queue} the order in which the single four-GPU node runs the 26
jobs that fill it, about 23 days end to end.
\end{abstract}
