\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Six sorghum-hydrolysate inhibitors, their 63 combinations and three two-way dose grids
were measured on one strain in 2021: bAID, the CRISPR-AID host of Lian 2019, carrying no
deletion, in aerobic YPD, 977 wells over five Bioscreen C runs. Four claims were
registered before anything was scored, and the model-free half of the analysis is now
complete on all four.

Growth under the combinations departs from independence and stops short of Loewe
additivity. Loewe from the single-agent Hill fits separates the combinations that grew
from those that did not at AUROC 0.958 and accuracy 0.825 over all 63, while Bliss
independence from those fits and highest single agent predict growth for every one of
the 63 (accuracy 0.333, AUROC 0.777 and 0.663) and Bliss from the observed single-agent
wells reaches 0.556 and 0.872; 42 of 63 did not grow within 85 h. On the
21 that grew, Bliss from the observed single-agent wells reaches Spearman 0.573 and
overestimates fitness by 0.142 on average, Loewe reaches 0.713 and underestimates it by
0.128. Twelve of the 15 pairs are synergistic against Bliss with bootstrap intervals
below zero, two are additive and one antagonistic, and no interior cell of any isobole
grid grew less than Loewe predicts. Every score is reported under both growth calls,
which disagree on 12 of 197 ex23 wells, all of them 5-HMF combinations.

What the chemogenomic representation adds is, so far, nothing measurable. Profile
similarity does not anticipate which pairs deviate: with the measured profiles in the
matrix no similarity measure reaches $p = 0.26$ over 15 pairs, and the only association
runs the other way in the all-predicted matrix, which is fingerprint similarity among
the small acids. Compound-cold ridge profiles reach a median centered Spearman of 0.387
over 32 screened compounds and carry only the generic stress component: 19 of 20
significant terms for predicted furfural are terms of the 32-compound gene mean, and no
compound shares a significant term between its measured and predicted centered profiles.
On public data the composition rule is measurable and is the mean, not the sum: over
Hillenmeyer's 26 two-compound environments the mean of the matched single profiles has
the highest $R^2$ of the fixed rules in 24 of 26 pairs (median 0.365 against 0.091),
with the per-pair linear fit giving $a + b = 1.06$, while the emergent and masked gene
rates sit inside the hit-calling noise floor. The bars a dose- and mixture-aware model
has to clear are therefore model-free and already on file; the trainer that would clear
them is being written and nothing has run.
\end{abstract}
