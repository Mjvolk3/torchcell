\tcfigfit[1.0]{figures/summary_2026-09-20-01-33-09.pdf}{\textbf{Gene essentiality on
  yeast-GEM: the comparators, the forms, the solve and the store.} Every value is read
  from a committed result file.
  \textbf{a}, Flux balance analysis on four media, AUROC over the 1,121 labeled genes
  with a gene bootstrap. The medium moves the ranking by less than the interval.
  \textbf{b}, Flux Cone Learning's published numbers beside the recomputation of each on
  the gene set the source used, which reproduces every one to the third decimal.
  \textbf{c}, The five-fold ensemble of each form, in the order the rounds reached them,
  from the indicator alone to the Gene Ontology multi-hot in the affine. Intervals are
  gene bootstraps of the pooled per-gene logits.
  \textbf{d}, The four best single families in orange and the three best post hoc
  averages of them in purple, a family being one form's fold models with their
  standardized logits averaged.
  \textbf{e}, The same forms on the genome-wide held-out set, red for message passing
  over a gene graph, gray for the transformer and the multilayer perceptron, dashed for
  chance. What ranks 195 metabolic genes and what ranks the genome are not one model.
  \textbf{f}, One growth program solved seven ways: a probe fitted on one solve's flux,
  scored on another's, the diagonal being the within-solver number. A probe loses up to
  0.35 across solvers and nothing across tolerances of one solver.
  \textbf{g}, How far each store's flux sits from the pFBA store's in the head's
  encoding, as a median relative $L_2$ and a mean share of reactions moved. The four
  agree on growth to $10^{-5}$ and disagree on the flux attaining it.
  \textbf{h}, What the vertex store's clamp-carrying columns cost, by how many of its 429
  are dropped, against the same count of never-clamping columns and the pFBA store
  (dashed). Bars span six seeds. A hundred columns of 4,131 carry the whole deficit.
  \textbf{i}, The anchored layer's growth program at initialization under each turnover
  tier, median growth over 32 genotypes against the capacity scale; the dotted rule is
  where every genotype's program becomes feasible.
  \textbf{j}, What sizes the store over the 025 solid-growth build, logarithmic: the
  records, those reading the wild-type row, the gene subsets, the programs solved, and
  the lethal and uncertified rows. Written by
  \texttt{experiments/028-gene-essentiality/scripts/summary\_figure.py}.}{fig:summary}
