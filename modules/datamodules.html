torchcell.datamodules

PyTorch Lightning data modules for training on cell datasets. CellDataModule builds train, validation and test splits of a cell dataset and caches the split indices, and the perturbation_subset module’s data module draws a size-limited subset of those splits. The split-index records (DataModuleIndex and related models) are pydantic objects, cached as JSON in the data module’s cache directory.
