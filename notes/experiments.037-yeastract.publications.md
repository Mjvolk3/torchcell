## 2026.10.04 - Publication manifest and typeset table

Pairs with the typeset document `notes-tex/yeastract-publications/`. The source is the *S. cerevisiae* member of the YEASTRACT+ 2022 regulation archive, `$DATA_ROOT/data/safe_keeping/YeastractPlus_2022_all_regs.zip` (sha256 `2f2e9f8b3c905ae333814f04161d0ffd2fabf00cc9878c1bd86a8f5e366ebb3e`; member `YeastractPlus_2022_Scerevisiae_regs_flatfile.tsv.gz`, decompressed sha256 `d879c1f4997c4521a68edcad5f8814add6186db4522839507b373b9b1a12f95f`). The purpose of the table is to decide how the file enters torchcell: which papers are worth tracing to their source data, and which are taken as curated edges with the PMID as provenance.

Generated by, in order:

- [[experiments.037-yeastract.scripts.yeastract_publication_manifest]] writes `experiments/037-yeastract/results/yeastract_publications.csv` and `yeastract_pubmed_esummary.json`.
- [[experiments.037-yeastract.scripts.yeastract_publication_tables]] writes `notes-tex/yeastract-publications/tables/*.tex`.

The manifest reads the SPELL manifest of [[experiments.015-spell.publications]], so that one is generated first.

Measured on the 2026.10.04 run (all counts from `yeastract_publications.csv`, n = 1,671 PMIDs over 356,181 flat-file rows):

- The file has eleven columns, no header row and no repeated row. Column meanings are read off the values, not given by the file.
- 226 transcription factors, 6,943 targets, 215,398 distinct factor-target pairs. 264,772 rows are labeled `Indirect`, 91,155 `Direct`, 254 `N/A`.
- 84 PMIDs are also SPELL studies. They carry 75,342 rows (21.2%), 71,846 of them `Indirect`.
- One paper is a reanalysis of a SPELL study: Reimand 2010 (PMID 20385592, 66,920 rows, 18.8%) reprocesses Hu 2007 (PMID 17417638, SPELL GSE4654, 269 conditions). The link is from the Reimand abstract, quoted verbatim in the manifest script. The other 1,586 papers were not read for the origin of their data.
- 1,587 PMIDs are not in SPELL and carry 280,839 rows (78.8%). Ranked by rows, 2 papers reach 50% of those rows, 20 reach 80%, 47 reach 90%, 89 reach 95%, 457 reach 99%.
- 1,077 papers contribute five rows or fewer (2,415 rows); 384 contribute one row.
- PubMed `esummary` returns no record for one PMID, 33402426 (3 rows). 1,632 records carry a DOI and 976 a PMC id.
