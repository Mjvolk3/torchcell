---
title: Query by homologs of yeast genes
sidebar_label: Homologs
description: Planned query mode that starts from a gene of another organism.
---

<StatusBadge status="planned" />

Planned: start from a gene of another organism, map it to its yeast homologs, and
retrieve the experiments that perturb those yeast genes. A homolog is a gene related
to another by shared ancestry. The intended use is to ask what the yeast data says
about a gene studied elsewhere. The source of the homology mapping is not decided.
