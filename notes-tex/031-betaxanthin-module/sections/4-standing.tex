\section{Where the module stands on this target\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:standing}

Six things are measured. The whole-vector store affine lifts the width-12 backbone's
rank to where the width-90 backbone sits and, on the strain as built, lifts its call
too; the two do not add, at either width. The gain lives in the production half of the
vector and in a free affine that trains at full rate; it does not come from the first
epochs, and a release rule on the validation curve keeps it where a fixed late start
loses it, while a nonlinear head over the store loses it and brings the plateau back.
The physically stated stores read in the transformer sit at the parsimonious store's
level: the adjustment store within the seed spread with half its spread and the best
single-seed call, the clean parsimonious vector a little above it on five seeds of six
with the best width-12 ensemble, and the driving-force concentrations at the same rank
with twice the spread; the second law adds a second representation at the flux store's
level, not a higher one. Without any backbone the clean vector read by nearest
neighbors carries more rank than any single transformer arm, and the sampled
driving-force polytope is the one flux-side representation every model reads above
0.12; in the transformer the polytope's mean reads at the flux store's level on every
seed above the control, with the best width-12 ensemble rank, and the sampled cone is
the weakest flux representation. The release rule that lifted the degenerate GLPK
store adds nothing to the adjustment store and takes from the HiGHS store (rounds 149,
152); a store without the vertex noise is read best from the first epoch. The
annotation family sets the level: a perceptron over the propagated GO multi-hot reads
0.200 in three seconds a seed, above every transformer arm, and the same columns in the
affine slot read 0.202 with the seeds identical (round 150), the backbone adding
nothing; the HiGHS vector appended to GO in that affine reads 0.211, above GO alone on
five seeds of six, the highest rank of the study (round 151). Dropout 0.3 on the store
arm reads 0.145, the best single-seed rank and call of any store arm, where width 6 to
90 left the level flat (144, 145). Every arm overfits by epoch 60 and averaging seeds
is worth more than any representation on the call. Cells queued: the three best levers
on one cell (round 154), dropout 0.5 (155), the polytope's spread beside its mean
(156), and the GO affine under the release rule and at dropout 0.3 (158, 159).
