\tcfigfit[1.0]{figures/summary_2026-09-19-19-46-42.pdf}{\textbf{Gene essentiality on
  yeast-GEM: the comparators, the forms, the solve and the store.} Every value is read
  from a committed result file; no number here is typed.
  \textbf{a}, The growth ratio of a single-gene deletion under flux balance analysis on
  four media, AUROC over the 1,121 labeled genes with a gene bootstrap. The medium moves
  the ranking by less than the interval.
  \textbf{b}, Flux Cone Learning's published numbers beside the recomputation of each on
  the gene set the source used. Every one reproduces to the third decimal, which is what
  licenses comparing against them.
  \textbf{c}, The five-fold ensemble of each form, in the order the rounds reached them:
  the indicator alone, then the FBA affine, the chromosome and ProtT5 blocks, the pathway
  block, the flux layer, the cached solve with nothing learned, the layer-free $\log(1 +
  |v|)$ vector, that form at width 48, the interior-point and PDLP stores, the Gene
  Ontology multi-hot added to the affine, and the pinned-kernel rerun. Intervals are gene
  bootstraps of the pooled per-gene logits.
  \textbf{d}, The four best single families in orange and the three best post hoc averages
  of them in purple, where a family is one form's fold models with their standardized
  logits averaged. An average over two families is the highest number in the document, and
  it carries the selection of the best combination.
  \textbf{e}, The same forms scored on the genome-wide held-out set of 4,674 genes, red for
  message passing over a gene graph and gray for the transformer and the multilayer
  perceptron. The dashed line is chance. What ranks 195 metabolic genes and what ranks the
  genome are not the same model.
  \textbf{f}, One growth program solved seven ways: a probe fitted on the flux of one
  solve, scored on another's. The diagonal is the within-solver number. A probe carried
  across solvers loses up to 0.35 and reaches chance on the second exact vertex, while
  across tolerances of one first-order solver it loses nothing, so the store and the head
  must be built from one solver and the tolerance is free.
  \textbf{g}, How far each store's flux sits from the pFBA store's in the head's encoding:
  the median relative $L_2$ of $\log(1 + |v|)$ and the mean share of reactions that moved
  by more than 0.01. The four stores agree on growth to $10^{-5}$ and disagree on the flux
  that attains it.
  \textbf{h}, The anchored layer's growth program at initialization under each turnover
  tier, median growth over 32 held-out genotypes against the capacity scale. The dotted
  rule is the smallest scale at which every genotype's program is feasible. The measured
  tier is the looser box at every scale, since it leaves most catalytic units on the
  organism default.
  \textbf{i}, What sizes the store over the 025 solid-growth build, on a logarithmic axis:
  the records, the records that read the wild-type row, the distinct gene subsets, the
  programs actually solved, and the lethal and uncertified rows among them. The transform's
  cost is set by the program count, 3.5 percent of the records.
  Written by
