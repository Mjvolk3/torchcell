\section{Every rung, with its regime\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:performance}

Every rung of the lower-order ladder on both arms: validation and test Pearson, the test
Pearson within screen (both series centered by screen, which removes the floor), the test
Spearman, and the fraction of test rows the rung's own term covered (a missing term falls
back to the screen mean for that row). The fitness rung scores $f_{ijk}$, not $\tau$, and
is not on the same scale as the others. The transformer is not a row: it has one seed and
no test score, and its validation reads are in \ref{sec:models}.
