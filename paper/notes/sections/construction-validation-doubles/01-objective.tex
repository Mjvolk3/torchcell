\section{Why the pure set-cover was the wrong objective}

The essential wet-lab task is a small set of double mutants that both (a) reconstructs many
top-ranked triples and (b) has \textbf{variance in DMF / interaction} to validate the new
echo-plating assay against (versus plate-stamping). The triple-coverage set-cover
(\texttt{[[experiments.010-kuzmin-tmi.scripts.optimized\_doubles\_setcover\_constructed\_10]]})
does (a) with 8 doubles but fails (b): those 8 are all \textbf{near-neutral} --- Costanzo DMF
span 0.15, $|\varepsilon| \le 0.039$, and \textbf{zero significant interactions} --- so the
assay would have almost nothing to discriminate. An assay validated only on near-1.0 fitness
and null interactions has not been shown to detect signal.
