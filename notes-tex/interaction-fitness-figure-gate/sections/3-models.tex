\section{The models and comparators on file\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:models}

\pfstep{The transformer} The cell graph transformer with the perturbed class token read
through a linear probe, graph regularization (the KL form of 010) toward nine gene graphs,
the full-gene composite sequence embedding (fungal upstream and downstream transformers,
CaLM, ProtT5) through a parameter-matched preprocessor, a fitness head beside the
interaction head, and the per-entry dataset token of experiment 030, which lets one
genotype carry every screen's entry as its own row. Seed \gcgtRSeed{} on arm R has
synced \gcgtREpochsSynced{} epochs to the registry up to epoch \gcgtRLastEpoch{}, the
maximum over epochs is \gcgtRValMax{} at epoch \gcgtRValMaxEpoch{} (a biased order
statistic), and the last-ten-epoch mean is \gcgtRValWindow{}; arm Q has synced
\gcgtQEpochsSynced{} epochs and reads \gcgtQValWindow{} over epochs \gcgtQFirstEpoch{} to
\gcgtQLastEpoch{}. Runs: \texttt{\gcgtRRuns{}} and \texttt{\gcgtQRuns{}}. Every
seed that carries an arm's tag is read on its own and never stitched into another seed's
history; the headline number is seed \gcgtRSeed{}, and a seed with fewer synced epochs
than the ten-epoch window is on file as partial and drawn nowhere. Seeds on file, arm R:
\gcgtRSeedsLogged{}; arm Q: \gcgtQSeedsLogged{}.

\pfstep{The gene-identity ladder of 025, same arms} Test Pearson on the 025 build, from
\file{025-additive-baselines}: train mean \gbRBZerotrainmean{} and \gbQBZerotrainmean{},
screen mean \gbRBThreehierarchicalmean{} and \gbQBThreehierarchicalmean{}, query pair
only \gbRBFourquerypaironly{} and \gbQBFourquerypaironly{}, additive gene ridge
\gbRBOneadditivegene{} and \gbQBOneadditivegene{}, additive plus pair
\gbRBTwoadditivepluspair{} and \gbQBTwoadditivepluspair{}, and the gene-embedding MLP
over three seeds \gbRBFivegeneembeddingmlp{} and \gbQBFivegeneembeddingmlp{}. The 030
ridge of Figure~\ref{fig:sheet} reproduces the 025 ridge on the same arms
(\gloRBOneadditivegenetest{} against \gbRBOneadditivegene{}; \gloQBOneadditivegenetest{}
against \gbQBOneadditivegene{}), which is the end-to-end check that the two builds and
the pinned splits agree.

\pfstep{Comparators, each on another build} The earlier draft of Figure 2 placed the
transformer beside DCell, DANGO and flux balance analysis. Their results are on file,
but on other builds and other splits, so none is a row of the ladder: the three 010
checkpoints read \gcgtOldTestMin{} to \gcgtOldTestMax{} on the 010 build's test split
and the 025 replication of that configuration reached \gcgtRepValBest{} at validation
epoch \gcgtRepBestEpoch{}; DCell's best checkpoint reads \gdcellBestVal{} (validation,
006 build, one \gdcellDays{}-day run); DANGO reads \gdangoBestMean{} at best (STRING
\gdangoBestRelease{}, \gdangoBestSchedule{}, mean of \gdangoBestN{}) on the 005 Kuzmin
2018 build; Yeast9 FBA with nothing fitted reads \gfbaPearson{} against the released
score over \gfbaN{} triples, with \gfbaFracZero{}\,\% of its predictions at zero.
Table~\ref{tab:comparators} lists them with their build and statistic. Refitting the
three on the 030 pinned triples is entry E7 of the registry.
