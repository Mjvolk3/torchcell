\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Vanacloig-Pedros 2022 is the one genome-wide screen in the served graph that doses the
industrial inhibitor panel, furfural, 5-hydroxymethylfurfural, the phenolic aldehydes
and acids, the ionic liquids, ethanol and isobutanol, at a matched IC30, and it is the
only dataset in which isobutanol appears as a stress. Hillenmeyer 2008 is the only
other genome-wide environment-response compendium of comparable size. Every number here
is read from the served records of the two datasets through their loaders. The two
agree on genes (3{,}550 of Vanacloig's 3{,}598 queried genes are in the homozygous
Hillenmeyer arm) and disagree on almost everything else: Vanacloig strains are
quadruple mutants on a drug-sensitized host, anaerobic, in a defined hydrolysate mimic
at pH 5, dosed to a target inhibition; Hillenmeyer strains are single deletions in
diploids, aerobic, in YPD, dosed by mass. Only two compounds are shared per arm.

Replication sets the ceiling. Vanacloig's three batch replicates agree at a median
Spearman of 0.33 over genes and its bottom-5\% hit sets at a Jaccard of 0.24; the
served-standard-error reliability index tracks that agreement at rank 0.87 and gives a
median ceiling of 0.84 on the correlation any model can reach with the noise-free
response, 0.84 for isobutanol and 0.80 for furfural, and no ceiling at all for six
compounds including 5-hydroxymethylfurfural. For Hillenmeyer the served standard error
overstates reliability (index 0.92 against a raw replicate agreement of 0.39), so its
raw replicate agreement is the number to use. The response structure the datasets
share is real and small: a per-gene mean-response correlation of 0.118 and a gene-gene
similarity agreement eleven null standard deviations from zero for the homozygous arm,
a third of that for the heterozygous arm, and exactly one compound, methyl
methanesulfonate, whose per-gene profile transfers. Benomyl, the acetate salt against
the free acid, and ferulic acid at two doses do not. Molecular encoders embed every
compound of the three datasets, and whether chemical similarity predicts response
similarity where exact matches do not exist is measured for each encoder in
Section~\ref{sec:chemistry}.
\end{abstract}
