## 2026.10.04 - Inclusion condition, and what SPELL adds beyond Kemmeren and Sameith

**Inclusion condition for a SPELL study.** A study is admissible when its genotype can be captured at least as a probabilistic representation of the genome, in the sense of the segregant mosaic of [[torchcell.datamodels.eqtl-data-model]] (per-interval parent posterior against hash-pinned parent assemblies). A study that fails this is marked **not possible for now**, never impossible: the block is the missing genome representation, and it lifts when that representation exists. Wild wine, clinical and other natural isolates with no pinned assembly are the expected cases, as are evolved lines with unsequenced mutations. This status has not yet been assigned per study.

**Most recent content.** The newest publication is Park 2023 (PMID 37639444, PLoS Genet, August 2023). Every README carries `File last modified: Thu Apr 25 2024`.

**Deletion genotypes beyond Kemmeren 2014 and Sameith 2015** (from [[experiments.015-spell.scripts.spell_knockout_coverage]], header-text estimates, n = 603 studies):

- 788 distinct deletion genotypes: 677 single-gene, 111 multi-gene.
- 90 are in neither Kemmeren Table S1 (single-gene) nor SPELL's Sameith 2015 folder (any genotype): 51 single-gene and 39 multi-gene, spread over 44 studies.
- Largest contributors of new genotypes: Hu 2007 (14 single), van Wageningen 2010 (14 multi), then 4 or fewer per study.
- Outside the deletion count, Hughes 2000 names 276 genes, 154 not in Kemmeren, and Mnaimneh 2004 names 215 genes, 214 not in Kemmeren. Hypothesis (untested against the papers): Hughes 2000 are deletions and Mnaimneh 2004 are tet-promoter alleles of essential genes.

Conditional-allele reuse with Costanzo 2016 and Kuzmin 2020 is in [[experiments.015-spell.scripts.spell_allele_reuse]].

## 2026.10.04 - What a SPELL value is, and what a loader would still need

**Imputation, measured over all 778 PCL files (92,503,743 cells).** 107 files carry `knn` in their name (10,519,207 cells, 11.4%); all of those have zero missing values. 751 of 778 files have no missing value at all; the 27 that do hold 71,724 missing cells. So for the `knn` files some values are imputed and cannot be told apart from measured ones. For the GEO-derived files (`.final`, `.mapped`, `.sfp`) the absence of gaps is observed, but whether SGD imputed them is not stated anywhere in the archive.

**Requirements a SPELL-derived loader has to meet from the paper, because the PCL header does not carry them** (stated by the author, 2026.10.04):

- Timing of a conditional perturbation. A tet-promoter allele is shut off at a specific time, so the record needs the time since doxycycline addition, not just the allele.
- Growth state. Every experiment grows cells to some state before sampling; the record needs the growth phase or density at harvest and the culture mode.

Retrieval provenance for the archive is in [[experiments.015-spell.scripts.spell_archive_retrieve]].
