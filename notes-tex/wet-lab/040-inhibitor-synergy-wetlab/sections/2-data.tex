\section{Data: one private strain screen and four public chemogenomic screens\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:data}

\notesec{The private dataset} \texttt{InhibitorBioscreenVolk2021Dataset} serves 977
records, one per well, from the dev LMDB at
\file{\$DATA\_ROOT/data/torchcell/inhibitor\_bioscreen\_volk2021}. Every record carries
the run, the well duration, each inhibitor's dose, the derived fitness and the growth
call. The provenance record
(\file{results/wetlab\_provenance.json}, pydantic \texttt{WetlabProvenance}) pins the
report OCR by sha256 together with the raw-mirror manifest and the eight archive files
the loader consumed, so the table below rebuilds from hash-pinned bytes rather than from
a directory listing. The tidy well table
(\file{results/wetlab\_wells.csv}) is read from that LMDB and is re-pointed at the
knowledge graph once the served build carries the private dataset; the records are the
same.

\notesec{The growth call, and why there are two} The served records call growth from the
raw curve: a well grew when its baseline-subtracted optical density rose by at least
0.3, and its generation time is taken from the steepest 3 h window. The 2021 analysis
instead used the Bioscreen software's generation times, computed by PRECOG from
calibrated cell counts.
\external{2021 report, section 1.2.3: \sourcetext{Growth curves are then processed with
PRECOG software using estimated cell counts from calibration curves to compute doubling
times}}
The two calls agree on 185 of 197 ex23 wells, which the loader pins at 0.939086. The 12
that disagree are all three replicates of four 5-HMF combinations: 5-HMF with lactic
acid, 5-HMF with levulinic acid, furfural with acetic acid and 5-HMF, and furfural with
5-HMF and formic acid. The software assigned them generation times of 6.6 to 10.6 h
while their optical density rose only 0.085 to 0.277, below the 0.3 rise the raw-curve
call requires (\file{results/wetlab\_growth\_call\_check.csv}). The scales also differ:
the ex23 control generation time is 1.345 h from the raw curves and 1.815 h from the
software ($n = 8$ control wells), so the two fitness columns are never mixed.

The served call is primary. The disputed wells are all 5-HMF combinations, which is
exactly where a synergy call for 5-HMF would land, so the software call is carried as a
sensitivity and every claim-1 score is reported under both.

\notesec{Doses} Every dose is stored in g\,L$^{-1}$, the unit the stock sheets use, and
converted to mM with the standard formula weight, which is what makes a dose comparable
to a public screen's (Table~\ref{tab:coverage}). The spread is wide: at the ex23 doses
lactic acid is 222 mM and furfural is 15.6 mM, a factor of 14, so a rule that ignores
potency and counts inhibitors cannot order these combinations.

\notesec{The public tables, and which inhibitor each screen holds} The pooled
chemogenomic table of experiment 033 (build 002, TMM-normalized) holds the 32 published
Vanacloig-Pedros 2022 compounds \texttt{10.1093/femsyr/foac036}, of which only furfural
and 5-HMF are among the six. Levulinic acid and sodium acetate were dropped from build
002 as tokens the paper does not report (issue \#501); build 001 still serves them and
is read for supplementary comparison rows only. Sodium acetate is measured in Hoepfner
2014 \texttt{10.1016/j.micres.2013.11.004}, at 125 and 150 mM in YPD with homozygous
diploid deletions, a different screen. Formic and lactic acid are in no screen, so their
profiles can only be predictions. Hillenmeyer 2008
\texttt{10.1126/science.1150021} contributes no inhibitor, and enters as the only public
data with two compounds in one well (Section~\ref{sec:methods}).

\notesec{Confounds, carried into every claim} Three, and none is removable from these
data.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Run length.} 72 h for ex21, 85 h for ex23, 96 h for the isoboles. No
  growth is a statement about the run, so a combination recorded as not grown in ex23
  might grow in 96 h. The classification scores of claim 1 inherit this.
\item \textbf{Strain and medium.} bAID in aerobic YPD against Vanacloig's haploid
  deletion pool in anaerobic SYNH3 at IC30, and against Hillenmeyer's heterozygous
  diploid pool in aerobic YPD read as $\log_2$ depletion over 20 generations. A profile
  measured in one is carried to the other without a correction.
\item \textbf{The ex21 control.} Its uninhibited wells grew more slowly than its
  low-dose wells (mean fitness 1.012, 17 of 18 grew), so ex21 is normalized by the
  fitted top of each Hill curve rather than by its controls, and the fitted tops run
  1.00 to 1.25.
\end{itemize}
