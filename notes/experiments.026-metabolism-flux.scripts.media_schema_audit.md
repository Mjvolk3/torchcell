## 2026.09.01 - Media schema audit

Runs the ontology-to-exchange-bounds mapping for SM, SC, SC-URA and YPD-approx against
yeast-GEM 9.0.2 and reports per-medium coverage, pairwise bound differences, and FBA growth.

Confirms the prediction that our four media collapse to one real axis: SM against SC differs
by 22 exchanges (20 amino acids plus adenine and uracil), SC against SC-URA by exactly one
(uracil). No bound ever differs in magnitude, only in presence.
