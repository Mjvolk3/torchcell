torchcell.graph

Gene graphs for S. cerevisiae. SCerevisiaeGraph assembles the Gene Ontology DAG, SGD physical and genetic interaction networks, regulatory networks and STRING networks over the genes of a genome; GeneGraph and GeneMultiGraph wrap the resulting NetworkX graphs as named, typed containers that the cell datasets consume. The filter_* functions prune the GO DAG (by annotation date, by contained genes, by evidence code, and by redundant terms).
