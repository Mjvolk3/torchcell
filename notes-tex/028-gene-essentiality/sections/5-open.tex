\section{Open\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:open}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The layer's feasibility gap is the module's problem on this endpoint: with
    balance exact the box is 58 percent violated, with the box exact the balance residual
    is 0.85 to 2.0. Whether more alternations, Dykstra's correction, or a warm start from
    the wild-type solve closes the gap enough for the growth column to become growth is
    the layer question; the essentiality endpoint is the cheap place to test it, since
    FBA's 0.65 is the number a feasible growth column has to reproduce at initialization.
  \item A solve-derived gene encoding: the per-deletion parsimonious FBA flux vector, or
    the wild-type flux and variability of the gene's own reactions, as a node feature or
    a ladder encoding, since one linear program per gene costs about 10 ms.
  \item The transformer ties the ladder's best rungs with FBA (0.82 against 0.81 to
    0.83) and does not pass them; width and depth on the FBA cell, under the split
    protocol, is the untested lever for passing them.
  \item The module's own hyperparameters have not been moved on this endpoint: the
    $k_{cat}$ tier (measured against predicted), the thermodynamic source, the
    parameterization (projection against nullspace), the number of alternations.
  \item Every cell here is width 12. The depth-against-width isolation (width 90 at one
    block, width 12 at two) has not run on the split-file protocol.
\end{itemize}
