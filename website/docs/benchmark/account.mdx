Submitting requires an account. There is no password to create: sign in through
[CILogon](https://www.cilogon.org/) with your university or institute, or with ORCID,
GitHub, Google or Microsoft, and the first sign-in opens the account. One person holds
one account, and an email address belongs to one account whichever provider released
it.

The benchmark stores what CILogon reports about the sign-in: your name, your email
address, and the identity provider you chose. The display name, the affiliation and
the provider's name are public and appear with your submissions; the email address is
not shown on any board. The session is kept for the browser tab and ends when the tab
closes or the access token expires.

A script does not hold a browser session. It sends an API token, which is created and
revoked on this page once you are signed in; the [submit page](./submit.mdx) describes
how to use one. Reading the datasets and the boards needs no account.

A scored submission you find a problem with, a leak noticed or a bug in the
predictions, can be withdrawn under **My submissions** below, or with
`tc-bench withdraw <submission_id> --note "..."`. It leaves the board; the row, the
file and your note are kept, and the attempt still counts toward the quota.
