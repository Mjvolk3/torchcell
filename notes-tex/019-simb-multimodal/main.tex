\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Six phenotype strands were run against one shared cell embedding: knockout expression,
knockout expression jointly with a second knockout, morphology, betaxanthin production,
free amino-acid pools, and beta-carotene production. Two of them were presented at SIMB
2026. This is the record of what each measured, against what ceiling, and with what
scoring rule. The headline is that most of the strands are further from their ceilings
than the manuscript's placeholder text assumes, that the expression strand contains no
valid comparison between mechanisms because no run in it was ever trained to saturation,
and that the metabolite strands converge and then overfit within a few hundred epochs,
which is the opposite failure. One new measurement is reported: the free amino-acid
profile of a deletion predicts that deletion's betaxanthin production at an
out-of-fold Pearson $r$ of $0.298$, while tyrosine, the chromophore precursor, predicts
it at $0.064$. The closing section ranks what to run next and states what each option
would settle.
\end{abstract}
