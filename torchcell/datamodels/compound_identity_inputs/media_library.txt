# torchcell/datamodels/media.py (55 labels: the SGA selection medium, YPD/YPAD, the
# SD/YNB/SC family, and the Bloom 2019 YP + carbon-source variants).
# Every label is `canonical`: media.py owns the bench spelling, the metabolism resolver's
# _NAME_SYNONYMS is keyed on it, and a rename would re-hash every media node. PubChem's
# Title is kept as a synonym where it differs (D-glucose keeps the D prefix; myo-inositol
# does not become "an inositol").
# Four preparations are intrinsically undefined and stay without any identifier by design;
# agar has a PubChem CID but its InChIKey belongs to the agarobiose repeat unit, not to the
# algal polysaccharide mixture that goes into a plate.
