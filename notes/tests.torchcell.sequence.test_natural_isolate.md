## 2026.10.12 - What the synthetic panel exercises

Five isolates on hand-written releases (`tests/torchcell/sequence/_peter_tier.py` writes the loss-of-function matrix with CR line ends, the frameshift matrix and the Supplementary Tables workbook with byte-stable save stamps). Every branch of the builder is reached: calls on a gene with a variant, with an allele equal to S288C and with no inferred allele, each stored the same way; an HO deletion beside the presence matrix's natural HO absence (BBB) and one beside a present HO and its own variant (CCC); 'Xn' ploidy, an empty 'Aneuploidies' cell and a two-chromosome aneuploidy; a coded frameshift row under its code's ORF name, and seven code-less rows each stored under its `Gene` exactly as released, including the names the S288C annotation would resolve elsewhere or not at all (SLM6, AAD16, DDI3, LTO1, MOVED). The ledger is asserted field by field, and the README deposit is checked for its record, its idempotence and its refusal of other bytes. With `--data` (and `--slow` for the last), the same builder over the real tier reproduces the counts #988 and #987 state over the 971 phenotyped isolates: ploidy 750 / 131 / 46 / 38 / 5 / Xn 1, zygosity 501 heterozygous / 470 homozygous, 211 aneuploid, 122 HO deletions (124 of 1,011), 231,339 nonsense/SIFT calls on 4,069 genes and 141,453 frameshift calls (8,625 code-less), every one stored; every quote, including the README's, is audited against its pinned source.
