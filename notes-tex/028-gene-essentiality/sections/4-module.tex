\section{The metabolic module: three ways to read essentiality off it\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:module}

The pooled backbone reads its logit from an MLP on the perturbed context
(Figure~\ref{fig:readout-pooled}). A flux arm builds the enzyme-constrained thermodynamic
flux layer on the same context and exposes one physically named quantity, the flux through
the growth reaction \texttt{r\_2111}. Three readouts put that quantity to different uses,
and they differ in what the backbone's gradient has to pass through.

\begin{description}
  \item[biomass (forced)] $\ell = a\,v_{\text{growth}} + b$. The growth column is the
    only path from the context to the logit, so the module carries the whole prediction or
    the cell scores chance (Figure~\ref{fig:readout-biomass}). This is FBA's own reading of
    essentiality with a learned threshold, and it is the wave-9 design.
  \item[context + growth] $\ell = \mathrm{MLP}(h) + a\,v_{\text{growth}} + b$. Both paths
    from the first step; the per-module gradient norms say how much of each update the
    module carries when it is not forced to carry all of it.
  \item[gated] $\ell = \mathrm{MLP}(h) + g\,(a\,v_{\text{growth}} + b)$ with $g_0 = 0$
    (Figure~\ref{fig:readout-gated}). At initialization the cell is the pooled model
    exactly; the gate's trajectory, logged every epoch, says whether the optimizer chose
    the flux path, and the \texttt{flux\_layer} gradient line says whether gradient reached
    the layer, which at $g = 0$ it cannot.
\end{description}

  MLP from the perturbed context to a logit, binary cross entropy against the released
  label. No stoichiometry, no kinetic parameters, no flux. Rendered from

\tcfigfit[1.0]{figures/readout_2.pdf}{\textbf{The forced biomass readout.} The context sets

\subsection{Round 2: the module on the stable backbone, with and without the penalties\secstatus{todo}}

Round 2 (jobs 2182 to 2208) puts the three readouts on the width-12 cell of
Section~\ref{sec:backbone}, on the released-label split, at three seeds each: the forced
and gated readouts at constraint scale 0.1 and 0, the context readout at both scales, the
forced readout at scale 0 with the $k_{cat}$ vector permuted across catalytic units (same
architecture, no unit-to-value correspondence), and the stoichiometry-only layer
(\texttt{flux\_off}: no thermodynamics, no capacity) with the forced and context readouts.
Every flux arm carries the wave-9 flux configuration otherwise: the predicted $k_{cat}$
tier, the union-external thermodynamic source, twenty warmup epochs, the dataset medium,
the mixture concentration space. The paired comparator is the round-1 pooled cell on the
same split at the same batch, weight decay and budget (Table~\ref{tab:w13},
Figure~\ref{fig:w13}).

The reads were fixed before the numbers: \emph{not hurt} is the gated arm against the
pooled cell paired by seed, with the gate's trajectory beside it; \emph{help} is the forced
arm above the pooled cell on three of three seeds and above its shuffled-$k_{cat}$ control;
the \emph{collapse's cause} is the forced arm at scale 0.1 against scale 0, since on the
betaxanthin targets the penalty scale was the dose that starved the layer's input.

\emph{Not hurt} holds. The gated arm at scale 0 scores 0.714, 0.744 and 0.747 against the
pooled cell's 0.745, 0.722 and 0.708 on the same seeds, two of three above by 0.02 to
0.04 and one below by 0.03, a seed mean of 0.735 against 0.725; at scale 0.1 the same
comparison is one of three above. The gate leaves zero and stops: at epoch 80 it reads
$-0.056$ to $-0.063$ on all six gated cells, and the flux layer's gradient norm sits at
0.01 to 0.07 against 0.22 to 0.36 on the forced arm's two seeds that train. The optimizer takes a small negative
weight on the growth affine and the layer receives a tenth of the forced arm's gradient,
so the gated cell is a pooled cell with a small correction, and it scores like one.

\emph{Help} does not hold. The forced arm at scale 0 scores 0.770, 0.653 and 0.772, two
of three seeds above the pooled cell and one seed 0.07 below; at scale 0.1 the split is the
same and the low seed reads 0.585. The shuffled-$k_{cat}$ control, the same layer with the
catalytic constants permuted across units, scores 0.771, 0.718 and 0.765, a seed mean of
0.751 against the real table's 0.732, so whatever the growth column carries here does not
come from the correspondence between units and constants. The forced arm's pooled
bootstrap, 0.773 [0.68, 0.86], is above Flux Cone Learning's 0.742 and FBA's 0.640 to
0.666 on the same genes, level with the pooled cell's 0.738, and below the ladder's
0.80 line.

The collapse on this endpoint is not the penalty's. Seed 2701 falls at both scales, to
0.585 at 0.1 and 0.653 at 0, and the two runs are the same run: the gradient norms start
where the other seeds' do, decay within ten epochs to a hundredth of them (preprocessor
0.002, transformer 0.003 at epoch 80, against 1.0 and 0.5 on seed 2700) and stay there,
with the training loss flat at 0.46 against 0.34, which is a stalled seed rather than a
starved layer. The
stoichiometry-only layer collapses on all three seeds (0.524, 0.631, 0.661), so the
capacity bound and the thermodynamic term are what keep the growth column informative
rather than what starve it, the opposite of the betaxanthin reading. Adding the context
head beside the growth affine without a gate costs 0.06 on the seed mean (0.666 and
0.667 against 0.725) and puts one seed at 0.56; the same sum behind the zero-initialized
gate loses nothing.

\subsection{Round 4: the module on the best backbone\secstatus{todo}}

Round 4 (jobs 2246 to 2248, three cells per GPU) repeats the gated readout, the forced
readout and the shuffled-$k_{cat}$ control at constraint scale 0 on the round-3 cell,
batch 32 with the class weight at 200 epochs, paired by seed against it
(Table~\ref{tab:w15}, Figure~\ref{fig:w15}). The reads are the round-2 reads.

Every arm lands within 0.015 of the pooled cell on every seed. The gated readout scores
0.766, 0.782 and 0.765 against 0.780, 0.769 and 0.773, one of three above; the forced
readout 0.772, 0.781 and 0.778, two of three above by 0.005 and 0.012 and one below by
0.008; the shuffled-$k_{cat}$ control 0.785, 0.781 and 0.772, two of three above, a seed
mean of 0.779 against the real table's 0.777. The gate now moves: it leaves zero after
epoch 10 and climbs steadily to $-0.17$, $+0.20$ and $+0.11$ at epoch 200, of either sign,
with the flux layer's gradient at 0.06 to 0.12 against 0.22 to 0.65 in the forced arm.
The stalled seed of round 2 does not recur under batch 32 with the class weight. On every
arm the validation curve peaks between epochs 60 and 110 and falls afterwards
(Figure~\ref{fig:w15}a), so the 200-epoch budget is more than the cell uses and the
smoothed selection rule is what the number rests on.

The two rounds together answer the two questions. The module does not hurt: on the
stable backbone and on the best one, the gated cell is the pooled cell to within seed
noise. The module does not help: the forced growth column, which carries the whole
prediction, matches the pooled MLP head to within 0.01, and it matches it equally well
with the catalytic constants permuted, so the growth column is as expressive a readout as
the head but its $k_{cat}$ content is not what it reads. On this endpoint, at this
width, the enzyme-constrained flux is a learned nonlinearity over the context rather than
a physical prior that ranks genes.
