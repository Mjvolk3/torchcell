\newcommand{\regimekey}{Regime is what a method reads when it scores a held-out triple:
\emph{identity}, the three genes and nothing measured (the gene-identity ridge, and the
transformer as trained, which sees the lower-order records only through its weights);
\emph{lower-order terms}, the measured singles, doubles and digenic scores of the same
strains, looked up at scoring time (a transductive read over the closure); \emph{screen
only}, the training mean of the triple's screen. Light fill is a test score, the darker line
color a validation score of a model with no test evaluation.}
