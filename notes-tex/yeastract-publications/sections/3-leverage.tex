\section{Papers outside SPELL, by rows\secstatus{todo}}\label{sec:leverage}

\ytNOutsidePapers{} papers have a PMID that is not in the SPELL archive, and
they carry \ytNOutsideRows{} rows, \ytPctOutsideRows{} percent of the file. The
rows are concentrated in a few papers. The largest, Salin 2008, carries
\ytTopOneRows{} rows (\ytTopOnePct{} percent of the file) and the second,
Reimand 2010, carries \ytTopTwoRows{} (\ytTopTwoPct{} percent). Ranked by rows,
\ytNPapersToFifty{} papers reach half of the rows outside SPELL,
\ytNPapersToEighty{} reach 80 percent, \ytNPapersToNinety{} reach 90 percent,
\ytNPapersToNinetyFive{} reach 95 percent and \ytNPapersToNinetyNine{} reach 99
percent.

Table~\ref{tab:sizes} gives the same distribution over all \ytNPapers{} papers.
\ytNFiveRowPapers{} papers contribute five rows or fewer and together hold
\ytNFiveRowRows{} rows; \ytNOneRowPapers{} contribute a single row.
