<CardBlurb>

Each record is one strain of the Keio collection, *Escherichia coli* K-12 BW25113 with
one gene deleted. The cells of the strain were imaged, and their shape and cell-cycle
state were measured and summarized over the cells. The dataset holds 3,664 records.

</CardBlurb>

<CardNotation>

$$
g = \{\Delta i\}, \quad i \in \mathcal{G}
\qquad\qquad
(g, e) \mapsto y \in \mathbb{R}^{T}, \quad T = 15
$$

The genotype holds one deletion $\Delta i$. The environment $e$ is the same for every
record. The phenotype $y$ is one number per feature, the mean of that feature over the
imaged cells of the strain. Six of the 15 are not determined for a strain imaged
without a nucleoid channel.

</CardNotation>
