## 2026.10.12 - Every loader that leaves out released records on genome, identity or field grounds

Owner, 2026-10-12, verbatim: "those ORF ko were actually experiments that they ran ... you should never never do this ... this is a terrible mistake and makes me worried it is being committed elsewhere... remember when we create a dataset we load genome and experimental data separate!!! they have to come together based on their underlying gene sets!"

The rule applied to every row: the genome layer and the experiment layer are loaded independently and joined at use time by gene sets. A loader never drops a released measurement because a gene, locus tag, ORF, b-number, strain, compound or medium does not resolve in our annotation or tables, and it does no cross-referencing at all: a load-time resolution status, synonym route, rename or liftover is the same class as a drop, even when nothing is lost. The only allowed drop is an author withdrawal.

Classes:

- `withdrawal`: the authors withdrew the value or said it is wrong (allowed).
- `genome_gate` (forbidden): a drop or a load-time rewrite because a gene name, ORF, locus tag, b-number or symbol is merged, retired, ambiguous, a non-gene feature, absent from our annotation or gene set, or because a synonym route, ECK join or UniProt crosswalk was computed in the loader.
- `identity_gate`: a compound with no InChIKey or structure, a medium not in `MEDIA_LIBRARY`, a strain or host genome not in our tables.
- `field_gate`: a value refused because a field (unit, pH, medium formulation, aerobicity, denominator, dose, genotype) is not stated, or because the schema has no slot or leaf for it.
- `dedup`: the same measurement released twice; each row says whether the release really duplicates it.
- `other`: anything else; a source's own exclusion (a read floor, an ND cell, a QC flag) is marked `n/a (source)` in the owner column.

**Scope and method.** Every module in `torchcell/datasets/` on `origin/main` at `007cd23b0` (54 yeast, 51 E. coli, 13 P. putida, 4 private, and the shared helpers), read in parallel by five sweep agents and by hand. Counts are the stored ledgers under `$DATA_ROOT/data/torchcell/<slug>/preprocess/` (78 `dropped_records.json`, 35 `build_accounting.json`, 29 `identifier_reconciliation.json`, 10 `locus_tag_reconciliation.json`, 9 `systematic_name_ledger.json`, 5 `identifier_mapping.json`, 5 `not_stored.json`, plus `release_ledger.json` and `refused_cells.csv` where a loader writes them), the loader's own constants, its dataset note, or a read-only recount of the raw release ("recomputed"); nothing was rebuilt and no LMDB was opened. Units are mixed because the loaders count in their own grain (records, cells, values, rows, samples); each unit is one released number or one released record, and the table names it. Totals per class therefore add unlike units and are an order of magnitude, not a record count.

**Dev stores built from in-flight branches.** Several dev stores on disk were rebuilt today from the open audit branches, so their ledgers already show the fix: `smf/dmf/dmi_costanzo2016`, the Kuzmin 2020 stores and `env_chemgen_yoshikawa2009` (built from `fix/audit-yeast-refusals-1045`), `env_chemgen_parsons2006` (built from that worktree's uncommitted Parsons change) and `rbtnseq_price2018_ecoli` (built from `fix/audit-bacterial-refusals-1045`). For those rows the count is what the code on `origin/main` refuses, taken from the PR body, the removals audit or the pre-branch ledger.

**Cross-reference to the removals audit** ([[database.removals-audit-since-kg-3.0.2026.10.12]], tracking issue #1045). The "audit row" column names the audit row a line belongs to. Every audit row that drops records appears here once, at its per-store rule; nothing is counted twice. Audit row 3 (317,234 Costanzo 2016 and Kuzmin 2020 rows) is entered per store. This sweep adds every loader older than KG 3.0 (the yeast loaders the audit did not cover), every load-time rewrite, and the verifier gates.

### Findings that change the record

- **The largest single gate is a compound gate, not a genome gate.** Hoepfner 2014's `encodable_filter` builds a sensitivity column only for a CMB id with a released SMILES, which leaves out 1,702 proprietary compounds and 26,992,230 released records (15,263,802 HIP, 11,728,428 HOP; `env_chemgen_hoepfner2014/dropped_records.json`). The loader's dataset note calls them "black-box perturbations"; under the owner's rule they are released measurements with an unidentified compound, the same case as Parsons 2006's nine name-only compounds that #1053 gaps.
- **Nadal-Ribelles 2025 drops about 1.13M expression values (3.1%) whose gene names do not resolve** ("ncRNA/retired symbols such as 15S-RRNA/SNR*", [[torchcell.datasets.scerevisiae.nadal_ribelles2025]]); this is the largest genome gate and predates KG 3.0.
- **Rousset 2018's `growth_screen_measurement_is_served_by_cui2018` is not a byte duplicate.** The removals audit lists it as justified ("r 1.0000"); the loader's own docstring (lines 69 to 74) says only 426 of 54,326 shared values are bit-identical and the rest differ by up to 0.0066, "a re-analysis". That makes it the Wetmore 2015 against Price 2018 question (#931), not a dedup.
- **`ohnuki2018.py::create_calmorph_experiment` (line 365) writes a NaN trait as 0.0.** No cell is NaN in today's release, so nothing is fabricated now; it is a live path that would store an invented zero.
- **Fang 2025's `DROP_RULES` total is a typo**, as the audit found: 95,962 stated, 40,291 + 55,340 = 95,631; the table uses 95,631.
- **The genome verifier rules feed the same retirement as a drop.** `verify_after_build` retires the manifest on any FAIL, so `current_genome_genes` on Puddu 2019 (2026-10-11) led to a loader change that refused 9 colonies; the same rules are failing now on `smf_costanzo2016`, `ffa_xue2025` and `gene_essentiality_sgd`, whose manifests will be retired on their next `--verify` build unless `fix/genome-rules-report-not-gate` lands first.
- **Load-time genome rewrites touch most stores, not a few.** `reconcile_locus_tags` runs in 53 bacterial modules and rewrites every released name in, for example, Cui 2018 (141,542 records), Rousset 2018 (68,436), Hawkins 2020 (24,149, through the ECK liftover), Goodall 2018 (8,203) and the Fitness Browser family (3,625 genes lifted from MG1655 b-numbers to BW25113 loci); on the yeast side the resolver keys Hale 2024 (8,692,635 records), Wildenhain 2015 (430,820), Lian 2019 (266,304), Nadal-Ribelles (35.2M values) and Cachera 2023 (4,719). Under the owner's rule each of these stores the resolver's name, not the released one.

### What this sweep does not settle

- Counts marked "recomputed" are read-only recounts of the raw release by the sweep agents, not loader ledgers; counts marked "count not recorded" are listed in the uncounted table or noted in their row.
- Where a row gives cells, values or gene rows rather than records (Nadal-Ribelles, Hillenmeyer ORFs, Hu 2007, Balakrishnan, Teteneva), the record count was not derived.
- The Teteneva 2024, Li 2024 (D2Cell), Cai 2023, Zhang 2015 and Typas 2008 rows are datasets the loader refuses whole; their counts are the loader's constants, and the units overlap within each dataset.
- Rewrite counts for Parsons 2006, Vanacloig 2022, Piotrowski 2017, Hillenmeyer 2008, Hoepfner 2014, Lian 2019 and Hale 2024 give the number of records keyed through the resolver, not how many of those names changed.
