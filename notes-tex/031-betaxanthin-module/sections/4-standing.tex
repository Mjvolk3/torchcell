\section{Where the module stands on this target\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:standing}

Four things are measured. The whole-vector store affine lifts the width-12 backbone's
rank to where the width-90 backbone sits and, on the strain as built, lifts its call
too; the two do not add. The gain lives in the production half of the vector and in a
free affine that trains at full rate; it does not come from the first epochs, and a
release rule on the validation curve keeps it where a fixed late start loses it. The
clean parsimonious vector read by nearest neighbors, with no backbone, carries more rank
than any single transformer arm, and the metabolite side, the driving-force
concentrations, carries rank linearly. Every arm overfits by epoch 60 and averaging
seeds is worth more than any representation on the call. Open, with cells queued: the
physically constrained stores in the transformer (rounds 135, 139, 140, 142), the size
question (rounds 144 and 145), and a probabilistic concentration representation, the
polytope sampled rather than its max-min point, which is not built.
