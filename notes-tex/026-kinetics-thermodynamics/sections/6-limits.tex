\section{What these tables cannot support\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:limits}

\subsection{A predicted turnover number is not a measured one\secstatus{todo}}

Every row is a model output, recorded as one in a provenance column. Two predictors trained
on overlapping data, applied to the same 7{,}351 pairs, disagree by a median factor of about
three, which is wider than either model's own reported error and is the honest uncertainty
on any single value.

The accuracy of Section~\ref{sec:accuracy} does not narrow it, being an upper bound under
training leakage. The join also has to be on the \emph{pair}:
joining on enzyme alone compares a prediction for one substrate against a measurement for
another, and doing so moves one of these correlations from 0.98 to 0.82 and another from
0.62 to 0.12. The looser join is the one that is easy to write by accident.

\subsection{$K_M$ is built and deliberately not consumed\secstatus{todo}}

$K_M$ would enter the flux layer only through a saturation factor
$\eta_{\mathrm{sat}} = \prod_i c_i/(K_{M,i} + c_i)$, whose intracellular concentrations are
measured for 191 of 2{,}806 metabolites, 6.8 percent, so adding it today adds a parameter
and a free variable per reaction with no anchor for either.

The measured argument for adding it anyway is promiscuity: underground reactions show
roughly twice the $K_M$ at indistinguishable $k_{cat}$, so a $k_{cat}$-only model cannot
tell a promiscuous side activity from a native one. The measured argument against is that
only one of the four $K_M$ predictors clears the EC-median lookup under the admission rule
(Section~\ref{sec:kinetic-tiers}), so these values are order-of-magnitude priors, not
constants.

\subsection{What no method reaches\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:unreachable}

%% SOURCE: results/reaction_thermo_coverage_final.json (ceiling_ladder: denominator 3,847,
%% n_closable 21, n_permanently_closed_reactions 291 with permanently_closed_reasons
%% counted per blocking class and therefore overlapping; metabolite_reasons;
%% flux_carrying), results/thermo_exclusions.json (by_reason),
%% results/thermo_tier_d_probe.json (no refused structure reachable by reactant
%% contribution or a surrogate inside the admitted spread), and
%% results/kinetic_coverage_closure.json (ceiling: n_default_units 18,
%% n_reachable_with_open_source_tools 0).

\textbf{312 of the 3{,}847 chemical reactions carry no energy, and 291 of them never will.}
A reaction is permanently closed when every species blocking it is not a molecule: a
placeholder formula naming a family of compounds, a SLIME backbone whose formula is a
difference formula, or a residue inside a macromolecule. Counted by at least one blocking
species of each class, those three hold 202, 96 and 3 reactions, and the counts overlap
where a reaction is blocked by two of them.
\textbf{The 21 that remain are the whole of what any further method could
buy}, and the probe that measured them found none of the refused structures reachable by
the reactant-contribution path or by a surrogate inside the admitted spread, so they need
new data rather than new code (Section~\ref{sec:permanent}). Counted over the whole model
the gap is 330 reactions, the extra 18 being exchange, demand, sink and biomass
pseudoreactions for which a standard reaction energy has no meaning.

\textbf{Five chemistries are refused by the group system rather than by the species.} The
porphyrin macrocycle has no group, and it holds heme a, heme o, siroheme and ferroheme b;
the sulfoxide of L-methionine S-oxide has none; the aryl phosphate of 4-nitrophenyl
phosphate has none. The nitro group is the opposite failure and is worth keeping separate:
the group exists and sits in the component-contribution null space with 0 of 631 training
compounds carrying it, so the estimate has infinite variance rather than no value.
Triphosphate is a third kind, decomposing nowhere and appearing in none of the 3{,}999
training reactions, so the reactant contribution that prices phosphate and diphosphate
despite their empty group vectors is closed to it too. \textbf{Curation cannot move any of
these}: triphosphate's p$K_a$ ladder is measured, admitted and recorded, and it prices
nothing on its own, because a ladder fixes a transform and there is no untransformed
energy for it to act on.

\textbf{Novel compounds needed a licensed tool and no longer do.} The selected p$K_a$
cascade creates a compound eQuilibrator has never seen through
\texttt{pkas\_provided\_externally}, and it priced 70 of the model's metabolites
(Section~\ref{sec:pka}). \textbf{What binds is reach rather than accuracy}: the cascade on
its own reaches only a species that already has a structure and lacks a ladder, so the
structure writers and not the predictor are what moved coverage. The
777.5\,kJ\,mol$^{-1}$ protonation-convention offset those values once carried is closed;
they were resubmitted as their pH 7 major microspecies, which moved them by a median of
1{,}585\,kJ\,mol$^{-1}$ and left a residual of 0.13.

\textbf{Eighteen catalytic units run on the organism default and no open-source tool reaches
any of them} (Section~\ref{sec:kinetic-tiers}). Of the 28 substrate rows behind them, 14
carry an R-group placeholder, which is a curation decision in the model rather than a
missing structure; 7 are macromolecular species with no formula and need a predictor that
accepts a protein substrate, which none of the seven mirrored models is; and the substrate
writer was run on the rest and refused each one.

\textbf{Proton translocation still carries zero in every arm reported here.}
\texttt{transport\_delta\_g\_source: computed} is off by default, so the 183
proton-translocating reactions get none of the term Section~\ref{sec:transport-term}
computes and checks, and the protonation contribution to $N_H$, worth 613 reactions, is
still unwritten.

\textbf{Compartment pH is assumed, and nothing in the library can settle it.} The values are
textbook organellar figures recorded as \texttt{assumed} in the build output; the two
in-library sources tabulate rather than measure them and disagree by 1.2 units on the
peroxisome (Section~\ref{sec:compartments}).

\subsection{What the mirror records when a predictor cannot run\secstatus{todo}}

All six now run. Where a predictor could not be run at all, the mirror records
\texttt{runnable: false} with the specific blocker, so that a gap in coverage is never
mistaken for a modeling choice. Boost\_KM carried that flag wrongly
(Section~\ref{sec:mirrors}); the real obstacle was that UniRep is a TensorFlow~1 mLSTM stepping one residue at a time on CPU, which on a first attempt
occupied roughly 85 cores, and the ESM-1b variant Wu et al.\ used embeds all 887 enzymes on
one GPU in four minutes.
