\subsection{Sampler and weighting arms\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:sampler}

The two overnight samplers are the ends of one family. A batch slot draws dataset $s$ with
probability
\begin{equation}
\pi_s(\alpha) = \frac{n_s^{\alpha}}{\sum_{s' \in \mathcal{S}} n_{s'}^{\alpha}},
\qquad \alpha \in [0, 1],
\label{eq:sampler}
\end{equation}
then a record of $s$ uniformly, with replacement, for the same number of batches per epoch.
$\alpha = 0$ is the balanced sampler of \dsec{4.4}; $\alpha = 1$ matches the records
sampler in expectation (the records sampler draws without replacement). $\alpha = 0.5$ is
the one new point: Vaishnav, with 42{,}694 training records against Kemmeren's 1{,}202, is
drawn about 6 times as often as Kemmeren rather than 36 times (records) or equally
(balanced).

Under the balanced sampler the two weightings of \deq{21} give every dataset the same
expected weight $1/|\mathcal{S}|$ (design Table~2), so the weighting contrast is degenerate
there and lives under the records sampler only. The distinct cells are therefore four:
balanced with equal weighting (the stage-1 reference), records with equal weighting, records
with record weighting (the canceled run 6151), and $\alpha = 0.5$ with equal weighting. The
reference is the balanced sampler because it is the only joint arm whose seed-0 Kemmeren
transfer was positive and whose isolate means were highest (\dsec{6}); that is a choice of
reference, not a finding, and the contrasts of Sec.~\ref{sec:polaris} decide it.

\subsection{Operator residual: plain against ReZero\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:residual}

The overnight operator gates its two sublayers with ReZero scalars initialized at zero
(\deq{9}), so at step 0 it is the identity and the marks reach nothing until
$\zeta_{\mathrm{attn}}$ moves. The owner's objection, 2026-10-23: ``it seems to allow
models to cheat ... shut off info before dynamics mature''. The plan makes the plain residual
the main arm, post-normalized like the trunk (\deq{5}) and like the manuscript's operator,
\begin{equation}
h'_i = \operatorname{LN}\!\big(h_i + \operatorname{Drop}(\Delta h_i)\big),
\qquad
h^{\mathrm{pert}}_i = \operatorname{LN}\!\big(h'_i + \operatorname{Drop}(\operatorname{FFN}(h'_i))\big),
\label{eq:plain}
\end{equation}
with $\Delta h_i$ of \deq{8}. A record without marks attends only to the null key, whose value
is zero, so its $\Delta h_i = 0$ and its state passes through the operator's normalizations
alone. ReZero (\deq{9}) is the control. The control arm logs $\zeta_{\mathrm{attn}}$ and
$\zeta_{\mathrm{ffn}}$ every 50 steps, which turns the objection into a measurement: how many
steps the gates stay near zero, and whether the ReZero arm's Kemmeren and segregant
statistics, the ones that depend on marks, trail the plain arm's at the window.

\subsection{Where the graphs act\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:graph}

Off-graph attention is not forbidden in any arm: self-attention stays full in every trunk
layer except, in one arm, the single masked layer. Three placements are compared.

\notesec{Mask (reference)} The overnight form: in layer $\ell = 2$ head $a$ attends only along
graph $k(a)$ (\deq{6}); the other three layers are unmasked.

\notesec{Readout gather} No layer is masked. The gene head reads, beside the gene's own
perturbed state, the mean perturbed state of its neighbors in each relation,
\begin{equation}
\bar h^{(k)}_{b,i} =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{1}{|\mathcal{N}_k(i)|} \displaystyle\sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}_k(i)} h^{\mathrm{pert}}_{b,j} & \text{if } \mathcal{N}_k(i) \neq \varnothing,\\[8pt]
\mathbf{E}^{\mathrm{nbr}}_{k} & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\qquad
\hat{\mathbf{o}}_{b,i} = \operatorname{MLP}_{\mathrm{gene}}\big([h^{\mathrm{pert}}_{b,i} ;\ W_{\mathrm{ga}} [\bar h^{(1)}_{b,i}; \ldots; \bar h^{(9)}_{b,i}] ;\ \mathbf{c}_b]\big) + \mathbf{b}^{\mathrm{gene}}_{s(b)},
\label{eq:gather}
\end{equation}
with $\mathcal{N}_k(i) = \{j : A^{(k)}_{ij} = 1 \text{ or } A^{(k)}_{ji} = 1,\ j \neq i\}$, the
same nine relations as the mask, and $\operatorname{MLP}_{\mathrm{gene}}: \mathbb{R}^{328} \to
\mathbb{R}^{128} \to \mathbb{R}^{n_{\mathrm{out}}}$. A gene with no neighbor in a relation
reads a learned vector of that relation, never a zero standing in for a mean. The record head
(\deq{14}) is unchanged.

\notesec{None} No mask and no gather: the graphs enter nowhere, the control for both.

\notesec{Not in the grid} A soft form, an additive learned bias on graph edges in place of
$-\infty$ off them, sits between mask and none and is left to the owner (decision 13).

\subsection{Background-strain marks\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:background}

Every non-isolate record is read today on the S288C reference, although the strains carry
fixed edits relative to it. The plan adds those edits as marks shared by every record of a
background,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{P}_b = \mathcal{P}^{\mathrm{bg}}_{\mathrm{bg}(b)} \cup \mathcal{P}^{\mathrm{eng}}_b,
\qquad
\mathbf{p}_t \leftarrow \mathbf{p}_t + \mathbf{E}^{\mathrm{orig}}_{\iota_t},
\label{eq:bgmarks}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{P}^{\mathrm{eng}}_b$ are the record's engineered marks of \deq{7},
$\iota_t = 1$ for a background mark and $\mathbf{E}^{\mathrm{orig}} \in \mathbb{R}^{2 \times
d}$, so the operator can tell a shared background edit from the perturbation. Isolates under
route A carry their own alleles in their context and take no background marks.
Table~\ref{tab:backgrounds} lists what the loaders name.

Two representation choices are open and are decision 12. The mating type differs between
backgrounds at one locus, and the change types of \dsec{3.3} (step 8) have no mating-type
change. A complemented auxotrophy carries a chromosomal deletion and a plasmid copy of the
same gene, and the precedence rule of step 8 (deletion outranks present) would read the gene as

\subsection{Isolates: route A, with the route B consistency test\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:routes}

Route A, each isolate as its own reference context (\dsec{3.1}), stays the main arm. Route B
reads an isolate on S288C with its variant, loss-of-function and absence genes as marks, a
median of 5{,}038 marks per Caudal record (job 6120); it is not implemented in the trainer
(design Sec.~7, item 3). In the route-B arm only the three isolate datasets change route. Beside
the per-route statistics of \deq{24} and \deq{25}, a label-free readout compares the two
routes' predictions on the same held-out isolate, centered on the training-mean profile
$\bar z_i$ of \deq{25},
\begin{equation}
\rho_b = \operatorname{corr}_{i}\big(\hat z^{A}_{b,i} - \bar z_i,\ \hat z^{B}_{b,i} - \bar z_i\big),
\qquad
\text{reported as } \operatorname*{median}_{b} \rho_b \text{ beside } \operatorname*{median}_b r^{A}_b \text{ and } \operatorname*{median}_b r^{B}_b,
\label{eq:routecons}
\end{equation}
for routes A and B trained with the same seed. A median $\rho_b$ well above both $r_b$ means
the routes learned the same function of the genotype; a $\rho_b$ near or below them means
they reach their scores by different functions, and neither route's isolate score then speaks
for the representation.

\subsection{The mark cap\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:markcap}

The overnight runs kept at most 1{,}024 marks per record (deletion, absence and
loss-of-function marks first), a smoke-time cap the design does not have. N'Guessan carries
a median of 2{,}910 marks and route-B isolates about 5{,}000. The reference keeps 1{,}024; the
arm raises it to 4{,}096; an uncapped run is first measured for memory and step time on one
GilaHyper card (smoke G6) and enters the grid only if it fits a 40\,GB A100.
