\tcfigfit[1.0]{figures/module_lp.pdf}{\textbf{The layer that solves, rounds 15 to 15d.}
  The abundance head (red) sets each gene's enzyme abundance with availability a hard
  fact, the unit abundance and the $k_{cat}$ ceilings form the box (purple), and the flux
  is the growth optimum of the linear program over that box (yellow), one HiGHS solve per
  genotype; the program's value $z^*$ enters the readout (blue). Mass balance and the
  box hold exactly at the optimum. The dashed path is the backward pass: the derivative of
  $z^*$ in a bound is that bound's dual, nonzero only where the bound binds, so the
  backbone is trained through the ceilings and only through them; an infeasible program
  returns no flux and no gradient. Drawn in draw.io from
  \texttt{notes/assets/drawio/028-module.gen.py}.}{fig:module-lp}
