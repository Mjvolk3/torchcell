\section{The full pool\secstatus{todo}}\label{sec:full}

Table~\ref{tab:candidates} is the whole pool the sixty were cut from, every row the
generating script holds, in rank order with its class, both axes, instance and measurement
counts, phenotype, sequence basis and a clickable link. All 154 rows are inside the 200-row
cut, so all are recommended; the 46-row shortfall is open. Table~\ref{tab:sources-all}
gives every row's citation, link, the reason for its position and where the per-record
values live, in the same order; for the first sixty rows that reason is also the
\emph{Why} column of Table~\ref{tab:final}. Table~\ref{tab:verdicts} gives the candidate-gate
verdict of every ranked row from the verdict store. The ranking is produced by
\texttt{experiments/database/scripts/build\_candidate\_datasets\_table.py} and is also
written to \texttt{experiments/database/results/candidates/candidate\_datasets.json}.
