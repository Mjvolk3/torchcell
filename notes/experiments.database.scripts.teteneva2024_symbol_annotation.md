## 2026.10.12 - Table S4's symbols on the W3110 annotations: a downstream measurement (#910)

Not loader logic: the loader stores every Table S4 value under the symbol as released
([[torchcell.datasets.ecoli.teteneva2024]]). This script measures how the 3,691 symbols
land on the W3110 annotations and writes
`experiments/database/results/teteneva2024_symbol_annotation.json` (git-ignored,
regenerated by the script).

By `/gene` alone (`symbol_routes`):

| annotation member | one gene feature | two or more | none |
|---|---|---|---|
| GenBank GCA_000010245.1 (no locus tags) | 3,036 | 0 | 655 |
| RefSeq GCF_000010245.2 (`Y75_RS` tags) | 3,607 | 44 | 40 |

Onto one RefSeq locus (`resolve_symbols`), three ordered steps:

| step | rule | symbols |
|---|---|---|
| `refseq_symbol` | the RefSeq `/gene` of exactly one gene feature | 3,607 |
| `genbank_symbol_eck_jw_b` | the GenBank `/gene` of one feature, whose stop codon (strand and 3' end on the byte-identical sequence) is one RefSeq locus's, not a split pseudogene | 65 |
| `mg1655_symbol_eck_jw_b` | one MG1655 gene by exact name, its b-number on one GenBank CDS `ECK:JW:b` note, that CDS's stop codon on one RefSeq locus | 9 |
| unresolved | `dcuC`, `gatA`, `wbbL` (split pseudogenes), `tal`, `tuf` (two loci, no route), `dmpG`, `mddA` (no name anywhere), `nanS`, `rzoD` (no RefSeq stop match), `pat` (step 3 lands on `ygjG`'s locus, which step 1 holds) | 10 |

Two cross-checks, both findings rather than refusals: 23 step-1 symbols the GenBank
route would not place on the same locus (6 on another locus: `cysT`, `ecpD`, `rarA`,
`rbn`, `ykgM`, `zupT`, each with a Table S4 description matching the step-1 RefSeq
product; 3 split, 14 with no RefSeq stop match), and step 3 run on the 65 step-2
symbols agrees on 55 and resolves no locus for 10. Measured 2026-10-12; the counts are
pinned in `RESOLVED_BY_STEP` / `UNRESOLVED` and re-derived by
`tests/torchcell/datasets/ecoli/test_teteneva2024_symbol_annotation.py` (`--data`).
