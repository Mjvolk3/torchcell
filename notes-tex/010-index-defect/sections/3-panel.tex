\section{What the panel is actually predicted to do\secstatus{todo}}
\label{sec:panel}

Construction had already started when the defect was found, so the operative
question is not which genes to pick but which combinations among the picked genes
the model favors. Every combination was therefore rescored under the correct
index space with all three checkpoints, rather than one, because two training
runs share only 0.39 to 0.47 of their top 100.

\subsection{The plate carries one locus twice\secstatus{todo}}

The panel at the bench is not the model-selected panel-12. Read from the Echo
transfer report of the plating run itself, it is twelve strain labels:
\texttt{COS111}, \texttt{ELC1}, \texttt{LCL1}, \texttt{MMS2}, \texttt{SPH1},
\texttt{YEH1}, \texttt{YER079W}, \texttt{YJR060W}, \texttt{YKL033W-A},
\texttt{YLR312C-B}, \texttt{YOS9} and \texttt{YPL081W}.

\texttt{SPH1} and \texttt{YLR312C-B} are separate wells and, by
Section~\ref{sec:alias}, one gene. Twelve labels are eleven distinct loci. Either
the same deletion is present twice under two names or one well is not the strain
its label says, and only the bench can settle which.

\subsection{The corrected ranking, and how far it can be read\secstatus{todo}}

All 165 triples over the eleven loci fall between $-0.19$ and $+0.03$, against a
training-label standard deviation of $0.063$. Nothing on this plate is predicted
far from the mean.

The three checkpoints agree on the sign of the interaction for 133 of the 165.
That agreement is not evenly distributed. Among the top of the ranking most
triples straddle zero and several have one checkpoint positive and another
negative on the same triple; only \texttt{YER079W+MMS2+YEH1} has all three
clearly on one side, at $+0.032$ with a spread of $0.0035$. The bottom is
resolved: every one of the twelve most negative is negative in all three
checkpoints, the strongest being \texttt{MMS2+YJR060W+YEH1} near $-0.19$.

So the model has a reproducible opinion about which triples are strongly negative
and almost none about which are positive.

The single positive is not usable. \texttt{YGL087C+YLL012W+YPL046C} has a
corrected mean of $+0.0108$ with a spread of $0.0607$ across the three
checkpoints, and they do not agree on its sign. Every one of the 17 triples the
checkpoints do agree on is negative, the strongest being
\texttt{YJR060W+YLL012W+YPL046C} at $-0.1336$, which the original list ranked
twenty-first and therefore excluded.

\notesec{Why the top six were the top six}
Ranks 1 through 6 all contain \texttt{YLR312C-B} and all scored between $0.683$
and $0.711$. Each was scored as the double formed by its other two genes, by
Section~\ref{sec:doubles}. Under the correct index the six land between $-0.007$
and $-0.052$. Their rank was an artifact of the collapse, not a claim about them.
