\section{The measurement plate\secstatus{todo}}\label{sec:plate}

Seventy strains on one 384-well layout, five wells each, one pick per strain.

\textbf{These counts are approximate.} They fix how many strains the plate has to
carry, not where any of them goes. Three things can still move the arithmetic: the
twenty wells held for the wild-type reference are a working figure rather than a
fixed allocation, thirteen wells are spare once every strain has its five, and SPH1
may ride along as a seventy-first strain, which the spare absorbs without changing
the five. The final well assignment comes from the picklist, not from this table.
